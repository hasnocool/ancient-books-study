## Story

The Lord warns that humanity will refuse His yoke and abandon the seeds given to them. People will take another yoke, sow vain seeds, bow to vain gods, and deny the one God. Wickedness and impurity will spread throughout the world, including practices the text condemns but declines to describe. Because of this corruption, the Lord says He will bring a deluge, destroy all, and leave the earth devastated.
