## Story

The Lord tells Adam that he is earth and will return to the earth from which he was taken. Yet the Lord says He will not destroy him, but send him back to his origin and may take him again at the second coming. The Lord declares that He has blessed all visible and invisible creation and blesses the seventh day, the Sabbath, because He rested from His labours.
