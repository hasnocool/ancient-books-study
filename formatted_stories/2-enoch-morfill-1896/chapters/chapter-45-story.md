## Story

Enoch says that the person who hastens to bring an offering before the Lord will have the Lord hasten the work’s fulfilment and execute just judgment for him. The person who increases his lamp before the Lord will have his heavenly treasure greatly increased. Yet God does not need bread, light, animals, or other sacrifices, which are as nothing to Him. What God requires is a pure heart, and through these offerings He tests the human heart.
