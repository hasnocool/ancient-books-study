## Story

The Lord gives Enoch thirty days to work in his house and instruct his sons and household. They are to listen, learn that there is no God beside the Lord, keep His commandments, and begin reading and understanding Enoch’s books. At the end of the thirty days, angels will come to take Enoch from the earth and from his sons, according to the Lord’s will.
