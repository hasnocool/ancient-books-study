## Story

The Lord recalls Adam's life and the garden in Eden, in the East, where Adam was to observe the law and instruction. Paradise is continually light-filled, and Adam can perceive the angels singing. The devil sees that earthly things are subject to Adam and wishes to make another world and rule it. The text says the evil spirit was formerly Satanail before leaving heaven and becoming Satan. He understands the judgment against him and plots against Adam, entering and deceiving Eve but not touching Adam. The Lord says He cursed the devil for his ignorance, but did not curse humanity, the earth, or other created things; He cursed the evil fruit of human action and its works.
