## Story

The Lord establishes an eighth day as the first after His work and describes the days according to a pattern of seven thousand. At the beginning of the eighth thousand there will be a time beyond calculation, without ordinary years, months, weeks, days, or hours. The Lord tells Enoch that all he has seen and written was devised through divine wisdom. No counsellor or inheritor shares the Lord's works; His thought is unchanging, His wisdom counsels Him, and His word is reality. Enoch is told to take his books and go with Samuil and Raguil to tell his sons what he has seen. The books are to pass through generations and nations. Michael is appointed in connection with Enoch's writings and those of his forefathers; Ariukh and Pariukh are set as guardians so the family record will survive the coming deluge.
