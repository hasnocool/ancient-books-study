## Story

Enoch teaches that wrongdoing against animals also wrongs one’s own soul. He connects the offering of clean animals and birds with the preservation of the offerer’s soul, gives an instruction about binding animals by their four feet when preparing them for food, and condemns killing an animal without a wound or secretly injuring it.
