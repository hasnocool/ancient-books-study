## Story

[The Lord calls one of His greatest angels and places him beside Enoch. The angel’s appearance is like snow, his hands like ice, and his presence so cold that Enoch’s face is chilled by fear of the Lord. The text compares this fear with the unbearable heat of mighty fire and the sun, and the frost of the air. The Lord tells Enoch that, if his face is not chilled there, no human being could look upon it.]
