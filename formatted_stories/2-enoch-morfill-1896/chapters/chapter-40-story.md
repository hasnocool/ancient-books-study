## Story

Enoch tells his children that he has learned all things from the Lord and recorded the heavens, their hosts, the stars and their names, the courses of the sun and moon, and the divisions of time. He has written about the seasons, years, months, days, and hours; the plants and creatures of the earth; the clouds and their rain; the paths and restraints of thunder and lightning; and the treasuries of snow, hail, and cool breezes. He has also observed the measured release of the winds, mapped the earth’s mountains, rivers, fields, and stones, and measured the heights from earth to the seventh heaven and down to the place of judgment. Finally, he has recorded the prisoners who suffer while awaiting judgment, together with the judgments and deeds entered against them.
