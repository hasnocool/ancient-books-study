## Story

Enoch says that he has recorded every person’s actions and that no one born on earth can hide or conceal their deeds. He urges his children to complete their days with patience and meekness so that they may inherit the endless life to come. They should endure wounds, affliction, evil words, and attacks for the Lord’s sake. Even when they have the power to take revenge, they must not repay neighbour or enemy, because God will repay in the day of judgment. They should spend gold or silver for a brother’s sake and extend their hands to the orphan, widow, and stranger.
