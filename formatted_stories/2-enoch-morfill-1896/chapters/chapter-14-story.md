## Story

The guides take Enoch to the western side of the heavens. There he sees six great gates, corresponding to the eastern gates through which the sun emerges.

The sun sets through the western gates according to the year's count of 365 days and a quarter. When it passes out through the western gates, four hundred angels take its crown and carry it to the Lord.

The sun continues in its chariot without light for seven complete hours during the night. When it approaches the east at the eighth hour, the four hundred angels bring back its crown and crown it again.
