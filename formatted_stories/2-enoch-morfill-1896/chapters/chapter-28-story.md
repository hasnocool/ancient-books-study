## Story

The Lord gathers the waters beneath heaven, dries the waves, forms stones from the waves, and heaps dry substance from the stones, naming it earth. In the earth He appoints an abyss. He gathers and restrains the sea with a yoke, giving it an eternal boundary, and fixes the firmament above the water. The text calls this the first day, then says evening and morning mark the second day.
