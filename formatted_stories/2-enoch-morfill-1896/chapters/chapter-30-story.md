## Story

The Lord says the heavens are completed on the third day. The earth produces trees, fruit, mountains, herbs, and seed; Paradise is planted and guarded by fiery angels. On the fourth day the Lord places seven named heavenly bodies in seven circles—Kruno, Aphrodite, Ares, the Sun, Zeus, Hermes, and the Moon—and appoints their lights and zodiacal courses. Thunder and the revolutions of hours are fixed. On the fifth day the waters produce fish, birds, creeping things, four-footed creatures, and every breathing life. On the sixth day Wisdom makes Adam from seven substances: flesh from earth, blood from dew, eyes from the sun, bones from stones, thoughts from angelic swiftness and clouds, veins and hair from grass, and spirit from divine spirit and wind. Seven faculties or qualities are given. Adam unites visible and invisible natures, receives rule and wisdom, is named through the four directions, and is given four special stars. He receives free will and is shown light and darkness, good and evil. The Lord appoints death because of sin, then makes a wife from Adam's rib; she is called Eve, the mother.
