## Story

The messengers lead Enoch toward the northern region and show him a place of terrible punishment.

There he sees savage darkness and impenetrable gloom, with no ordinary light. A gloomy fire burns continually, and a river of fire flows through the place. Fire surrounds it, yet cold and ice are also present: the place burns and freezes at once.

The prisoners are savage, and the angels who guard them are terrifying and without pity. They carry savage weapons, and the punishment is merciless.

Enoch cries out at the horror of the place. His guides explain that it is prepared for those who dishonour God and commit evil deeds. The text lists sexual wrongdoing, witchcraft, enchantments, devilish magic, boasting in evil, theft, lying, slander, envy, evil thoughts, fornication, and murder.

The list continues with exploitation of the poor: taking their possessions and growing rich at their expense, allowing the hungry to die when they could be fed, and stripping the naked when they could be clothed.

The chapter's final preserved lines condemn those who do not know their Creator and worship lifeless gods and handmade idols. The source transcription ends this chapter at verse 6.
