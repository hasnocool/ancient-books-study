## Story

The Lord orders a separation between light and darkness and brings forth a thick substance, then water beneath the light. He makes the waters firm, surrounds them with light, and creates seven circles like crystal, moist and dry, like glass and ice. He assigns paths to the waters and elements in relation to the seven stars. He separates light and darkness, naming them day and night; evening and morning mark the first day.
