## Story

Enoch tells his children to extend their hands to the poor according to their ability and not to hide their silver in the earth. They should assist an honest person in distress and endure every violent and grievous yoke for the Lord’s sake, trusting in reward at judgment. He urges them to enter the house of the Lord in the morning, afternoon, and evening to glorify the Creator. Finally, everything that has breath and every visible and invisible creature is called to praise Him.
