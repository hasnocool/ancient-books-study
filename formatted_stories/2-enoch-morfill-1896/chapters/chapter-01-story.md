## Story

Enoch recalls that he was 165 years old when his son Methusal was born. He then lived another 200 years, completing 365 years in all.

On the first day of the first month, Enoch was alone in his house and resting on his bed. During sleep, a deep grief came over him. He wept in the dream but could not understand what the grief meant or what would happen.

Two extraordinarily tall men appeared beside his bed. Their faces shone like the sun, their eyes were like burning lamps, and fire came from their mouths. Their clothing seemed feathered; their feet were purple, their wings brighter than gold, and their hands whiter than snow. They called Enoch by name.

Enoch awoke, saw the two men clearly, and bowed before them in fear. They told him not to be afraid: the everlasting God had sent them, and that very day he would ascend with them into heaven. They instructed him to tell his sons and the people who worked in his house, and to ensure that no one searched for him until the Lord brought him back.

Enoch obeyed. He went outside, called his sons Methusal, Regim, and Gaidal, and told them what the two visitors had said.
