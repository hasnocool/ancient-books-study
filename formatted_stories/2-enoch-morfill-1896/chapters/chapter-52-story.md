## Story

Enoch sets paired blessings and curses before his children. The blessed praise God, bless His works, work with their own hands, preserve their fathers’ foundations, and establish peace inwardly as well as outwardly. The cursed misuse speech, exploit another’s labour, break inherited enactments, or disturb peace. Enoch says these matters will be disclosed in the great judgment.
