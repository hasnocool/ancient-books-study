## Story

Enoch teaches that God made human beings with His own hands and in the likeness of His countenance, both small and great. Therefore, whoever reviles another person’s face reviles the Lord’s countenance. Unprovoked anger and insulting spitting bring the threat of divine judgment. Blessed, by contrast, is the person who harbours no malice, helps the injured and accused, raises the oppressed, and fulfils the request of one who asks. Enoch adds that measures, standards, and weights used in trade will be known by their own measure and receive a measured reward at the great judgment.
