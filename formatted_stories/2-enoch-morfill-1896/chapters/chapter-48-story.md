## Story

Enoch describes the sun moving through seven circles of heaven. He says that he assigned it 182 thrones during the short day and another 182 during the long day, as well as two great thrones on which it rests while moving between the monthly thrones. He names the months Tsivan, Thevan, and Thevad and gives the sun’s descent and ascent by reference to the seventeenth day. The earth rejoices and bears fruit as the sun approaches, but becomes sad and its trees and fruits cease developing when the sun departs. Enoch says that all things have been arranged by measured wisdom and that the invisible Creator made visible things from what is invisible. He then urges his children to distribute his books among their families and the nations, to receive and read them, and warns that those who reject them and do not fear God will face terrible judgment. He blesses the person who bears the books’ yoke, who will be set free on the day of judgment.
