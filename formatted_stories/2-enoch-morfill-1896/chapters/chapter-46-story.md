## Story

Enoch asks his people to consider an earthly prince who receives gifts from someone whose heart is disloyal. If the prince knows the truth, he will reject the gifts and condemn the giver. Likewise, a person who speaks flattering words while planning evil will be exposed by the other person’s discernment and condemned. Enoch then says that when God sends a great light, it will bring judgment upon the just and unjust, and nothing will remain concealed.
