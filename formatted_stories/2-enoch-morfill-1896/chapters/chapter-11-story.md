## Story

The messengers take Enoch to the fourth heaven. They show him the movements and rays of the sun and moon. Enoch measures their courses and calculates their light.

He sees that the sun's light is seven times greater than the moon's. He observes their circles and the chariots in which they travel, moving with astonishing speed like the wind and never resting, by day or by night.

Four great stars stand on each side of the sun's chariot. Each has a thousand stars beneath it, making eight thousand in all.

A vast company of angels goes out with the sun and attends it during the day; a thousand attend by night. Each angel has six wings, and they go before the sun's chariot. Another hundred angels keep the sun warm and light it.
