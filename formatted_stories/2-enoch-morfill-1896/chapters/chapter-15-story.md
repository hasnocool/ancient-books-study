## Story

The phoenixes and chalkadri sing. Their song causes every bird to clap its wings in rejoicing at the giver of light. They sing at the Lord's command.

Their song announces that the giver of light comes to bring brightness to the whole world.

The guides also show Enoch the calculation of the sun's course. Its gates of entry and departure are great gates made by God for the reckoning of the year. The chapter concludes by declaring that the sun is great.
