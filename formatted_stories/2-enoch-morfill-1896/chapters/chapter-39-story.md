## Story

Enoch gathers his children and tells them that he has been sent to report what was, what is happening, and what will happen before the day of judgment. He insists that the message is not merely his own: he speaks from the lips of the Lord, though his children hear a mortal man. He describes the Lord’s face as heated iron scattering sparks, His eyes as brighter than the sun, His hand as filling the heavens, and His form as measureless. The Lord’s voice sounded to Enoch like great thunder amid continually moving clouds. Enoch concludes by comparing the terror of standing before an earthly prince with the greater terror of standing before the Lord of lords and the heavenly and earthly hosts.
