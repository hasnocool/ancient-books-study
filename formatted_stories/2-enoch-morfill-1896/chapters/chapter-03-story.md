## Story

After Enoch has spoken to his sons, the two heavenly messengers summon him. They take him on their wings and place him upon the clouds, which begin to move.

As they rise, Enoch sees the air and then, higher still, the ether. The messengers bring him into the first heaven.

There they show him a sea of immense size, greater than the sea on earth.
