## Story

Before departing, Enoch tells his children that he does not know where he is going or what awaits him.

He urges them not to turn away from God. They are to live before the Lord, keep his judgements, and refuse to worship vain gods that did not create heaven and earth. Such gods, and those who worship them, will perish.

Enoch asks that God make their hearts steadfast in reverence for him. He then repeats his instruction that no one should search for him until the Lord brings him back.
