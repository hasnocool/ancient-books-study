## Story

The Lord fashions a fiery nature for the heavenly hosts. Lightning receives its wondrous nature from the brightness of His eye. Fire and water are interwoven: neither extinguishes nor dries the other; lightning is brighter than the sun, and soft water stronger than hard stone. From stone the Lord cuts mighty fire and makes ten thousand spiritual beings with fiery weapons and burning garments. One archangel and the rank below him turn away, imagining a throne above the clouds and equality with divine power. The Lord hurls the rebel and his angels from the heights; the rebel flies continually above the abyss.
