## Story

Enoch sees other flying creatures, called phoenixes and chalkadri. They are strange in appearance: they have the feet and tails of lions and the heads of crocodiles. Their colour is purple like a rainbow, and their size is given as nine hundred measures.

Their wings resemble those of angels, and each creature has twelve. They attend the sun's chariot and travel with it, bringing heat and dew as God commands.

The sun continues its revolutions beneath the heavens and beneath the earth, while its beams give light without ceasing.
