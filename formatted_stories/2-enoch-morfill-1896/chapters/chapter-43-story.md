## Story

Enoch says that he has recorded what he learned from the Lord in both winter and summer, calculating hours and their differences. He compares years with people: just as one year may be more honourable than another, people may be distinguished by possessions, wisdom, understanding, cunning, silence, purity, strength, beauty, youth, sharpness of mind, bodily quickness, or perception. Yet he declares that no one is greater than the person who fears God, who will be most glorious for ever.
