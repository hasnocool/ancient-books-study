## Story

Enoch urges his children to guard against unrighteousness and treat every living soul with care. He describes many mansions prepared for people: good ones for the good and evil ones for the evil. The blessed mansions offer rest, while the evil ones offer no rest or return. He then teaches that offerings must involve both honest work and a willing heart; labour without the heart, or a murmuring heart without fitting labour, brings no success.
