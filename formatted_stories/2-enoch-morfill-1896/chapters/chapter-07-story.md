## Story

The messengers carry Enoch to the second heaven. There he sees a place of darkness, where prisoners hang suspended while awaiting the eternal judgement.

The angels appear even gloomier than the darkness of the earth. They weep continually, hour after hour. Enoch asks why they suffer such torment.

His guides explain that these are angels who turned away from the Lord, refused God's commandments, followed their own will, and joined their prince in transgression. They have already been confined in the second heaven.

Enoch feels pity for them. The angels bow before him and ask him, as a man of God, to pray to the Lord on their behalf.

Enoch replies that he is only a mortal man. He does not know where he is going or what awaits him, and he questions who could pray for him.
