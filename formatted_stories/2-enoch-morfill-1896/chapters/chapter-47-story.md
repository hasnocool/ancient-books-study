## Story

Enoch urges his children to keep his words in their hearts and receive them as teachings that came from the Lord. He tells them to take his books, read them, and learn the works of the Lord. Although many books have existed since creation and will continue to exist, he says that none will reveal things to them as his writings do. If they preserve these writings, they will not sin against God, for there is no other God in heaven, on earth, or in the depths. Enoch then describes foundations established upon unknown things, visible and invisible heavens, the earth set upon waters, waters resting on what is not fixed, and the innumerable works of creation. The surviving verse ends with the unfinished words “so that.”
