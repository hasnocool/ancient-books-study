## Story

In Aravoth, the tenth heaven, Enoch sees the Lord's face compared to burning iron, yet says it cannot be told. The Lord tells him to rise and remain before Him. Michael lifts him, removes his earthly robe, anoints him with holy oil, and clothes him in glorious raiment. The oil shines, resembles dew, and smells of myrrh. Enoch becomes like the glorious ones and loses his fear. Vretil is summoned with books and a reed to instruct him.
