## Story

Vretil teaches Enoch about heaven, earth, sea, celestial movements, weather, time, angels, human life, languages, commandments, and songs. For thirty days and nights Vretil speaks while Enoch writes. Enoch is then told to write about human souls, including those not yet born, and their prepared places. Vretil says every soul was created eternally before the world's foundation. Enoch writes for another thirty days and nights and completes 366 books.
