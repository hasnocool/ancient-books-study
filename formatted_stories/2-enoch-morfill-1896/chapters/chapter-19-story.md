## Story

Enoch sees seven radiant angelic orders in the sixth heaven. They oversee the movements of sun, moon, and stars, the fortunes of the world, teaching, speech, song, seasons, years, rivers, seas, fruits, and herbs. Angels record human deeds and lives. Seven phoenixes, seven cherubim, and seven six-winged creatures sing together before the Lord.
