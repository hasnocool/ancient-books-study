## Story

The Lord calls visible things out of the invisible. Adoil appears, red and radiant. At the Lord's command he bursts asunder, and a great light emerges; within it the great world appears, revealing the creation intended by the Lord. The Lord declares it good, establishes His throne, and commands the light to rise above it as the foundation of things on high.
