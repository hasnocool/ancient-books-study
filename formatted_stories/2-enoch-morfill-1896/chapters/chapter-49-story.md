## Story

Enoch tells his children that he will not swear by heaven, earth, or any creature, because God has said that there is no swearing or injustice in Him, but truth. Where human truthfulness is absent, people should let their words be simply yes or no. Enoch nevertheless affirms to his children that no person has been in the mother’s womb for whom a place has not been prepared for every soul, and that a measure has been fixed for how long each person will be tested in this world. He repeats that a place is prepared for every human soul.
