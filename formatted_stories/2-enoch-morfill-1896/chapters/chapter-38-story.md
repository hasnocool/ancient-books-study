## Story

The Lord tells the men who first took Enoch to bring him back to earth and wait for him until the appointed day. At night they place him on his bed. Mathusal keeps watch, expecting Enoch’s return day and night. When he hears Enoch arrive, he is terrified. Enoch then instructs him to gather the whole household so that he can tell them everything.
