## Story

The guides explain that the garden is prepared for the righteous.

It is for those who endure attacks and afflictions in life, turn their eyes away from unrighteousness, and judge rightly. It is also for those who feed the hungry, clothe the naked, raise those who have fallen, and help oppressed orphans.

Those who walk blamelessly before the Lord and serve him alone are promised this place as an eternal inheritance.
