## Story

Enoch enters the seventh heaven and sees immense light, fiery hosts, archangels, incorporeal powers, principalities, cherubim, seraphim, thrones, and watchfulness of many eyes. Terrified, he is steadied by his guides. They show him the Lord on a lofty throne. The hosts approach in rank, bow on ten steps, and return joyfully to serve and sing.
