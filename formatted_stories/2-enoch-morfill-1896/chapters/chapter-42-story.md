## Story

Enoch sees the guardians of hell, who hold its keys and guard its gates. They resemble great serpents, with faces like quenched lamps, fiery eyes, sharp teeth, and bare upper bodies. He laments that people of his race have come to them after sins committed in this life. He then goes east to the paradise of Eden, prepared for the just, open to the third heaven and shut off from this world. Fiery angels guard its great eastern gates and sing without ceasing. At the final coming they will bring Adam and the forefathers there to rejoice together. Enoch then blesses those who fear and serve the Lord, judge justly without seeking reward, clothe the naked, feed the hungry, defend orphans and widows, help the wronged, leave the unstable path, sow righteousness, speak truth, show love and tenderness, and understand and glorify the Lord’s works.
