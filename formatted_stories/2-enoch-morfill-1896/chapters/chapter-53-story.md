## Story

Enoch warns his children not to rely on the idea that their father’s prayer can release them from their own sins. He says that human works are written before creation and cannot be altered by human speech. God sees even thoughts hidden in the heart. Enoch urges his children to heed his instruction now rather than later complain that he failed to warn them.
