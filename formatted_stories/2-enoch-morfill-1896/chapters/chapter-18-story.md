## Story

Enoch sees the Grigori in the fifth heaven: immense, withered, and silent. His guides explain their rebellion with Satanail, the descent of three to Ermon, their unions with human women, and the resulting corruption and giants. The Grigori mourn condemned brethren. Enoch says he has prayed for them but their sentence remains; he urges the Grigori present to resume service. They take their places in four orders and sing sorrowfully at four trumpets.
