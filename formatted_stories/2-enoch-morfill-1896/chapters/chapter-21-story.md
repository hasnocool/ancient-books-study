## Story

Six-winged creatures sing “Holy, Holy, Holy” before the Lord. Enoch's guides announce that their commission is over and leave him alone at heaven's edge. He falls in fear. Gabriel comes, reassures him, and carries him like a leaf in the wind to the Lord. Enoch sees the eighth heaven, Muzaloth, with the zodiac and seasonal changes, and the ninth, Kukhavim, where the zodiacal signs have heavenly homes.
