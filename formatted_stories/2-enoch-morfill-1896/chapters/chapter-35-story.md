## Story

The Lord promises to leave one righteous man from Enoch’s race together with his household, and says that a numerous generation will later arise from their descendants. Many in that generation will be insatiable. When that family has passed away, the books written by Enoch and his fathers will be brought to the attention of truthful people who please the Lord and do not misuse His name. They will pass the writings to another generation, whose readers will finally receive greater glory.
