## Story

The Lord seats Enoch at His left hand with Gabriel and promises to explain creation: visible things came from the nonexistent and invisible. The Lord says even the angels have not received these secrets or understood the full extent of creation. Before visible things existed, He alone moved among invisible things like the sun travelling east and west. Unlike the sun, He found no rest because He was continually creating.
