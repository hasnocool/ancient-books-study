## Story

Enoch directs his listeners to look at the lights in the sky. They rise and set in their appointed order, each at its proper time, without abandoning the courses assigned to them.

He then turns their attention to the earth. Its processes, from beginning to end, display the consistency of God’s work. Summer and winter bring their own conditions, while clouds, dew, rain, and water cover and sustain the land. The natural world is presented as an example of ordered activity that follows its appointed pattern.
