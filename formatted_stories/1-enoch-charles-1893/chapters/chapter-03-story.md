## Story

Enoch observes the trees as their leaves fall and they appear to wither. He notes an exception: fourteen trees do not lose their foliage in the same way. They keep their old leaves for two or three years, until new growth appears.

The observation is brief and focused on the contrast between the ordinary seasonal change of trees and the unusual persistence of these fourteen.
