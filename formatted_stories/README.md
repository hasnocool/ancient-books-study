# Formatted Stories

Narrative retellings and readable story adaptations derived from the source editions in this repository.

## Purpose

Turn source paragraphs, sections, and chapters into coherent, well-formatted stories while preserving the meaning and sequence of the source.

## Editorial rules

- Keep each story traceable to its source book, edition, chapter/section, and page or paragraph range.
- Preserve the source's key events, claims, people, places, and relationships.
- Distinguish source content from editorial transitions, interpretation, and creative reconstruction.
- Do not present invented dialogue, motives, scenes, or details as though they appear in the source.
- Use clear headings, readable paragraphs, and consistent terminology.
- When summarizing, retain important qualifications and avoid changing the source's meaning.
- Record uncertainties and textual problems rather than silently resolving them.

## Suggested organization

Create one subdirectory per book or source work, then organize files by chapter or section. Use Markdown (`.md`) for drafts and final story texts.

Suggested file naming:
`<book-slug>-chapter-<number>-story.md`

## Suggested story document structure

1. Title
2. Source and passage covered
3. Story
4. Brief note on adaptation choices or unresolved ambiguities

## Production workflow

Follow [`docs/formatted-content-pipeline.md`](../docs/formatted-content-pipeline.md) from the repository root. Start new documents from [`_templates/story-template.md`](_templates/story-template.md). Story and meaning outputs must be grounded independently in the identified source passage; neither is a substitute for the diplomatic text or proofing ledger.
