## Short summary

The chapter holds mortality and hope together: Adam returns to the earth from which he came, but this is described as a sending back rather than annihilation, with a reference to the second coming. The blessing of all creation and the Sabbath is stated directly. The promised return is not elaborated here.

## Key themes and concepts

- **Creation and moral order:** The chapter develops its own account of the created world and human conduct.
- **Textual specificity:** Names, numbers, and descriptions are preserved in the form transmitted by this witness.
