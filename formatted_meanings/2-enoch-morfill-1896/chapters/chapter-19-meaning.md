## Short summary

The chapter depicts heaven as an ordered administration of cosmic, natural, and human affairs. Angelic governance combines oversight, instruction, record-keeping, and praise. The sevenfold groups at the centre are not symbolically explained; their number and grouping are retained.

## Key themes and concepts

- **Order and revelation:** The chapter presents its own account of heavenly or created order.
- **Textual specificity:** Names, numbers, and descriptions are preserved in the form transmitted by this witness.
- **Interpretive restraint:** The passage does not always explain the symbolism or mechanics of what it describes.
