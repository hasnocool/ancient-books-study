## Short summary

The chapter links the Grigori's rebellion and the descent of three beings to earthly corruption and the birth of giants. Enoch's prayer does not reverse the sentence on the condemned brethren, who remain under the earth until heaven and earth end. The Grigori he addresses resume service, but their song remains sorrowful. The distinction between groups, and the spellings Ermon/Iermon, are preserved.
