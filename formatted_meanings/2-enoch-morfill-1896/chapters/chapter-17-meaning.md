## Short summary

Chapter 17 offers a brief vision of worship in the middle of the heavens.

### Verse 1: The host and its music

Enoch sees an armed host serving the Lord with cymbals, organs, and an unceasing voice. The scene combines martial imagery with musical praise. Enoch's response is delight at hearing the heavenly sound.

## Key themes and concepts

- **Heavenly worship:** The host's service is expressed through continuous music and voice.
- **Ordered service:** The host is presented as serving the Lord.
- **Affective response:** Enoch responds with delight, in contrast to the fear and distress of earlier visions.

## Interpretive limits and open questions

- The host is described as armed, but the verse does not narrate a battle.
- The text does not identify the instruments more precisely.
- The chapter is a single-verse scene; no additional episode should be inferred.

## Related passages

- The preceding chapters describe the heavenly bodies and their attendants; this verse shifts to a host serving through music.
