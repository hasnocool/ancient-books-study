## Short summary

Chapter 10 describes a northern place of punishment and gives an extended catalogue of the conduct associated with it.

### Verses 1–3: The place and its torment

The guides take Enoch to the northern region. The place combines darkness, fire, a fiery river, cold, and ice. The simultaneous burning and freezing intensifies the description of an environment wholly unlike ordinary life. Its prisoners and their angelic tormentors are portrayed in severe terms.

### Verses 4–6: The conduct condemned

The guides associate the place with dishonouring God and a broad range of wrongdoing. The list includes magical practices, theft, falsehood, slander, envy, sexual misconduct, murder, and boastfulness about evil.

The passage gives particular attention to economic oppression. It condemns those who take the possessions of the poor, enrich themselves through injury, and withhold food and clothing from people in need when they could provide them.

The final preserved verse condemns ignorance of the Creator and worship of lifeless, handmade idols. The source transcription supplied for this project ends at 10:6; this guide does not infer or supply any further verse.

## Key themes and concepts

- **Judgement:** The place is prepared for conduct that dishonours God.
- **Social injustice:** Exploiting the poor and refusing aid to the hungry and naked receive explicit condemnation.
- **False worship:** The chapter contrasts the Creator with lifeless idols made by human hands.
- **Comprehensive wrongdoing:** Religious, interpersonal, sexual, and economic offences all appear in the catalogue.

## Interpretive limits and open questions

- The phrase “vitium sodomiticum” appears in the 1896 translation; this guide does not silently replace or modernize it.
- The chapter's descriptions of fire and ice are retained as visionary imagery, not a physical account.
- The supplied source ends at verse 6. No missing continuation is reconstructed.
- The text does not identify the northern place with a later named infernal geography.
