## Short summary

The chapter orders the cosmos through separation, water, seven crystalline circles, and assigned paths for elements and stars. Its overlapping comparisons—crystal, glass, and ice—should not be forced into one literal model. The first-day ending is retained, including the sequence tension with Chapter 28.
