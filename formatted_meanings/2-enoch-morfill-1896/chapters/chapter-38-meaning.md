## Short summary

Enoch is returned to earth by the men who first took him, and they are told to wait until an appointed day. Mathusal watches for him and is frightened when he hears him arrive. Enoch asks that the whole household be gathered so he can tell them everything.

## Key themes and concepts

This chapter bridges Enoch’s heavenly experience and his public farewell to his family. The night-time return and Mathusal’s vigil create a scene of recognition and preparation.

## Interpretive limits and open questions

The “appointed day” is not identified here. The chapter does not explain the mechanics of Enoch’s return or what Mathusal had previously been told.
