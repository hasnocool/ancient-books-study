## Short summary

The chapter explains the deception through the devil's envy of Adam's earthly dominion. It distinguishes the devil's former name, Satanail, from his later state as Satan. The Lord's statement that He did not curse humanity or the earth, but the evil fruit and works, is a material qualification. The opening ellipsis is not expanded.
