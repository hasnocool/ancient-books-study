## Short summary

Enoch tells his children to treat the books he has given them as an inheritance of peace. They are not to hide them, but to share them with those who desire them and admonish others to understand the Lord’s wondrous works.

## Key themes and concepts

Written instruction becomes a communal inheritance, meant for circulation rather than private possession.

## Interpretive limits and open questions

The chapter does not identify the books individually or specify how they are to be copied or distributed.

## Related passages

The instruction follows Enoch’s warning that his children must heed and preserve his teaching.
