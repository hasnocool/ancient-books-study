## Short summary

Enoch tells his children to preserve and read his writings, claiming that they disclose the Lord’s works more fully than other books. He connects preservation of the writings with avoiding sin and affirms that there is no other God. He then describes the foundations of creation, but the supplied verse ends abruptly with “so that.”

## Key themes and concepts

The chapter presents the writings as a privileged source of instruction for Enoch’s descendants. Its statements about the books’ unique value are claims made by Enoch in his farewell speech. The cosmological description reinforces the theme that creation rests on foundations and relations not fully visible to human observers.

## Interpretive limits and open questions
