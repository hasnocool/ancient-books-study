## Short summary

The chapter presents Enoch's change from earthly to heavenly condition. The divine face is described vividly but remains ineffable. Michael's clothing and anointing signify transformation without a fuller ritual explanation. Vretil's books introduce Enoch's instruction. The name and details are retained as transmitted.
