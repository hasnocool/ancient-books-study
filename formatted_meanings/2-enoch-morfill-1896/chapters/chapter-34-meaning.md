## Short summary

The chapter gives the moral reason for the coming flood: rejection of the Lord, false worship, and widespread wrongdoing. Its sexual language reflects the source's own polemical categories and is not elaborated beyond the text. The flood is presented as divine judgment. The adaptation does not anticipate the flood's events from later chapters.
