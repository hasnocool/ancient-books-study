## Short summary

Enoch tells his children that the appointed time of his departure is near. Angels will come and wait on earth for the command concerning him. He expects to ascend to his eternal dwelling in the highest heavens the next morning, and urges his family to do good before the Lord.

## Key themes and concepts

The chapter combines the certainty of departure with a final ethical exhortation. The angelic attendance presents Enoch’s departure as an appointed heavenly event.

## Interpretive limits and open questions

The source’s punctuation around “depart, The angels” is awkward; the adaptation conveys the sequence without claiming to repair the transcription. The stated “morning” is retained without supplying a calendar date.
