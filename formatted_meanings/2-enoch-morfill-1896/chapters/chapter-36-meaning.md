## Short summary

The Lord gives Enoch thirty days to instruct his sons and household before angels remove him from earth. The household is to learn that there is no other God, keep the commandments, and read Enoch’s books.

## Key themes and concepts

The interval creates a final teaching period in which Enoch’s household is prepared for his departure. The instruction combines belief, conduct, and textual study: knowing the one God is joined to keeping commandments and reading the writings.

## Interpretive limits and open questions

The chapter does not specify the date of removal, the angels’ identities, or where Enoch will be taken. It should not be expanded with details from other ascension traditions.

## Related passages

The thirty days are explicit and should not be converted into an approximate period. The passage emphasizes the household as the immediate audience for Enoch’s final instruction.
