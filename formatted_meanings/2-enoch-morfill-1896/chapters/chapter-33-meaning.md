## Short summary

The chapter moves from creation to sacred time and Enoch's mission. The eighth day and eighth thousand are associated with a new order beyond ordinary measurement, while the seven-thousand pattern is retained without imposing a complete chronology. Enoch's books preserve knowledge of the Creator across generations. The named angels' distinct roles are maintained.

## Key themes and concepts

- **Creation and moral order:** The chapter develops its own account of the created world and human conduct.
- **Textual specificity:** Names, numbers, and descriptions are preserved in the form transmitted by this witness.
- **Interpretive restraint:** Apparent tensions are documented rather than silently resolved.
