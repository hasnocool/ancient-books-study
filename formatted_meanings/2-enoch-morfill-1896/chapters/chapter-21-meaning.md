## Short summary

The departure of Enoch's first guides marks a transition; Gabriel becomes his intermediary. Enoch's request for his former companions reveals his dependence on them. The eighth and ninth heavens extend the text's cosmological map. Muzaloth and Kukhavim are retained as transliterated, without imposing modern astronomical categories.
