## Short summary

Enoch asks Methosalem to summon his brothers, their families, and the elders so he can speak before departing. Methosalem gathers Regim, Riman, Ukhan, Khermion, [Gaidal], and the elders. Enoch blesses those assembled and begins to address them.

## Key themes and concepts

The chapter stages Enoch’s final public instruction as a gathering of family and community elders.

## Interpretive limits and open questions

The name [Gaidal] is bracketed in the source and must remain qualified. The chapter ends by introducing Enoch’s speech, which continues in Chapter 58.

## Related passages

The assembly directly prepares the extended ethical instruction in Chapters 58–66.
