## Short summary

The chapter joins the fiery constitution of heavenly beings to an archangel's rebellion. Its paradoxes about fire and water express extraordinary created nature rather than a technical account of matter. The rebel seeks to rival divine power. This chapter does not name the rebel, so no name is imported from elsewhere.
