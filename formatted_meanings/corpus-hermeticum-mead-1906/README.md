# Corpus Hermeticum — Formatted Meanings

Plain-language explanations of the tractates in G. R. S. Mead's 1906 English translation, *Thrice-Greatest Hermes*, Volume II.

## Editorial rules

- Ground each explanation in the corresponding tractate and identify section numbers.
- Separate what Mead's translation says from contextual explanation and interpretation.
- Treat historical, philosophical, and linguistic claims as research questions unless supported by reliable scholarship.
- Do not retrofit modern scientific, technological, or biological claims into ancient cosmological language.
- Preserve translation uncertainty and mark unverified source wording.
- Keep meaning explanations distinct from story adaptations.
