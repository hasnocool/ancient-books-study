## Chapter 1

There was a very wise man, and a worker of great things: God loved him, and received him, so that he should see the heavenly abodes, the kingdoms of the wise, great, inconceivable, and never- changing God, the Lord of all, the wonderful, and glorious, and bright, and all-beholding station of the servants of the Lord, and the unapproachable throne of the Lord, and the degrees, and manifestations of the incorporeal hosts, and should be an eye-witness of the unspeakable ministrations of the multitude of creatures, and of the varying appearance, and indescribable, singing, of the host of Cherubim, and of the immeasurable world. At that time he said: Hardly had I accomplished 165 years when I begat my son Metusal: after that I lived 200 years, and accomplished all the years of my life, 365 years. On the first day of the first month, I was alone in my house, and I rested on my bed, and slept. As I slept a great grief came upon my heart, and I wept with mine eyes in my dream, and I could not understand what this grief meant, or what would happen to me. There appeared to me two men very tall, such as I have never seen on earth. Their faces shone like the sun, and their eyes were like burning lamps; and fire came forth from their lips. Their dress had the appearance of feathers: their feet were purple, their wings were brighter than gold; their hands whiter than snow. They stood at the head of my bed, and called me by my name. I awoke from my sleep, and saw clearly these men standing in front of me. I hastened, and made obeisance to them, and was terrified, and the appearance of my countenance was changed from fear. These men said to me: —Be of good cheer Enoch, be not afraid; the everlasting God has sent us to you, and lo, today, you shall ascend with us into heaven. Tell your sons, and your servants, all who work in your house, and let no one seek you, until the Lord bring you back to them. I hastened to obey them, and went out of my house. I called my sons Metusal, Regim, and Gaydal, and told them what wonderful things the two men had said to me.

## Chapter 2

Hear me my children, for I do not know where I am going, or what awaits me. Now my children, I say unto you: turn not aside from God: walk before the face of the Lord, and keep his judgements, and do not worship vain gods who did not make heaven and earth, for these will perish, and also those who worship them. But may God make confident your hearts in the fear of Him. Now my children, let no one seek me until the Lord brings me back to you.

---

## Chapter 3

It came to pass when I had spoken to my sons, these men summoned me, and took me on their wings, and placed me on the clouds. Lo! the clouds moved. Again higher, I saw the air, and higher 6 I saw the ether. They placed me in the first heaven. They showed me a very great sea, greater than the earthly sea.

---

## Chapter 4

And they brought before my face the elders, and the rulers of the orders of the stars. They showed me the two hundred angels who rule the stars, and their heavenly service; And they fly with their wings, and go round all as they float.

---

## Chapter 5

And then I looked, and saw the treasuries of the snow, and ice, and the angels who guard their terrible store-places, and the treasuries of the clouds from which they come forth, and into which they enter.

---

## Chapter 6

And they showed me the treasuries of the dew, like oil for anointing, and its form was in appearance like that of all earthly colours. Also many angels keeping their treasuries, and they shut, and open them.

---

## Chapter 7

And the men took me, and brought me to the second heaven, and showed me the darkness. There I saw the prisoners suspended, reserved for awaiting the eternal judgement. These angels were gloomy in appearance, more than the darkness of the earth. They unceasingly wept every hour. And I said to the men who were with me: Why are these men continually tortured? And the men answered me: —These they who repelled from the Lord: who obeyed not the commandments of God, and took counsel of their own will, and transgressed together with their prince, and have been already confined to the second heaven. I felt great pity for them. Lo! The angels made obeisance to me, and said to me: —O man of God! Pray for us to the Lord. I answered them: Who am, a mortal man, that I should pray for angels? Who knows where I go, or what awaits me, or who prays for me?

Enoch 12. Before all these things fell out, Enoch was hidden and no one of the children of men knew where he was hidden, and where he abode, and what had become of him. All his activities had to do with the holy ones, and with the watchers in his days. I Enoch, was blessing the great Lord, and the king of the world, when lo! The watchers called me Enoch, the scribe, and spoke to me. Enoch you scribe of righteousness, go, announce to the watchers of the heaven who have abandoned the high heaven, and the holy eternal place, and have defiled themselves with women, and have done as the children of men do, and have taken unto themselves wives, and have grossly defiled themselves on the earth. They will have no peace on the earth nor forgiveness of sin:, and inasmuch as they delight themselves in their children, the murder of their beloved ones they shall see, and over the destruction of their children will they lament, and will make supplication unto eternity, but mercy, and peace will they not attain.

Chapt 13. Enoch went and said: —Azazel. You shall find no peace. A severe sentence has gone forth against you. Rufael shall put you in bonds. Alleviation, intercession, and mercy will not be accorded to you, because of the oppression which you have taught, because of all the works of blasphemy, oppression, and sin which you have shown to the children of men. Then proceeding farther, I spoke to them all together, and they were all afraid, and were seized with fear, and trembling. They besought me to draw up a petition for them that they might find forgiveness, and to take their petition into the presence of God in heaven. For from thenceforward they could not speak nor lift up their eyes to heaven for shame of their sins for which they were punished. Then I composed their petition, and the prayer on behalf of their spirit, for their individual 7 deeds for which they besought forgiveness, and forbearance. I went off, and sat down at the waters of Dan, in Dan, to the right of the west of Hermon; I read their petition until I fell asleep. Behold a dream visited me, and visions fell down upon me. I saw the vision of a chastisement to the intent that I should recount it to the sons of the heaven to reprimand them. When I awaked, I came to them, and they were all sitting together weeping with their faces covered at Ublesjael, which is between Lebanon, and Senecer. I recounted to them all the visions which I had seen in my sleep, and I began to recount those words of righteousness, and to reprimand the heavenly watchers.

Enoch 14. This book is the word of righteousness, and the reprimand of the eternal watchers, in accordance with the commandment of the Holy and Great One in that vision. I saw in my sleep what I will now recount with a tongue of flesh, and with my breath, which the Great One has put into the mouth of men, that they might converse with it, and understand in their heart. As He has created man, and given to him the power of understanding the word of wisdom, so has He created me also, and given me the power of reprimanding the watchers, the sons of heaven. I wrote out your petition, and in my vision it appeared so: your petition will not be granted throughout all the days of eternity. That judgment has been finally passed upon you, and no indulgence will be granted to you. From henceforth, you will never again ascend into heaven to all eternity. The decree has gone forth on the earth; they shall bind you for all the days of eternity. But previously you will see the destruction of your beloved sons, and you will not have them in your keeping, but they will fall before you by the sword. Your petition on their behalf will not be granted, nor that on your own: Likewise, despite your tears and prayers. You will receive nothing whatever contained in the writing which I have written.

The vision appeared to me so: behold in the vision clouds invited me, and a mist invited me: the course of the stars, and the lightnings drove, and impelled me, and the winds in the vision gave me wings, and drove me. They lifted me up into heaven, and I came until I drew nigh to a wall which is built of crystals, and surrounded by a fiery flame: and it began to affright me. I went into the fiery flame, and drew nigh to a large house which was built of crystals: and the walls of that house were like a mosaic crystal floor, and its groundwork was of crystal. Its ceiling was like the path of the stars, and lightnings, with fiery cherubim between, and their heaven was water. A flaming fire surrounded the walls of the house, and its portal blazed with fire. I entered into that house, and it was hot as fire, and cold as ice: there were no delights of life therein: fear covered me, and trembling gat hold upon me. As I quaked, and trembled, I fell upon my face, and beheld in a vision. Lo! There was a second house, greater than the former, all the portals of which stood open before me, and it was built of flames of fire. In every respect it so excelled in splendour, and magnificence, and extent that I cannot describe to you its splendour, and its extent. Its floor was fire, and above it were lightnings, and the path of the stars, and its ceiling also was flaming fire. I looked, and saw therein a lofty throne: its appearance was as hoarfrost; its circuit was as a shining sun, and the voices of Cherubim. From underneath the great throne came streams of flaming fire so that it was impossible to look thereon. The Great Glory sat thereon, and His raiment shone more brightly than the sun, and was whiter than any snow. None of the angels could enter, and could behold the face of the Honoured and Glorious One, and no flesh could behold Him. A flaming fire was round about Him, and a great fire stood before Him, and none of those who were around Him could draw near Him: ten thousand times ten thousand were before Him, but He stood in no need of counsel. The holiness of the holy ones, who were near Him, did not leave by night nor depart from Him. Until 8 then I had had a veil on my face, and I was trembling. Then the Lord called me with His own mouth, and spoke to me: —Come here Enoch, and hear My holy word. He made me rise up, and approach the door: but I turned my face downwards.

Enoch 15. He answered, and spoke to me with His voice I have heard: —Fear not Enoch, you righteous man, and scribe of righteousness: Approach, and hear my voice. Go say to the watchers of heaven who have sent you to intercede for them: You should intercede for men, and not men for you. Wherefore have you left the high, holy, eternal heaven, and lain with women, and defiled yourselves with the daughters of men, and taken unto yourselves wives, and done like to the children of earth, and begotten giants sons. Whilst you were still spiritual, holy, in the enjoyment of eternal life, you have denied yourselves with women, have begotten with the blood of flesh, and have lusted after the blood of men, and produced flesh and blood, as those produce them who are mortal and short lived. Therefore I have given them wives also that they might impregnate them and children be borne by them, that so nothing might be, wanting to them on earth. But you were formerly spiritual, in the enjoyment of eternal immortal life, for all generations of the world. Therefore I have not appointed wives for you; for the spiritual have their dwelling in heaven. Now, the giants, who are produced from the spirits, and flesh, will be called evil spirits upon the earth, and on the earth will be their dwelling. Evil spirits proceed from their bodies; because they are created from above, from the holy watchers is their beginning and primal origin; they will be evil spirits on earth, and evil spirits will they be named. The spirits of heaven have their dwelling in heaven, but the spirits of the earth, which were born upon the earth, have on the earth their dwelling. The spirits of the giants will devour, oppress, destroy, attack, do battle, and cause destruction on the earth, and work affliction: they will take no kind of food, nor will they thirst, and they will be invisible. These spirits will rise up against the children of men, and against the women, because they have proceeded from them.

Enoch 16. In the days of murder, and of destruction, and of the death of the giants, when the spirits have gone forth from the souls of their flesh, in order to destroy without incurring judgment. So they will destroy until the day when the great consummation of the great world be consummated over the watchers, and the godless. Now as to the watchers who have sent you to intercede for them, who had been aforetime in heaven. Say to them: — You have been in heaven, and though the hidden things had not yet been revealed to you, you knew worthless mysteries, and these in the hardness of your hearts you have recounted to the women, and through these mysteries women, and men work much evil on earth. Therefore Say to them: Have no peace.

## Chapter 8

And these men took me from thence, and brought me to the third heaven, and placed me in the midst of a garden, a place such as has never been known for the goodliness of its appearance. I saw all the trees of beautiful colours, and their fruits ripe, and fragrant, and all kinds of food which they produced, springing up with delightful fragrance. In the midst the tree of life, in that place, on which God rests when He comes into Paradise. This tree cannot be described for its excellence, and sweet odour. It is beautiful more than any created thing. On all sides in appearance it is like gold, and crimson, and transparent as fire, and it covers everything. From its root in the garden, there go forth four streams which pour honey, and milk, oil, and wine, and are separated in four directions, and go about with a soft course. They go down to the Paradise of Eden, between corruptibility, and incorruptibility. Thence they go along the earth, and have a revolution in their circle like also the other elements. There is another tree, an olive tree always distilling oil. There is no tree there without fruit, and every tree is blessed. There are three hundred angels very glorious, who keep the garden, and with never ceasing voices, and blessed singing, they serve the Lord every day. I said: What a very blessed place this is!

---

## Chapter 9

And those men spake unto me: —This place, Enoch, is prepared for the righteous who endure every kind of attack in their lives from those who afflict their souls: who turn away their eyes from unrighteousness, and accomplish a righteous judgement, and also give bread to the hungry, clothe the naked, raise the fallen, assist the orphans who are oppressed, and who walk without blame before the face of the Lord and serve him only. This place is prepared for them as an eternal inheritance.

---

## Chapter 10

Then the men led me to the Northern region, and showed me there a very terrible place. There are all sorts of tortures in that place. Savage darkness, and impenetrable gloom; and there is no light there, but a gloomy fire is always burning and a fiery river goes forth. All that place has fire on all sides, and on all sides cold, and ice, so it burns, and freezes. The prisoners are very savage. The angels terrible and without pity, carrying savage weapons, and their torture was unmerciful. I said: Woe, woe, how terrible is this place! And the men said to me: —This place, Enoch, is prepared for those who do not honour God; who commit evil deeds on earth: sodomitic vice, witchcraft, enchantments, devilish magic, and who boast of their evil deeds: stealing, lying, calumnies, envy, evil thoughts, fornication, and murder. Who steal the souls of wretched men, oppressing the poor, and spoiling them of their possessions, and themselves grow rich by the taking of other men’s possessions, injuring them. Who when they might feed the hungry, allow them to die of famine; who when they might clothe them, strip them naked. Who do not know their Creator, and have worshipped gods without life who can neither see nor hear, being vain gods; and have fashioned the forms of idols and bow down to a contemptible thing, made with hands; for all these this place is prepared for an eternal inheritance.

Enoch 17. They took me away to a place where there were forms like flaming fire, and when they wished they appeared as men. They conducted me to the place of the whirlwind, and to a mountain, the point of whose summit reached to heaven. I saw the places of the luminaries, and of the thunder at the ends thereof; in the depths thereof, a fiery bow and arrows, and their quiver, and a fiery sword, and all the lightnings. They took me to the water of life, and to the fire of the west, which receives every setting of the sun. I came to a river of fire, the fire of which flows like water, and discharges itself into the great sea towards the west. I saw all the great rivers, and came to a great darkness, and went to the place where all flesh wanders. I saw the mountains of the darkness of winter, and the place whence the waters of the entire deep flow. I saw the mouths of all the rivers of the earth, and the mouth of the deep.

10 Enoch 18. I saw the chambers of all the winds, and I saw how He had furnished with them the whole creation and the firm foundations of the earth. I saw the cornerstone of the earth; I saw the four winds which bear the earth, and the firmament of the heaven. I saw how the winds stretch out the vaults of heaven, and have their station between heaven and earth: these are the pillars of the heaven. I saw the winds which turn the heaven, which bring the circumference of the sun, and all the stars to their setting. I saw the winds on the earth which carry the clouds; and I saw the paths of the angels: I saw at the end of the earth the firmament of the heaven above. I proceeded towards the south, and there it burns day and night, where there are seven mountains of magnificent stones; three towards the east and three towards the south: indeed of those towards the east: one was of coloured stone, one of pearls, one of antimony; and those towards the south of red stone. But the middle one reached to heaven like the throne of God, of alabaster, and the summit of the throne was of sapphire. I saw a flaming fire which was in all the mountains. I saw there a place over against the great earth: there the heavens were gathered together. I saw a deep abyss with pillars of heavenly fire, and among them I saw pillars of heavenly fire fall which were in number beyond count, alike towards the height, and towards the depth. Over that abyss, I saw a place which had no firmament of the heaven above, and no foundation of earth beneath it: there was no water upon it, and no birds, but it was a waste place. What I saw there was horrible. Seven stars like great burning mountains, and like spirits, which besought me. The angel said: —This is the place where heaven and earth terminate. It serves for a prison for the stars of heaven and the host of heaven. The stars which roll over the fire, are they which have transgressed the commandment of God before their rising, because they did not come forth at the appointed time. He was wroth with them and bound them until the time when their guilt should be consummated in the year of the mystery.

Enoch 19. Uriel said to me: —Here will stand the angels who have connected themselves with women, and their spirits assuming many different forms have defiled mankind, and will lead them astray into sacrificing to demons as gods; here they will stand until the day of the great judgment on which they shall be judged until they are consummated. With their women also who led astray the angels of heaven, it will fare in like manner as with their friends. I, Enoch, alone saw the vision, the ends of all things: and no man will see what I have seen as I have seen.

Enoch 20. These are the names of the holy angels who watch. Uriel, one of the holy angels, the angel over the world and over Tartarus. Rufael, one of the holy angels, the angel of the spirits of men. Raguel, one of the holy angels, who takes vengeance on the world and on the luminaries. Michael, one of the holy angels, to wit, he that is set over the best part of mankind, over the people. Saraqael, one of the holy angels who is set over the spirits of the children of men whose spirits have sinned. Gabriel, one of the holy angels who is over Paradise, the seraphim and the cherubim.

Enoch 21. I went round to the place where nothing was made. I saw there something horrible. I saw neither a heaven above nor a firmly founded earth, but a place chaotic and horrible. Here I saw seven stars of the heaven bound together in it, like great mountains, and flaming as with fire. On this occasion I said: For what sin are they bound, and on what account they have been cast in here Then spoke Uriel, one of the holy angels, who was with me, and was chief over them, and said: —Wherefore do you ask, and why dost you 11 enquire, and are curious? These are the stars which have transgressed the commandment of God, and are bound here until ten thousand ages, the number of the days of their guilt, are consummated. From thence I went to another place, which was still more horrible than the former, and I saw a horrible thing: a great fire was there which flamed and blazed. The place was cleft as far as the abyss, being full of great descending columns of fire: its extent and size I could not see, nor was I able to see its origin. Then I spoke: How horrible this place is and how hideous to look upon! Then Uriel answered me, one of the holy angels who was with me: he answered and spoke to me: —Why do you entertain such fear, and alarm at this horrible place and in the presence of this pain? He spoke to me: —This place is the prison of the angels, and here they will be imprisoned for ever.

## Chapter 11

And the men took me, and conducted me to the fourth heaven, and showed me all the comings, and goings forth, and all the rays of the light of the sun and moon. I measured their goings and computed their light. I saw that the sun has a light seven times greater than the moon. I beheld their circle and their chariot, on which each goes like a wind advancing with astonishing swiftness, and they have no rest day or night coming or going. There are four great stars; each star has under it a thousand stars at the right of the chariot of the sun; and four at the left , each having under it a thousand stars, altogether eight thousand fifteen myriads of angels go out with the sun, and attend him during the day, and by night one thousand. Each angel has six wings. They go before the chariot of the sun. A hundred angels keep warm and light up the sun.

Enoch 22. Then I went to another place, and he showed me in the west a great high mountain and hard rocks, and four beautiful places. There were there deep and wide places perfectly smooth, as smooth as something which rolls, and deep, and black to look at. This time Rufael answered me, one of the holy angels who was with me and spoke to me: —These hollow places, whereon the spirits of the souls of the dead are assembled, have been created to this very end, that all the souls of the children of men should assemble here. These places are appointed as their habitation until the day of their judgment, and until their appointed period, and this appointed period is long until the great judgment comes upon them. I saw the spirits of the children of men who were dead, and their voice penetrated to the heaven and complained. This time I asked the angel Rufael who was with me, and spoke to him: Whose spirit is that one yonder whose voice so penetrates and complains? He answered me, and spoke so to me saying: —This is the spirit which went forth from Abel, whom his brother Cain slew, and he keeps complaining of him until his seed is destroyed from the face of the earth, and his seed disappears from amongst the seed of men. Therefore at that time I asked regarding him, and regarding the judgment of all: Why is one separated from the other? He answered me and spoke to me: —These three divisions are made to separate the spirits of the dead. And the souls of the righteous are so separated: there is a spring of water, and light above it. Such likewise has been made for sinners when they die, and are buried in the earth without incurring judgment in their lifetime. Here their souls are placed apart in this great pain, until the great day of judgment and punishment, and torture of the revilers for ever, and vengeance for their souls: there they will be bound for ever. Such a division has been made for the souls of those who complain, and make known their destruction when they were slain in the days of the sinners. So it has been made for the souls of men who were not righteous, but sinners complete in their crimes: they will be with criminals like themselves; but their souls will not be slain on the day of judgment, nor will they be raised from thence. Then I blessed the Lord of glory, and said: Blessed be my Lord, the Lord of righteousness, who rules for ever!

12 Enoch 23. From thence I went to another place towards the west, unto the ends of the earth. I saw a burning fire which ran without resting, and paused not from its course day or night but regularly. I asked, saying: What is this which rests not? This time Raguel, one of the holy angels who was with me, answered and spoke to me: —This burning fire in the direction of the west, the course of which you have seen all the luminaries of heaven.

Enoch 24. From thence I went to another place of the earth, and he showed me a mountain range of fire which flamed day, and night. I approached it and saw seven magnificent mountains, each different from the other, and magnificent beautiful stones, magnificent as a whole, of glorious appearance and fair exterior: three towards the east: one founded on the other, and three towards the south: one upon the other, and deep winding ravines; no one of which joined with any other. The seventh mountain was between these, and in their elevation they all resemble the seats of a throne:, and the throne was encircled with fragrant trees. Amongst them was a tree such as I had never yet smelt. Neither was any amongst them nor were others like it; it had a fragrance beyond all fragrance: its leaves, and blooms, and wood wither not for ever:, and its fruit is beautiful, and it resembles the dates of a palm. On this occasion I said: Behold this beautiful tree, beautiful to look upon; its leaves are fair and its fruit very delightful to the eye. Then answered Michael, one of the holy, and honoured angels who was with me, and was in charge thereof.

Enoch 25. He spoke to me: —Enoch, what do you ask as touching the fragrance of this tree, and what do you seek to know? Then I, Enoch answered him and said: I should like to know about everything, but especially about this tree. He answered me, and said: —This high mountain which you has seen, whose summit is like the throne of the Lord, is His throne, where the Holy and Great One, the Lord of Glory, the Eternal King, will sit when He shall come down to visit the earth with goodness. No mortal is permitted to touch this tree of delicious fragrance until the great day of judgment, when He shall avenge and bring everything to its consummation for ever. This tree, I say, will be given to the righteous and humble. By its fruit life will be given to the elect: it will be transplanted to the north, to the holy place, to the temple of the Lord, the Eternal King. Then they will rejoice with joy and be glad: they will enter the holy habitation: the fragrance thereof will be in their limbs, and they will live a long life on earth such as your fathers have lived. And in their days no sorrow or pain or trouble or calamity will affect them. Then I blessed the Lord of Glory, the Eternal King, because He has prepared such for the righteous, and has created it and promised to give it to them.

Enoch 26. I went from thence to the middle of the earth and I saw a blessed and fruitful place, and there were branches there which had taken root, and grew out of a dismembered tree. Then I saw a holy mountain, and underneath the mountain to the east of it, a stream which flowed towards the south. I saw towards the east another mountain of the same height, and between them a deep and narrow ravine: in it also ran a stream skirting the mountain. To the west thereof there was another mountain, lower than the former and of small elevation, and underneath it, and between them both was a ravine and other deep, and sterile ravines were at the extremities of all three. All the ravines were deep and narrow of hard rock, and trees were planted upon them. I marvelled at the rocks, and I marvelled at the ravine, yea, I marvelled very much.

Enoch 27. 13 Then I said: For what object is this blessed land which is entirely filled with trees, and this accursed valley between. Then Uriel, one of the holy angels who was with me, answered me and spoke: —This accursed valley is for those who are accursed for ever: here will all those be gathered together who utter unseemly words with their lips against God, and speak hard things of His Glory; here will they be gathered together and here is the place of their punishment. In the last days there will be the spectacle of a righteous judgment upon them in the presence of the righteous continually for ever: here will those who have found mercy bless the Lord of glory, the Eternal King. In the days of judgment over the former, they will bless Him for the mercy in accordance with which He has assigned them. At that time I also blessed the Lord of Glory, and spoke to Him, and remembered His greatness, as was befitting.

Enoch 28. Then I went towards the east, into the midst of the mountain range of the desert, and I saw here nothing save a plain. Nevertheless it was filled with trees of this seed and water streamed down from above over it. It was manifest that there were many watercourses which flowed as well towards the north as to the west, and here also as everywhere water and dew ascended.

Enoch 29. I went to another place, away from the desert, drawing near to the east of the mountain range. Then I saw the trees of judgment, particularly such as give forth the fragrance of frankincense, and myrrh, and the trees also were similar.

Enoch 30. Above that above these, above the Eastern Mountain, and not far distant, I saw another place, valleys with unfailing streams. I saw a beautiful tree, which resembles a fragrant tree such as the mastic. on the sides of those valleys I saw sweet smelling cinnamon And passing over these I drew nigh to the east.

Enoch 31. I saw other mountains on which there were trees, and there flowed forth from them as it were nectar, it is named Sarira and Galbanum. Over that mountain I saw another mountain whereon were aloe trees, and those trees were full of a hard substance like almonds. One of that fruit was better than all fragrant odours.

Enoch 32. After these fragrant odours, as I looked towards the north, over the mountains, I saw seven mountains full of choice nard, and aromatic trees, and cinnamon, and pepper. Thence I went over the summits of those mountains, far towards the east, and passed above the Eritrean Sea, and went far from it, and passed over the angel Zutel. I came into the garden of righteousness, and saw beyond those trees many large trees growing there of goodly fragrance, large, very beautiful, and glorious, and the tree of wisdom which imparts great wisdom to those who eat of it. It is like the Carob tree: its fruit is like the clusters of the vine, very beautiful: the fragrance of the tree goes forth, and penetrates afar. I said: This tree is beautiful! How beautiful and attractive is its look! The holy angel Rufael, who was with me, answered me, and said: —This is the tree of wisdom of which your old father, and your aged mother, who were before you, have eaten and they learnt wisdom, and their eyes were opened, and they recognised that they were naked, and were driven out of the garden.

14 Enoch 33. From thence I went to the ends of the earth and saw there great beasts, each differed from the other, and birds also differing in appearance, and beauty, and voice, and they all differed the one from the other. To the east of these beasts I saw the ends of the earth whereon the heaven rests, and the portals of the heaven were open. I saw how the stars of heaven come forth and I counted the portals out of which they proceed, and wrote down all their outlets, of each individual star by itself, according to their number, their names, their connexions, their positions, their times, and their months, as the holy angel Uriel who was with me showed me. He showed all things to me and wrote them down for me: also their names he wrote for me, and their laws, and their companies.

Enoch 34. From thence I went towards the north to the ends of the earth. There I saw a great, and glorious wonder at the ends of the whole earth. Here I saw three open portals of heaven in the heaven: from each of them proceed north winds: when one of these blows there is: cold, hail, frost, snow, dew, and rain. Out of one portal it blows for good: but when they blow through the two other portals, it is with violence and it brings misfortune on the earth, and they blow violently.

Enoch 35. From thence I went towards the west to the ends of the earth, and saw there, three open portals such as I saw in the East, the like portals, and the like outlets.

Enoch 36. From thence, I went to the south to the ends of the earth, and saw there, three open portals of the heaven thence come the south wind, dew, rain, and wind. From thence I went to the east to the ends of the heaven, and saw here, the three eastern portals of heaven open and small portals above them. Through each of these small portals pass the stars of heaven, and run their course to the west on the path which is shown to them. When I saw it, I blessed, and so each time I blessed the Lord of Glory who had made the great and glorious wonders, to show the greatness of His work to the angels, and the souls of men, that they might praise His work and all His creation, that they might see the work of His might, and praise the great work of His hands, and bless Him for ever.

Book 2 - Parables

Enoch 37. The second vision of wisdom. The vision which saw Enoch, the son of Jared saw, son of Mahalalel, son of Cainan, son of Enos, son of Seth, son of Adam. This is the beginning, of the words of wisdom which I lifted up my voice to declare, and recount to those which dwell on earth: hear, you men of old time, and see, you that come after, the holy words which I will speak before the Lord of Spirits. It were better to declare to those men of old time, but even from those that come after, we will not withhold the beginning of wisdom. Until the present day the Lord of Spirits has never given such wisdom as I have received, according to my insight, according to the good pleasure of the Lord of Spirits, by whom the lot of eternal life has been given to me.  Three Parables were imparted to me. I lifted up my voice and recounted them to those that dwell on the earth.

First parable.

15 Enoch 38. When the congregation of the righteous will appear, sinners are judged for their sins and driven from the face of the earth. When the Righteous One shall appear before the eyes of the elect righteous, whose works are wrought in dependence on the Lord of Spirits, light will appear to the righteous and the elect who dwell on the earth, where then will be the dwelling of the sinners, where the resting place of those who have denied the Lord of Spirits? It had been good for them if they had not been born. When the secrets of the righteous shall be revealed, then will the sinners be judged and the godless driven from the presence of the righteous, and elect. From that time those who possess the earth will no longer be powerful, and exalted, and they will not be able to behold the face of the holy, for the light of the Lord of Spirits is seen on the face of the holy, and righteous and elect. Then the kings, and the mighty perish, will be given into the hand of the righteous and holy. Thenceforward none will seek for mercy for them with the Lord of Spirits, for their life is at an end.

Enoch 39. It will come to pass in those days that elect, and holy children of the high heaven will descend, and their seed will become one with the children of men. In those days, Enoch, received books of zeal, and wrath, and books of disquiet, and expulsion, , and f mercy will not be accorded to them say the Lord of Spirits. In those days a cloud, and a whirlwind carried me off from the earth, and set me down at the end of the heavens. Here I saw another vision, the mansions of the holy and the resting places of the righteous. Here mine eyes saw their dwellings with His righteous angels, and their resting places with the holy, and they petitioned and interceded, and prayed for the children of men; righteousness flowed before them as water, and mercy like dew upon the earth so it is they fare for ever and ever. In that place mine eyes saw the Elect One of righteousness, and of faith, and how righteousness shall prevail in his days, and the righteous and elect shall be without number before him for ever and ever. I saw his dwelling place under the wings of the Lord of Spirits, and all the righteous and elect before him are beautifully resplendent as lights of fire, and their mouth is full of blessing; their lips extol the name of the Lord of Spirits and righteousness before Him never fail, and uprightness never fails before Him.  Here I wished to dwell, and my soul longed for that dwelling place: here already heretofore had been my portion, for so has it been established concerning me, before the Lord of Spirits. In those days, I lauded and extolled the name of the Lord of Spirits with blessings, and praises, because He has destined for me blessing and glory, according to the good pleasure of the Lord of Spirits. For a long time my eyes regarded that place, and I blessed Him and extolled Him, saying: Blessed be, and may He be blessed from the beginning for evermore. Before Him there is no ceasing. He knows what the world is before it is created, and generation unto generation that shall arise: Those who sleep not bless you: they stand before your glory, and bless, laud, and extol, saying: —Holy, holy, holy, is the Lord of Spirits: He fills the earth with spirits. Here my eyes saw all those who sleep not, how they stand before Him, bless and say: —Blessed be you, and blessed be the name of the Lord for ever and ever. My face was changed until I could no longer see.

## Chapter 12

And I looked and saw, other flying creatures, their names, phoenixes and shalkidri, wonderful, and strange in appearance, with the feet and tails of lions, and the heads of crocodiles; their appearance was of a purple colour, like the rainbow; their size, nine hundred measures. Their wings were like those of angels, each with twelve, and they attend the chariot of the sun, and go with him, bringing heat and dew, as they are ordered by God, So the sun makes his revolutions, goes under the heavens, and goes under the earthwith the light of his beams unceasingly.
