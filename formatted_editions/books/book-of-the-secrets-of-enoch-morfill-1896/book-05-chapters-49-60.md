# Chapters 49–60

## Chapter 49

For I swear to you my children; but I will not swear by a single oath; neither by heaven, nor by earth, nor by any other creature, which God made. God said: —There is no swearing in me, nor injustice, but truth. If there is no truth in men, let them swear by a word: —yea yea; or, nay nay. «… ; … » But I swear to you: yea yea! That there has not been, even a man in his mother’s womb, for whom a place has not been prepared for every soul. A measure is fixed, how long a man shall be tried in this world. O my children, be not deceived; for there is a place prepared there for every soul of man.

## Chapter 50

I have laid down in the writings the actions of every man; and no one born on the earth can hide himself, nor can his deeds be concealed. I see all. Now therefore, my children, in patience and meekness, accomplish the number of your days and you shall inherit the endless life which is to come. Every wound, every affliction, every evil word, and attack; endure for the sake of the Lord. When you might have vengeance, do not repay, either your neighbour or your enemy. For God will repay, as your avenger, in the day of the great judgement. Let it not be for you to take vengeance. Whoever of you shall spend gold or silver, for the sake of a brother, and stretch out your hands to the orphan, the widow, and the stranger, shall receive abundant treasure in the day of judgement.

---

## Chapter 51

Stretch out your hands to the poor man, according to your powers. Do not hide your silver in the earth: assist the honest man in his affliction; and affliction shall not come upon you in the time of your labour. Whatever violent and grievous yoke shall be put upon you, endure all for the Lord’s 60 sake; and so you will receive your reward in the day of judgement. Morning, afternoon, and evening, it is good to go into the house of the Lord to glorify the Creator of all. Wherefore let every thing that has breath, glorify Him, and let every creature visible and invisible, give forth praise.

---

## Chapter 52

Blessed is the man who opens his lips to praise God of Sabaot (God of Hosts), and praises the Lord with his heart. Cursed is every man who opens his lips to abuse and calumniate his neighbour. Blessed is he who opens his lips to the blessing and praise of God! Cursed is he who opens his lips to swearing and blasphemy before the face of the Lord, all his days. Blessed is he who blesses all the works of the Lord! Cursed is he who speaks against the works of the Lord. Blessed is he who looks to lift his own hand for labour. Cursed is he who looks to make use of another man’s labour. Blessed is he who preserves the foundations of his fathers from the beginning. Cursed is he who breaks the performances of his fathers. Blessed is he who establishes peace and love. Cursed is he who troubles those who are at peace. Blessed is he who does not speak peace with his tongue, but in his heart there is peace to all! Cursed is he who speaks peace with his tongue, but in his heart there is no peace. For all these things, in measures and in books, will be revealed in the day of the great judgement.

---

## Chapter 53

And now my children, do not say; Our father stands before God and prays for us against sin. For there is no person there, to help any man to sin. You see how I have written down, all the works of every man before his creation; which is done in the case of all men forever. No man can say, or unsay, what I have written with my hand. For God sees all things, even the thoughts of wicked men, which lie in the store places of the heart. And now my children, pay attention to all the words of your father which I say to you, that you may not grieve afterwards, and say: Our father for some cause, or other, never told them to us, in the time of this folly.

---

## Chapter 54

Let these books which I have given you be the inheritance of your peace: do not conceal them but tell them to all desiring them, and admonish them that they may know the works of the Lord, which are very wonderful.

---

## Chapter 55

My children, the appointed day and time have drawn near, and constrain me to depart. The angels will come and stand before me on the earth, awaiting what has been ordered them. In the morning I shall go to the highest heavens to my eternal habitation. Therefore I tell you, do all that is good before the face of the Lord.

---

## Chapter 56

Methuselah answered his father Enoch, said: If it is good in your eyes my father, let me put food before your face, then having blessed our houses and your sons and all your family, let your people be glorified by you; then afterwards you will depart as said. Enoch answered his son Methuselah, and said: Hear my child, since God has anointed me with the oil of his glory, there has been no food in me, and my soul remembers nothing of earthly pleasure, nor do I desire anything earthly.

---

## Chapter 57

But call all your brothers and all your families, and the elders of the people, that I may speak to them and depart as is appointed for me. Methuselah hastened and called his brethren, Regim, Riman , Ukhan, Khermion, Gaydal, and the elders of the people, and brought them all before the face of his father Enoch. Having blessed them, he spake to them.

---

## Chapter 58

Listen to me my sons. In those days when the Lord came upon the earth for the sake of Adam, and visited all his creation which He Himself had made The Lord called all the cattle of the earth, all creeping things, all the fowls that fly in the air; and brought them all before the face of our father Adam; and he gave names to all living things on the earth. The Lord made him lord over all, put all things under his hands, subdued to submission, and to all obedience to man. So the Lord 61 created man as master over all His possessions. The Lord will not judge any soul of beast, on account of man, but he will judge the soul of man, on account of the souls of beasts in the world to come. For as there is a special place for mankind, for all the souls of men according to their number, so there is also for beasts. Not one soul shall perish which God has made until the great judgement. Every soul of beast shall bring a charge against man, if he feeds them badly.

---

## Chapter 59

He who acts lawlessly regard to the souls of beasts, acts lawlessly regard to his own soul. For a man offers clean animals, and makes his sacrifice, that he may preserve his soul. If he offer, as a sacrifice, from clean beasts and birds, he preserves his soul. Everything that is given you for food, bind by the four feet: that is an atonement, he acts righteously and preserves his soul. But he who kills a beast without a wound, kills his own soul and sins against his own flesh. If any one does an injury secretly to an animal; it is an evil custom, he sins against his soul.

---

## Chapter 60

If he does an injury to the soul of man, he does an injury to his own soul; there is no salvation for his flesh, nor forgiveness forever. He who kills the soul of a man, kills his own soul and destroys his own body; there is no salvation for him forever. He who prepares a net for another man, will fall into it himself. There is no salvation for him forever who prepares a weapon against a man, he shall not escape punishment in the great judgement forever. If a man acts crookedly, or speaks evil, against any soul, he shall have no righteousness for himself forever.
