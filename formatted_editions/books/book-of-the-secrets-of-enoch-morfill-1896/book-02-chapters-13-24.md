## Chapter 13

These men brought me to the East and showed me the gates, by which the sun goes forth at the appointed seasons, and according to the revolution of the months of the whole year, and according to the number of the hours, day and night, and I saw the six great gates open, each gate having sixty-one stadia, and a quarter of one stadium. I truly measured them and understood their size to be so much, by which the sun goes forth; and he goes to the west and makes his course correspond. He proceeds through all the months. by the first gates he goes out forty-two days; by the second gates - thirty-five days; by the fourth gates - thirty-five; by the fifth gates - thirtyfive; by the sixth gates - forty-five. So he returns from the sixth gates in the course of time:, and he enters by the fifth gates during, thirty-five days, by the fourth gates - thirty-five, by the third gates during, thirty-five days; by the second gates - thirty-five. So the days of the whole year are finished according to the alternation of the four seasons. 35

## Chapter 14

And then these men took me to the West of the heavens, and showed me six great gates open, corresponding to the Eastern gates, opposite to which the sun goes out by the Eastern gates, according to the number of the days - three hundred and sixty-five - and the quarter of a day. So he sets by the Western gates. When he goes out by the Western gates, four hundred angels take his crown and bring it to the Lord. The sun revolves in his chariot and goes without light for seven complete hours in the night. When he comes near the East at the eighth hour of the night, the four hundred angels bring his crown and crown him.

## Chapter 15

Then sang the creatures called the Phoenixes and the Shalkidri. On this account every bird claps its wings, rejoicing at the giver of light, and they sang a song at the command of the Lord. The Giver of light comes to give his brightness to the whole world. They showed me the calculation of the going of the sun. The gates by which he enters and goes out are great gates, which God made for the computation of the year. On this account the sun is great.

## Chapter 16

The other, the computation of the moon, these men showed me ; all the goings and revolutions. They pointed out the gates to me, twelve great gates extending from the West to the East, by which the moon enters, and goes out at the customary times. She enters the first gate when the sun is in the West thirty-one days exactly; by the second gate thirty-one days exactly; by the third gate thirty days exactly; by the fourth gate thirty days exactly; by the fifth gate thirty-one days exactly; by the sixth gate thirty-one days exactly; by the seventh gate thirty days exactly; by the eighth gate thirty-one days exactly; by the ninth gate thirty-one days exactly; by the tenth gate thirty exactly; by the eleventh gate thirty-one days exactly; by the twelfth gate twenty-eight days exactly. And so by the Western gates in her revolutions, and corresponding to the number of the Eastern gates she goes, and accomplishes the year. Unto the sun there axe three hundred, and sixty-five days, and a quarter of one day But in the lunar year there are three hundred, and fifty-four days, making twelve months of twenty nine days, and there remain eleven days over, which belong to the solar circle of the whole year, and are lunar epacts of the whole year. So the great circle has five hundred, and thirty-two years. The fourth part is neglected during three years, and the fourth year completes it exactly. On account of this they are omitted from the heavens during three years, and are not added to the number of the days. On which account these change the seasons of the year in two new months, to make the number complete, and there are two others to diminish. When she has gone through the Western gates, she returns, and goes to the Eastern, with her light, and so she goes day, and night in the heavenly circles, below all the circles more quickly than the winds of the heavens, and there are spirits, and creatures, and angels flying, with six wings to each of the angels. Seven are computed to the circle of the moon during a revolution of nineteen years.

Enoch 73. After this law I saw another law dealing with the smaller luminary, which is called the moon. Her circumference is like the circumference of the heaven, and her chariot in which she rides is driven by the wind, and light is given to her in measure. Her rising, and setting changes every month: her days are like the days of the sun, and when her light is full it amounts to the seventh part of the light of the sun. Thus she rises. And her first phase in the east comes forth on the thirtieth morning: on that day she becomes visible, and constitutes for you the first phase of the moon on the thirtieth day together with the sun in the portal where the sun rises. the one half of her projects by a seventh part, and her whole circumference is empty, without light, with the exception of one seventh part of her, and the fourteenth part of the half of her light. When she receives one seventh part of the half of her light, her light amounts to one seventh part, and the half thereof. 36 She sets with the sun, and when the sun rises the moon rises with him, and receives the half of one part of light, and in that night in the beginning of her morning , in the beginning of her day, sets with the sun, and is invisible that night with the entire fourteen parts, and the half of one of them. She rises on that day with exactly a seventh part, and comes forth, and recedes from the rising of the sun, and in her remaining days she lightens up the thirteen parts.

Enoch 74. I saw another course, and the law prescribed to her as she performed her monthly revolution according to that law. Uriel, the holy angel who is the leader of them all, showed everything to me, and I wrote down their positions as he showed them to me, and I wrote down their months as they were, and the appearance of their lights until fifteen days are expired. In single seventh parts she waxes until her light is completed in the east, and wanes in single seventh parts until her darkness is entirely completed in the west. In certain months she alters her settings, and in certain months she pursues her own peculiar course. in two the moon sets with the sun, in those two middle portals the third, and the fourth. For seven days she goes forth, and turns about, and returns again through the portal where the sun rises; and in that portal her light is full, and she recedes from the sun, and in eight days enters the sixth portal from which the sun goes forth. When the sun goes forth from the fourth portal she goes forth seven days, so that she goes forth from the fifth, and turns back again in seven days into the fourth portal, and her light becomes full, and she recedes, and enters into the first portal in eight days. She returns again in seven days into the fourth portal from which the sun goes forth. Thus I saw their positions, the sun rising, and setting according to the order of their months. In those days the sun has an over plus of thirty days in five years taken together, and all the days which belong to one of those five full years amount to three hundred, and sixty four days. The overplus of the sun, and of the stars amounts to six days: in five years, six days every year come to thirty days; and the moon falls behind the sun, and stars to the number of thirty days. The moon brings in all the years exactly, so that their position is not prematurely advanced or delayed by a single day unto eternity; but complete the changing years with perfect justice in three hundred, and sixty four days. In three years there are one thousand, and ninety two days, and in five years eighteen hundred, and twenty days, so that in eight years there are two thousand nine hundred, and twelve days. In three years there accrue to the moon herself one thousand, and sixty two days, and in five years she falls fifty days behind. in five years there are seventeen hundred, and seventy days, so that the days in eight lunar years amount to two thousand eight hundred, and thirty two. Thus in eight years she falls behind to the amount of eighty days, and the sum total of the days she falls behind in eight years is eighty. The year is accurately completed in conformity with their stations, and the stations of the sun, as the sun, and moon rise from the portals through which the sun rises, and sets thirty days.

Enoch 77. The first quarter is called the east, because it is the first:, and the second, the south, because the Most High descends there, and there in quite a special sense He who is blessed for ever comes down. The west is named the waning quarter, because there all the luminaries of the heaven wane, and go down. The fourth quarter, called the north, is divided into three parts: the first of them is for the dwelling of men: the second for the seas of water, with the valleys, and forests, and rivers, and darkness, and clouds; and the third part with the garden of righteousness. I saw seven high mountains, higher than all the mountains which are on the earth: thence comes forth hoar frost, and days, seasons, and years pass away, and vanish. I saw seven rivers on the earth larger than all the others: one of them coming from the west pours its waters into the Great Sea. Two of them come from the north to the sea, and pour their waters into the Eritrean Sea in the east. The remaining four come forth on the side of the north to their own sea, to the Eritrean Sea, and two flow into the Great Sea there. Seven great islands I saw in the sea, and on the mainland: two on the mainland, and five in the Great Sea.

Enoch 78. The names of the sun are the following: the first Orjares, the second Tomas. The moon has four names: the first Asonja, the second Ebla, the third Benase, and the fourth Erae. These are the two large luminaries: their circumference is like the circumference of the heaven, and in size they are both alike. In the circumference of the sun there is a seventh portion of light wherewith additions are made to the moon, and definite measures are transferred until the seventh portion of the sun is exhausted. They set, and enter the portals of the west, and make their revolution by the north, and come forth through the eastern portals on the face of the heaven. When the moon rises she is seen in the heaven with the fourteenth part of the light; and in fourteen days she becomes full moon. Also fifteen parts of light are added to her so that on the fifteenth day her light is full, according to the sign of the year, and there arise fifteen parts, and the moon originates in the addition of fourteenth parts. In her waning the moon decreases on the first day to fourteen parts of her light; on the second to thirteen; on the third to twelve; on the fourth to eleven; on the fifth to ten; on the sixth to nine; on the seventh to eight; on the eighth to seven; on the ninth to six; on the tenth to five; on the eleventh to four; on the twelfth to three; on the thirteenth to two; on the fourteenth to the half of a seventh of all her light; and all her remaining disappears on the fifteenth. On the side whence the light of the moon comes forth, there again she wanes until all her light vanishes, and the days of the month are at an end, and her circumference is empty, void of light. Three months she makes of thirty days at her appointed time, and three months she makes of twenty nine days each, in which she accomplishes her waning in the first period of time, and in the first portal in one hundred, and seventy seven days. In the time of her going out she appears for three months: thirty days each, and she appears for three months: twenty nine each. At night she appears like a man for twenty days each time, and by day like the heaven, for there is nothing whatever in her save her light.

Enoch 79. Now my son, I have shown you everything, and the law of all the stars of the heaven is completed. He showed me all their laws for every day, for every season of bearing rule, for every year, and for its going forth, and for the law prescribed in every month, and every week: the waning of the moon which takes place in the sixth portal: mean, in this sixth portal her light comes to an end, and after that there is the beginning of the month: the waning which takes place in the first portal, in its season until one hundred, and seventy seven days have elapsed: reckoned according to weeks, twenty five weeks, and two days. She falls behind the sun, and in accordance with the order of the stars exactly five days in the course of one period, and when this place which you saw has been traversed. This is the picture, and sketch of every luminary, as they were shown to me by their leader, the great angel Uriel.

Enoch 80. In those days the angel Uriel answered, and said to me: —Behold I have shown you everything, Enoch, and I have revealed everything to you that you should see this sun, and this moon, and the leaders of the stars of the heaven, and all those who turn them, their tasks, and times, and departures. In the days of the sinners the years will be shortened, and their seed will be tardy on their lands, and fields, and all things on the earth will alter, and not appear in their season: the rain will be kept back, and the heaven will withhold it. In those times the fruits of the earth will be backward, and not grow in their season, and the fruits of the trees will be withheld in their season. The moon will alter her order, and not appear at her time. In those days there will be seen in the heaven a great unfruitfulness coming on the outermost chariot to the west, and she will shine more brightly than accords with order of light. Many chiefs of the superior stars will err, and these will alter their orbits, and tasks, and will not appear at the seasons prescribed to them. The whole order of the stars will be concealed from the sinners, and the thoughts of those who dwell on the earth will err concerning them, and they will be estranged from all their ways, and will err, and take them to be gods. Evil will be multiplied upon them, and punishment will come upon them to destroy everything.

Enoch 81. He said unto me: —O Enoch, observe the writing of the heavenly tablets, and read what is written thereon, and mark every individual fact. I observed everything on the heavenly tablets, and read everything which was written, and understood everything, and read the book of all the deeds of men, and of all the children of flesh that will be 40 upon the earth to the remotest generations. Forthwith after that I blessed the Lord, the King of the glory of the world, in that He has made all the works of the world, and I extolled the Lord because of His patience, and blessed Him because of the children of men. After that I spoke: Blessed is the man who dies in righteousness, and goodness, concerning whom there is no book of unrighteousness written, and no day of judgment is found. Those seven holy ones brought me, and placed me on the earth before the door of my house, and spoke unto me: —Declare everything to your son Methuselah, and show to all your children that no flesh is righteous in the sight of the Lord, for He is their Creator. One year we will leave you with your children, until again a command that you may teach your children, and record for them, and testify to them to all your children; and in the second year they will withdraw you from their midst. But the sinners will die with the sinners, and the apostate go down with the apostate. Those also who practise righteousness will die on account of the deeds of men, and be gathered together on account of the doings of the godless. In those days they ceased to speak to me, and I came to my people, blessing the Lord of the world.

## Chapter 18

The men took and brought me up into the fifth heaven, and I saw there, many hosts not to be counted called Grigori; and their appearance was like men, and their size was greater than that of the giants. Their countenances were dried and their lips are always silent. There was no service in the fifth heaven. I said to the men who were with me: Why are these men very dried, their faces melancholy, their lips silent, and there is no service in this heaven? And they said to me: —These are the Grigori, who, with their prince Satanail, rejected the holy Lord. In consequence of these things they are kept in great darkness in the second heaven; and of them there went three to the 16 earth from the throne of God to the place Ermon. They entered into dealings on the side of Mount Ermon, and they saw the daughters of men, that they were fair, and took unto themselves wives. They made the earth foul with their deeds. They acted lawlessly in all times of this age, and wrought confusion, and the giants were born, and the strangely tall men, and there was much wickedness. On account of this God judged them with a mighty judgement. They lament for their brethren and they will be punished at the great day of the Lord. I said to the Grigori: I have seen your brethren, and their works, and their great torments. I have prayed for them, but God has condemned them under the earth until the heaven, and earth are ended for ever. I said: Why do you wait, brethren, and not serve before the face of the Lord? Perform your duties before the face of the Lord, and do not anger your Lord to the end. They listened to my rebuke. They stood in the four orders in this heaven, and lo! As I was standing with these men, four trumpets resounded together with a loud voice, and the Grigori sang with one voice, and their voices went forth before the Lord with sadness and tenderness.

## Chapter 19

And these men took me thence, and brought me to the sixth heaven. I saw there seven bands of angels, very bright, and glorious, and their faces shining more than the rays of the sun. They are resplendent, and there is no difference in their countenances, or their manner, or the style of their clothing. These orders, arrange and study the revolutions of the stars, and the changes of the moon, and revolutions of the sun, and superintend the good or evil condition of the world. They arrange teachings and instructions, and sweet speaking, and singing, and all kinds of glorious praise. These are the archangels who are appointed over the angels! They hold in subjection all living things both in heaven and earth. There are the angels who are over seasons and years, and the angels who are over rivers and the sea, and those who are over the fruits of the earth, and the angels over every herb, giving all kinds of nourishment to every living thing. The angels over all souls of men, who write down all their works and their lives before the face of the Lord. In the midst of them are seven phoenixes, and seven Cherubims, and seven six-winged creatures, being as one voice, 9 and singing with one voice; and it is not possible to describe their singing, and they rejoice before the Lord at His footstool.

## Chapter 20

And these men took me thence and brought me to the seventh heaven, and I saw there, a very great light and the fiery hosts of great archangels, and incorporeal powers, and lordships, and principalities, and powers; cherubim, seraphim, thrones, and the watchfulness of many eyes. There were ten troops, a station of brightness, and I was afraid and trembled with a great terror. Those men took hold of me and brought me into their midst, and said to me: —Be of good cheer, Enoch, be not afraid. They showed me the Lord from afar sitting on His lofty throne and all the heavenly hosts having approached stood on the ten steps, according to their rank, and made obeisance to the Lord. So they proceeded to their places in joy, and mirth, in boundless light singing songs with low and gentle voices, gloriously serving Him.

## Chapter 21

They leave not nor depart day or night standing before the face of the Lord, working His will; Cherubim and seraphim, standing round His throne. The six-winged creatures overshadow all His throne, singing with a soft voice before the face of the Lord: —Holy, Holy, Holy, Lord God of Sabaoth! Heaven and earth are full of your glory! When I had seen all these things, these men said unto me: —Enoch, up to this time we have been ordered to accompany you! Those men departed from me and I saw them no more. I remained alone at the extremity of the heaven and was afraid, fell on my face and said within myself: Woe is me, what has come upon me! The Lord sent one of His glorious archangels, Gabriel, and he said to me: —Be of good cheer Enoch, be not afraid, stand up, come with me and stand up before the face of the Lord for ever. I answered him and said: Oh Lord, my spirit has departed from me with fear and trembling. Call to me the men who have brought me to this place: upon them I have relied, and with them I would go before the face of the Lord. Gabriel hurried me away like a leaf carried off by the wind, and he took me and set me before the face of the Lord.

The second voice I heard blessing the Elect One; the elect ones who cleave to the Lord of Spirits. Enoch 41. After that I saw all the secrets of the heavens: how the kingdom is divided, how the actions of men are weighed upon the balance. There saw I the mansions of the elect, and the mansions of the holy, and mine eyes saw there all the sinners being driven from thence which had denied the name of the Lord of Spirits, and being dragged off:, they could not abide there because of the punishment which proceeds from the Lord of Spirits. There mine eyes saw the secrets of the lightning and of the thunder, and the secrets of the winds, how they are divided to blow over the earth, and the secrets of the clouds and dew:, there I saw from whence they proceed in that place, and from whence they saturate the dust of the earth. There I saw closed chambers out of which the winds are apportioned, and the chamber of the hail and winds, and the chamber of the mist and the cloud thereof hovers over the earth from before eternity. I saw the chambers of the sun and moon whence they proceed, where they come again, their glorious return, how one is superior to the other, and their stately orbit, how they do not leave their orbit, and they add nothing to their orbit, and they take nothing from it, and they keep faith with each other, abiding by the oath. First the sun goes forth, and traverses his path according to the commandment of the Lord of Spirits and mighty is His name for ever and ever. After that comes the hidden and the visible path of the moon traversing the orbit of her path in that place, by day and by night, the one holding a position opposite to the other before the Lord of Spirits. And they give thanks and praise, and rest not; for to them their thanksgiving is rest. For the sun changes oft for a blessing or a curse, and the course of the path of the moon is light to the righteous and darkness to the sinners in the name of the Lord, who made a separation between the light and the darkness, and divided the spirits of men, and strengthens the spirits of the righteous in the name of His righteousness. For neither angel nor power is able to prevent; for He appoints a judge for them all and he judges them all before Him.

Enoch 42. Wisdom found no place where she might dwell; then a dwelling place was assigned her in the heavens. Wisdom came to make her dwelling among the children of men, and found no dwelling place: then Wisdom returned to her place, and took her seat among the angels. Unrighteousness came forth from her chambers and she found those whom she sought not, and dwelt with them as rain in a desert, and dew on a thirsty land.

Enoch 43. Again I saw lightning and the stars of heaven; I saw how He called them all by their names, and they heard Him. I saw how they are weighed in a righteous balance according to their proportions of light, the width of their spaces, and the day of their appearing, and how their revolution produces lightning:, and their revolution according to the number of the angels, and how they keep faith with each other. I asked the angel who went with me and showed me what was hidden: What are these? He said to me: —the Lord of Spirits has showed you their parable: these are the names of the holy who dwell on the earth and believe in the name of the Lord of Spirits for ever and ever.

Second Parable.

Enoch 45. This is the second parable concerning those who deny the name of the dwelling of the holy ones, and the Lord of Spirits. They will not ascend into the heaven, and on the earth they will not come: such will be the lot of the sinners who deny the name of the Lord of Spirits, who are so preserved for the day of suffering and tribulation. On that day, Mine Elect One will sit on the throne of glory, and make choice amongst their deeds; their mansions will be innumerable and their souls will grow strong within them when they see Mine elect ones, and those who have called upon My glorious name. On that day, I will cause Mine Elect One to dwell among them and I will transform the heaven, and make it an eternal blessing and light. I will transform the earth and make it a blessing, and cause Mine elect ones to dwell upon it: but the sinners, and evil doers, will not set foot thereon. For I have seen, and satisfied with peace My righteous ones, and have caused them to dwell before Me: but for the sinners there is judgment impending with Me, so that I may destroy them from the face of the earth.

Enoch 46. There I saw One who had a head of days, and His head was white like wool. With Him was another being whose countenance had the appearance of a man, and his face was full of graciousness like one of the holy angels. I asked the angel who went with me and showed me all the hidden things, concerning that Son of Man: who he was, whence he was, and why he went with the Head of Days? He answered, and said unto me: —This is the Son of Man who has righteousness, with whom dwells righteousness, and who reveals all the treasures of that which is hidden. The Lord of Spirits has chosen him because his lot before the Lord of Spirits has surpassed everything in uprightness for ever this Son of Man whom you has seen will arouse the kings, and the mighty ones from their couches, and the strong from their thrones, and will loosen the reins of the strong, and grind to powder the teeth of the sinners. The thunders according to the places where they fall, and all the divisions which are made among the lightnings that it may lighten and that their hosts may at once obey. Judgment of the Watchers.

He will imprison those angels who have shown unrighteousness in that burning valley which my grandfather Enoch, had formerly shown to me in the west, among the mountains of gold, and silver, iron, soft metal, and tin. I saw that valley in which there was a great convulsion, and a swelling of the waters. When all this took place, there was produced from that fiery molten metal, and from the convulsion wherewith they were convulsed in that place, a smell of sulphur; it was connected with those waters. And that valley of the angels who had seduced, burned continually under the earth there. Through the valleys of that land proceed streams of fire, where those angels are punished who had led astray those who dwell upon the earth. But in those days these waters will serve for the kings, the mighty, the exalted, and those who dwell on the earth, for the healing of the body, and for the punishment of the spirit, because their spirit is full of lust, that they may be punished in their body; for they have denied the Lord of Spirits, see their punishment daily and yet believe not in His name. In proportion as the burning of their bodies becomes severe, a corresponding change will take place in their spirit for ever and ever; for before the Lord of Spirits there will be none to utter a lying word. For the judgment will come upon them because they believe in the lust of their body, and have denied the Spirit of the Lord. Those same waters will undergo a change in those days; for when those angels are punished in these waters, these water springs will change their temperature, and when the angels ascend, this 27 water of the springs will change and become cold. I heard Michael answering and saying: —This judgment wherewith the angels are judged is a testimony for the kings, the mighty, and for those who possess the earth. Because these waters of judgment minister to the healing of the body of the kings, and to the lust of their body; therefore they will not see and will not believe that those waters will change, and become a fire which burns for ever.

Enoch 68. After that my grandfather Enoch, gave the signs of all the secrets in a book, and the parables which had been given to him; and he put them together for me in the words of the book of the parables. In those days Michael answered Rufael, and said: —The power of the spirit transports and provokes me: yet as regards the rigour of the judgment of the secrets, the judgment over the angels; who can endure the rigorous judgment which is passed, before which they melt away? Michael answered again, and spoke to Rufael: — Who is he whose heart is not softened concerning it, and whose reins are not troubled by this word of judgment that has been passed upon them, upon those whom they have so led out? it came to pass when he stood before the Lord of Spirits, Michael spoke so to Rufael: —I will not take their part under the eye of the Lord, for the Lord of Spirits is angry with them because they do as if they were like the Lord. Therefore all that is hidden will come upon them for ever and ever; for neither angel nor man will have his portion, but alone they undergo their judgment for ever and ever.

Enoch 69. After this judgment they will inspire fear and anger in them, because they have shown this to those who dwell on the earth. behold the names of those angels. These are their names: the first of them is Semjaza, the second Arestiqifa, the third Armen, the fourth Kokabael, the fifth Turel, the sixth Rimjal, the seventh Danel, the eighth Nuqael, the ninth Baraqel; the tenth Azazel, the eleventh Armers; the twelfth Batarjal, the thirteenth Basasael, the fourteenth AnaneL, the fifteenth Turjal, the sixteenth Simapisiel, the seventeenth Jetarel, the eighteenth Tumael, the nineteenth Tarel, the twentieth Rumael, the twenty first Izezeel. These are the chiefs of their angels, and the names of their chief ones over a hundred, over fifty, and over ten. The name of the first, Jequn: that is the one who led astray all the children of the angels, brought them down to the earth, and led them astray through the daughters of men. The second is called Asbeel: he imparted evil counsel to the children of the holy angels, and led them astray, so that they defiled their bodies with the daughters of men. The third is called Gadreel: it is he who has taught the children of men all the blows of death; he led Eve astray and showed to the sons of men the weapons of death, the coat of mail, the shield, the sword for battle, and all the weapons of death to the children of men. From his hand they have proceeded over those who dwell on the earth, from that hour for evermore. The fourth is called Penemue: he taught the children of men the bitter and the sweet, and taught them all the secrets of their wisdom. He instructed mankind in writing with ink and paper, and thereby many sinned from eternity to eternity and until this day. For when man was created to the end, it was not intended that he should give confirmation to his good faith with pen and ink in such wise. For man was created exactly like the angels, to the intent that he should continue righteous and pure; and death which destroys everything could not have taken hold of him; but through this, their knowledge, they are perishing, and through this power, it is consuming me. The fifth is called Kasdeja: he has taught the children of men all the wicked attacks of spirits and demons, the attacks of the embryo and the babe, that it may pass away; and the attacks of the soul, the bites of the serpent, the attacks which befall at noon, the son of 28 the serpent named Tabat. This is the number of Kesbeel who showed the head of the oath to the holy ones when he dwelt high above in glory; its name is Beqa.

He requested Michael to show this hidden name that they might mention it in the oath, so that those who revealed all that was hidden to the children of men might quake before that name and oath. This is the power of that oath, for it is powerful and strong; and He placed this oath Akae in the hand of Michael. These are the secrets of this oath: the heaven was made strong through the oath, and was suspended before the world was created and for ever: through it, the earth was founded upon the water, and from the secret recesses of the mountains come beautiful waters from the creation of the world unto eternity. Through that oath, the sea was created, and as its foundation He laid for it the sand against the time of anger and it dare not pass beyond it, from the creation of the world unto eternity. Through that oath, the depths are made fast, and abide, and stir not from their place, from eternity to eternity. Through that oath, the sun and moon complete their course and deviate not from the path prescribed to them, from eternity to eternity. Through that oath, the stars complete their course, and He calls them by their names, and they answer Him from eternity to eternity. In like manner, the spirits of the water, and of the winds, of all zephyrs, and the paths of all the bands of the spirits. In it are preserved the voices of the thunder and the light of the lightnings; in it are preserved the chambers of the hail, and of the hoar frost, the chambers of the mist, the chambers of the rain, and the dew. All these believe and give thanks before the Lord of Spirits, and glorify with all their power; and their food is nothing save thanksgiving: they thank, glorify, and extol, the name of the Lord of Spirits, for ever and ever. This oath is mighty over them, and through it they are preserved; their paths are preserved and their course is not destroyed. There was great joy amongst them, and they blessed, glorified, and extolled, because the name of the Son of Man was revealed unto them: he sat on the throne of his glory and the sum of judgment was committed unto him, the Son of Man; he caused the sinners and those who have led the world astray to pass away and be destroyed from off the face of the earth. With chains they shall be bound, and in their assemblage, place of destruction shall they be imprisoned and all their works vanish from the face of the earth. From henceforth, there will be nothing that is corruptible; for the Son of Man has appeared and sits on the throne of his glory; all evil will pass away before his face and depart. The word of the Son of Man will be strong before the Lord of Spirits. This is the third parable of Enoch.

Enoch 70. It came to pass after this that his name was carried aloft during his lifetime to the Son of Man and to the Lord of Spirits from amongst those who dwell on the earth. He was carried aloft on the chariots of the spirit, and the name vanished amongst them. From that day, I was no longer numbered amongst them; he set me between the two winds, between the North and the West, where the angels took the cords to measure for me the place for the elect and righteous. There I saw the first fathers and the righteous who from the beginning dwell in that place.

Enoch 71. After this it came to pass that my spirit was hidden, and it ascended into the heavens: I saw the sons of the holy angels stepping on flames of fire: their garments were white, and their raiment and their faces shone like snow. I saw two streams of fire and the light of that fire shone like hyacinth; I fell on my face before the Lord of Spirits. The angel Michael, one of the archangels, seized me by my right hand and lifted me up, and introduced me to all the secrets of mercy, and the secrets of righteousness. He showed me all the secrets of the ends of the heaven, all the chambers of all the stars and of the 29 luminaries, whence they proceed into the presence of the holy ones. The spirit translated Enoch, unto the heaven of heavens; there I saw in the midst of that light, a structure built of crystals, and between those crystals flames of living fire. My spirit saw how a fire girt that house around, on its four sides, streams full of living fire, and how they encircled that house. Round about were Seraphim, Cherubim, and Ophanim: these are they who sleep not and guard the throne of His glory. I saw angels who could not be counted; a thousand thousands and ten thousand times ten thousand, encircled that house, and Michael, Gabriel, Rufael, Fanuel, and the holy angels who are above in the heavens, go in and out of that house. There came forth from that house Michael, Gabriel, Rufael, Fanuel, and many holy angels without number. With them, the Head of Days: His head white and pure as wool, His raiment indescribable. I fell on my face and my whole body melted away, but my spirit was transfigured; I cried with a loud voice, with the spirit of power, and blessed, glorified, and extolled. These blessings which went forth out of my mouth were well pleasing before that Head of Days. That Head of Days came with Michael, Gabriel, Rufael, Fanuel, with thousands and ten thousand thousands angels without number. He came to me and greeted me with his voice, and said unto me: — You are the son of man who are born unto righteousness, and righteousness abides over you: the righteousness of the Head of Days forsakes you not. He said unto me: —He calls unto you peace in the name of the world to come; for from thence proceeds peace since the creation of the world, and so will it be with you for ever and ever, and ever. All who in the time to come walk in your ways, you whom righteousness never forsakes, their dwelling places will be with you and their heritage will be with you; they will not be separated from you for ever and ever, and ever. So there will be length of days with that Son of Man; the righteous will have peace, and to the righteous his path of uprightness in the name of the Lord of Spirits, for ever and ever.

## Chapter 22

I fell down and worshipped the Lord. The Lord spake to me with His lips: —Be of good cheer Enoch, be not afraid: rise up and stand before my face for ever. Michael, the chief captain, lifted me up and brought me before the face of the Lord, and the Lord said to His servants, making trial of them: —Let Enoch, come to stand before My face for ever! And the glorious ones made obeisance to the Lord and said: —Enoch, proceed according to your word! And the Lord said to Michael: —Go, take from Enoch, his earthly robe, anoint him with My holy oil, clothe him with the raiment of My glory. So Michael did as the Lord spake unto him. He anointed me and clothed me, and the appearance of that oil was more than a great light and its anointing was like excellent dew; its fragrance like myrrh, shining like a ray of the sun. I gazed upon myself and I was like one of His glorious ones, there was no difference, and fear and trembling departed from me. The Lord called one of His archangels by name Vretil, who was more wise than the other archangels and wrote down all the doings of the Lord. The Lord said to Vretil: —Bring forth the books from my store-places, give a reed to Enoch, and interpret to him the books. Vretil made haste and brought me the books, fragrant with myrrh, and gave me a reed from his hand.

## Chapter 23

And he told me all the works of the heaven, the earth, and the sea; their goings and comings, the noise of the thunder; the sun, and moon, the movement of the stars; their changings; the seasons, and years, days and hours; the goings of the winds; the numbers of the angels; the songs of the armed hosts; everything relating to man, every language of their songs, the lives of men, the precepts and instructions, the sweet-voiced singings, and all which it is suitable to be instructed in. Vretil instructed me thirty days and thirty nights, his lips never ceased speaking, and I did not cease thirty days and thirty nights writing all the remarks. Vretil said to me: —All the things which I have told you, you has written down. Sit down and write all about the souls of men, those of them which are not born, and the places prepared for them for ever; for every soul was created eternally before the foundation of the world. I wrote all out continuously during thirty days and thirty nights, and I copied all out accurately. I wrote 366 books.

## Chapter 24

And the Lord called me and said to me: —Enoch, sit on My left hand with Gabriel. I made obeisance to the Lord. The Lord spake to me: —Enoch, the things which you see at rest and in motion were completed by me. Not even to My angels have I told My secrets, nor have I informed them of their origin, nor have they understood My infinite creation which I tell you of to-day. For before anything which is visible existed, I alone held my course among the invisible things, like the sun from the east to the west, and from the west to the east. But even the sun has rest in himself, I did not find rest, because I was creating every thing. I planned to lay the foundations and to make the visible creation.
