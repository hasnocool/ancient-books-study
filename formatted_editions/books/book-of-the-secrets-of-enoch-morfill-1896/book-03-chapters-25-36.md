## Chapter 25

I commanded in the depths that visible things should come out of invisible. Adoil came out very great and I gazed upon him. Lo! His colour was red, of great brightness. I said unto him: —Burst asunder, Adoil, and let that which come from you be visible. He burst asunder and there came forth a great light, and I was in the midst of a great light, and as the light came forth from the light, there came forth the great world, revealing all the creation, which I had purposed to make; and I saw that it was good. I made for Myself a throne and sat upon it; and I said to the light: —Go forth on high, and be established above My throne, and be the foundation for things on high. There was nothing higher than the light; and as I reclined, I saw it from My throne.

## Chapter 26

I summoned a second time from the depths, and said: —Let the solid thing which is visible come forth from the invisible. Arkhas came forth firm, heavy, and very red. I said: —Be you divided, O Arkhas, and let that which is produced from you be seen. When he was divided, the world came forth, very dark and great, bringing the creation of all things below. I saw that it was good. I said to him: —Go you down and be you established. be a foundation for things below; and it was so. It came forth and was established, and was a foundation for things below. There was nothing else below the darkness.

---

## Chapter 27

I ordered that there should be a separation between the light and the darkness, and I said: —Let there be a thick substance; and it was so. I spread this out and there was water; I spread it over the darkness, below the light. So I made firm the waters, that is the depths, and I surrounded the waters with light, and I created seven circles and I fashioned them like crystal, moist, and dry, that is to say like glass and ice; and as for the waters and also the other elements, I showed each of them their paths; to the seven stars, each of them in their heaven, how they should go. I saw that it was good. I separated between the light and the darkness, that is to say between the waters here, and there. I said to the light: —Let it be day; and to the darkness, Let it be night. The evening and the morning were the first day.

---

## Chapter 28

And so I made firm the circles of the heavens and caused the waters below, which are under the heavens, to be gathered into one place, that the waves should be dried up, and it was so. Out of the waves I made firm and great stones, and out of the stones I heaped together a dry substance and I called the dry substance earth. In the midst of the earth I appointed a pit, that is to say an abyss. I gathered the sea into one place and I restrained it with a yoke. I said to the sea: —Lo I give you an eternal portion; you shall not move from your established position. So I made fast the firmament and fixed it above the water. This I called, the first day of the creation.

---

## Chapter 29

Then it was evening and again morning; it was the second day. And for all the heavenly hosts I fashioned a nature like that of fire, and My eye gazed on the very firm and hard stone. From the brightness of My eye the lightning received its wonderful nature. Fire is in the water, and water in the fire, and neither is the one quenched, nor the other dried up. On this account lightning is brighter than the sun, and soft water is stronger than hard stone. From the stone I cut the mighty fire. From the fire I made the ranks of the spiritual hosts, ten thousand angels. Their weapons are fiery and their garment is a burning flame; and I ordered them to stand each in their ranks. One of 31 these in the ranks of the Archangels, having turned away with the rank below him, entertained an impossible idea, that he should make his throne higher than the clouds over the earth, and should be equal in rank to My power. I hurled him from the heights with his angels. He was flying in the air continually above the abyss. So I created, all the heavens.

---

## Chapter 30

And it was the third day. On the third day I ordered the earth to produce great trees, such as to bear fruit, and mountains, every sort of herb, every seed that is sown, and I planted Paradise and enclosed it, and placed fiery angels armed; and so I made a renewing. Then it was evening, and it was morning, being the fourth day. On the fourth day I ordered that there should be great lights in the circles of the heavens. In the first and highest circle, I placed the star Kruno; on the second Aphrodite; on the third Eires; on the fourth the Sun; on the fifth Zeus; on the sixth ermes; on the seventh the moon. I adorned the lower air with the lesser stars. I placed the sun to give light to the day, and the moon and the stars to give light to the night; the sun that he should go according to each sign of the zodias; and the course of the moon through the twelve signs of the zodias. I fixed their names and existence, the thunders and the revolutions of the hours, how they take place. Then it was evening and the morning, the fifth day. On the fifth day I commanded the sea to produce fish and winged fowls of all kinds, all things that creep upon the earth and four-footed things that go about the earth, the things that fly in the air, male and female, and every living thing breathing with life. It was evening and morning, the sixth day. On the sixth day I ordered My Wisdom to make man of seven substances: His flesh from the earth; his blood from the dew; his eyes from the sun; his bones from the stones; his thoughts from the swiftness of the angels and the clouds; his veins and hair from the grass of the earth; his spirit from My spirit and from the wind. I gave him seven natures: hearing to his body; sight to his eyes; smell to the perception; touch to the veins; taste to the Mood; the bones for endurance; sweetness for thought. I purposed a subtle thing: from the invisible and visible nature I made man. From both are his death and life, and his form; and the word was like a deed both small in a great thing, and great in a small thing. I placed him upon the earth like a second angel, in an honourable, great, and glorious way. I made him a ruler to rule upon the earth and to have My wisdom. There was no one like him upon the earth of all My creations. I gave him a name from the four substances: the East, the West, the North and the South. I appointed for him four special stars, and I gave him the name, Adam. I gave him his will and I showed him the two ways, the light and the darkness, and I said unto him: —This is good and this is evil! That I should know whether he has love for Me, or hate, that he should appear in his race as loving Me. I knew his nature; he did not know his nature. Therefore, his ignorance is a woe to him that should sin. And I appointed death on account of his sin. I caused him to sleep and he slumbered. I took from him a rib and I made him a wife. Death came by his wife and I received his last word. I called her by a name, the mother; that is Eve.

## Chapter 31

Adam had a life on earth. I made a garden in Eden, in the East, and that he should observe the law and keep the instruction. I made for him the heavens open, that he should perceive the angels singing the song of triumph. There was light continually in Paradise without any darkness. because things were subservient to Adam on earth, the devil took thought to rule it and have lordship over it - as if wishing to make another world. The devil is to be the evil spirit of the lowest places; he became Satan after he left the heavens. His name was formerly Satanail. Then though he became different from the angels in nature, he did not change his understanding of justice but sinful thoughts. He understood the judgement upon him and the former sin which he had sinned. On account of this, he conceived designs against Adam: in such a manner he entered and deceived Eve; But he did not touch Adam. But I cursed him for ignorance: but those I previously blessed, I did not curse them, nor did I curse man, nor the earth, nor any other things created, but the evil fruit of man, and then his works.

## Chapter 32

I said to him: —Earth you are and to earth also from whence I took you shall you return. I will not destroy you, but will send you whence I took you. Then in My second coming I can also take you; I have blessed all My creation, visible and invisible. I blessed the seventh day, which is the Shabbath, for in it I rested from all My labours.

## Chapter 33

Then also I established the eighth day. Let the eighth be the first after My work, and let the days be after the fashion of seven thousand. Let there be at the beginning of the eighth thousand a time when there is no computation and no end, neither years, nor months, nor weeks, nor days, nor hours. Now Enoch, what things I have told you, what you has understood, what heavenly things you has seen, what you has seen upon the earth, and what you has written in books by My wisdom, all these things I devised so, as to create them, and I made them from the highest foundation to the lowest and to the end. There is no counsellor, nor inheritor of My works; I am the eternal One; and the One not made with hands. My thought is without change. My wisdom is My counsellor. My word is reality. And My eyes see all things. If I look to all things, they stand fast. If I turn away My face, all are in need of Me. Now pay attention Enoch, and know you who is speaking to you and do you: —Take the books which you yourself has written. I give you Samuil and Raguil who brought you to Me. Go with them upon the earth and tell your sons what things I have said to you, and what you has seen from the lowest heaven, up to My throne. For I have created all the hosts and all the powers; and there is none that opposes Me or is disobedient to Me. For all are obedient to My sole power, and labour for My rule alone. Give them the works written out by you; they shall read them and know Me to be the Creator of all;; they shall understand that there is no other God beside Me. They shall distribute the books of thy writing to their children’s children, from generation to generation, from nation to nation. I will give you Enoch, My messenger Michael the great captain, for your writings and for the writings of your fathers; Adam, Seth, Enos, Kainan, Malaleel, and Jared your father. I shall not require them until the last age, for I have instructed My two angels Aryukh and Parryukh, whom I have put upon the earth as their guardians. I have ordered them in time, to guard them, that the account of what I shall do in your family may not be lost in the deluge to come.

---

## Chapter 34

For I know the wickedness of men, that they will not bear the yoke, which I have put upon them, nor sow the seeds, which I have given them, but will cast off My yoke and accept another, and sow vain seeds, bow to vain gods, and deny Me, the only God. They will fill all the world with wickedness and iniquity, and unclean impurities with one another; sodomy and all other impure practices, which it is unclean to speak about. On this account I will bring a deluge upon the earth and I will destroy all, and the earth shall be destroyed in great corruption.

---

## Chapter 35

And I will leave a righteous man of your race, with all his house who shall act according to My will. From their seed, after some time, will be raised up a numerous generation; but of these, many will be very insatiable. Then on the extinction of that family, I will show them the books of your writings, and of your fathers; and the guardians of them on earth will show them to the men who are true and please Me, who do not take My name in vain. They shall tell to another generation; and these having read them shall be glorified at last more than before.

---

## Chapter 36

And now I Enoch, give you a period of thirty days to work in your house. Tell your sons and all your household before Me, that they may listen to what is spoken to them by you; that they read and understand how there is no other God beside Me and let them always keep My commandments; begin to read and understand the books written out by you. After thirty days, I will send My angels for you and they shall take you from the earth, and from your sons, according to My will.

Book 3 - The Luminaries

Enoch 72. The Book of the courses of the luminaries of the heaven and the relations of each, according to their classes, their dominion, their seasons, according to their names, places 33 of origin, according to their months, which the holy angel Uriel their leader who was with me showed me. He showed me all their laws exactly as they are, how it is with regard to all the years of the world, and unto eternity until the new creation is accomplished which dure until eternity. this is the first law of the luminaries: the luminary the Sun has its rising in the eastern portals of the heaven, and its setting in the western portals of the heaven. I saw six portals out of which the sun rises, and six portals in which the sun sets: the moon also rises, and sets through these portals, and the leaders of the stars, and those led by them: six in the east, and six in the west following each other in accurately corresponding order: also many windows to the right, and left of these portals. first there goes forth the great luminary, named the sun, and his circumference is like the circumference of the heaven, and he is quite filled with illuminating, and heating fire. The chariots on which he ascends are driven by the wind, and the sun disappears from the heaven as he sets, and returns through the north in order to reach the east, and is so guided that he comes to that portal, and shines in the face of the heaven. In this way he rises in the first month in the great portal, and indeed rises through the fourth of those six portals in the east. in that fourth portal through which the sun rises in the first month are twelve window openings from which proceeds a flame when they are opened in their season. When the sun rises in the heaven, he comes forth through that fourth portal thirty mornings in succession, and sets directly opposite in the fourth portal in the west of the heaven. during this period day becomes longer than day, and night shorter than night to the thirtieth morning. on that day the day is two parts longer than the night, and the day amounts exactly to ten parts, and the night to eight parts. the sun rises from that fourth portal, and sets in the fourth, and returns to the fifth portal of the east thirty mornings in succession, and rises from, and sets in the fifth portal. Then the day becomes longer by two parts, and amounts to eleven parts, and the night becomes shorter, and amounts to seven parts. The sun returns to the east, and enters into the sixth portal, and rises, and sets in the sixth portal one, and thirty mornings in succession on account of its sign. on that day the day becomes longer than the night, so that it amounts to double the night. the sun mounts up to make the day shorter, and the night longer, and the sun returns to the east, and enters into the sixth portal, and rises from it, and sets thirty mornings. when thirty mornings have elapsed, the day decreases by exactly one part, and amounts to eleven parts, and the night to seven parts. The sun goes forth from that sixth portal in the west, and goes to the east, and rises or thirty mornings in the fifth portal, and sets in the west again in the fifth western portal. On that day the day decreases by two parts, and amounts to ten parts, and the night to eight parts. the sun rises from that fifth portal, and sets in the fifth portal of the west, and rises for one, and thirty mornings in the fourth portal on account of its sign, and sets in the west. On that day the day is equalised to the night, and becomes of equal length, and the day amounts to nine parts, and the night to nine parts. The sun rises from that portal, and sets in the west, and returns to the east, and rises thirty mornings in the third portal, and sets in the west in the third portal. on that day the night becomes longer than the day, and night becomes longer than night, and day shorter than day until the thirtieth morning, and the night amounts exactly to ten parts, and the day to eight parts. 34 the sun rises from that third portal, and sets in the third portal in the west, and returns to the east, and for thirty mornings rises in the second portal of the east, and in like manner sets in the second portal in the west of the heaven. on that day the night amounts to eleven parts, and the day to seven parts. the sun rises on that day from that second portal, and sets in the west in the second portal, and returns to the east into the first portal for one, and thirty mornings, and sets in the west in the first portal. on that day the night becomes longer, and amounts to double the day: the night amounts exactly to twelve parts, and the day to six. The sun has traversed the divisions of his orbit, and turns again on that his orbit, and enters that portal thirty mornings, and sets also in the west opposite to it. on that day the night decreases in length by one part, and it amounts to eleven parts, and the day to seven parts. the sun returns, and enters into the second portal in the east, and returns on that his orbit for thirty mornings, rising, and setting. On that day the night decreases in length, and the night amounts to ten parts, and the day to eight. on that day the sun rises from that second portal, and sets in the west, and returns to the east, and rises in the third portal for one, and thirty mornings, and sets in the west of the heaven. On that day the night decreases, and amounts to nine parts, and the day to nine parts, and the night is equal to the day, and the year amounts exactly to three hundred, and sixty four days. the length of the day, and of the night, and the shortness of the day, and of the night, through the course of the sun they are separated. On that account its course by day becomes daily longer, and its course by night nightly shorter. this is the law, and the course of the sun, and his return as often as he returns sixty times, and rises, mean, the great luminary which is named the sun, for ever and ever. that which so rises is the great luminary, being so named according to its appearance, according to the command of the Lord. As he rises so he sets, and decreases not, and rests not, but runs day, and night, and his light is sevenfold brighter than that of the moon; but as regards size they are both equal.
