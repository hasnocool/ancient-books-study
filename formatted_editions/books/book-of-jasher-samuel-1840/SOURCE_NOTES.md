# Source Notes — Sefer ha-Yashar / Book of Jasher

## Historical work and translation
The work is the medieval Hebrew Sefer ha-Yashar, printed in Hebrew in 1625. The English text is the Moses Samuel translation first published in 1840 and reprinted by J. H. Parry & Company in Salt Lake City in 1887.

## Selected source
Repository OCR: `books_02/Book_of_Jasher_1887.txt`.

Reference edition page: https://ccel.org/a/anonymous/jasher/home.html

The repository OCR originates from a later photo-reprint. This formatted edition excludes the later reprint's copyright page, modern publisher details, advertising, and other contemporary matter. It retains the historical translation text and its translator's preface/contents.

## Rights and scope
The underlying 1840 translation and 1887 edition are public-domain historical works. No new 1988 publisher material is included. This is not an authentic copy of the ancient biblical Book of Jasher; it is the medieval Sefer ha-Yashar.

## Structural audit
The OCR yields 91 chapter headings. Chapter XLVII occurs before XLVI in the extracted sequence; the two complete blocks are ordered numerically in the reading edition and this intervention is recorded. Chapter LXXXIX lacks the usual terminal period in the OCR.

## Verification limits
This pass verifies chapter coverage and preserves each chapter block from the source transcription. It does not collate OCR against the 1887 facsimile, verify the underlying Hebrew, or assess the translation's historical claims.
