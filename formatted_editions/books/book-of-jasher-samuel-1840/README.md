## Editorial and historical cautions
- This Sefer ha-Yashar is a medieval Jewish work printed in Hebrew in 1625 and translated into English by Moses Samuel in 1840. It is **not** the lost ancient Book of Jasher mentioned in Joshua 10:13 and 2 Samuel 1:18.
- Chapter headings and verse numbers are editorial divisions in the English edition; they are not evidence of an ancient manuscript chapter system.
- The OCR extraction places Chapter XLVII before Chapter XLVI. This reading edition sorts the two intact chapter blocks into numeric order, XLVI then XLVII. The text within each block is retained; this order correction is documented and still needs facsimile confirmation.
- Chapter LXXXIX's heading has no trailing period in the OCR; it is retained as found.
- OCR line and column order may be corrupted. No wording is silently repaired.
