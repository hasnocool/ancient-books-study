# Vitruvius — The Ten Books on Architecture (Morgan, 1914)

**Author:** Vitruvius  
**English translator:** Morris Hicky Morgan  
**Publication:** Harvard University Press, Cambridge, 1914  
**Source:** `books_05/vitruviustenbook00vitruoft.txt` (OCR transcription of the 1914 volume)

## Scope and status

- Ten book files created from the body of the 1914 volume, Books I–X.
- The translation was unfinished by Morgan at his death; the original preface says Herbert Langford Warren completed Book X beginning with Chapter XIII and made other editorial changes.
- Index and front matter are not included in the reading files. The translation text is retained; chapter/paragraph layout and scan artifacts are normalized.
- OCR is not proofread against the facsimile. This is a formatted transcription draft, not a verified edition.
- The source contains illustrations and diagram references that are not reconstructed in this text-only draft.

## Editorial decisions

- Soft-wrapped OCR lines were joined into paragraphs.
- Obvious running headers and isolated page-number lines were removed.
- Book and chapter headings and numbered paragraph labels were retained where legible.
- OCR uncertainty, damaged/garbled text, and unusual punctuation are not silently corrected.
- Collation against the 1914 scan and a completeness audit of every chapter are still required.
