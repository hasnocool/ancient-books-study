## Translator/editor responsibility
The preface states that Morgan completed the translation except for the final four chapters of Book X. Herbert Langford Warren completed the translation from Chapter XIII onward and assumed responsibility for that portion. Warren also describes editorial changes made to earlier text. Book X is therefore not wholly Morgan's translation.

## Formatting and editorial policy
- All source text is retained in contiguous ranges; this pass changes file organization, not the wording of the OCR.
- Book headings are assigned from the source's ten main-text Book markers. OCR errors in chapter headings and Roman numerals remain visible.
- Front matter and index matter are preserved separately.
- No OCR spellings have been silently corrected or modernized.
- Illustrations and diagrams referenced in the print edition are not recreated here.

## Completeness verification
- Ten main-text Book divisions are separated in order, I–X.
- Front matter and index/end matter are preserved separately.
