# Enoch Verse Restoration Log

This log records verse boundaries restored from the printed 1893 Clarendon Press edition. The words inside the formatted reading edition remain the words of the repository's combined transcription, which is not a verbatim reproduction of the 1893 text.

## Reference edition

## Text-preservation check

The chapter bodies for Chapters II–X were compared with the selected repository source after removing the inserted verse labels and editorial notes; all matched. Chapter I also contains the opening blessing that appears before the table of contents in the repository transcription, so the comparison must include that prefatory sentence as the start of Chapter I.

This confirms that the verse markup did not change the transferred source wording. It does not establish that the repository's underlying wording is identical to the 1893 edition.

## Editorial limitation

Verse numbers and breaks have been borrowed from Charles's 1893 printing as a structural reference. The repository's wording is preserved in place. This is not a verbatim Charles transcription: direct comparison shows lexical and grammatical differences, and those differences need their own textual collation.
