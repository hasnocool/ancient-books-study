# Chapter 89: parenthetical identification audit and text-layer collation

**Witness:** R. H. Charles, *The Book of Enoch* (Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1893), Section IV, Chapter LXXXIX, printed pp.231–247. Cornell/Internet Archive searchable text: https://archive.org/stream/cu31924014590545/cu31924014590545_djvu.txt. OCR is a searchable witness, not a substitute for page images.

## Scope and method

This pass audited every parenthetical historical/geographical identification inserted into the repository's formatted Chapter 89, traced identifications against Charles's adjacent critical and exegetical notes, and compared the chapter sequence with the 1893 text layer. The chapter begins on printed p.231 and the next chapter begins on p.247. No page-image pixels were inspected.

## Main finding

The formatted Chapter 89 previously embedded these labels inside the running verses: Noah, Jacob, Red Sea, Egyptians, Moses, Jordan, Joshuah, Saul, David, Solomon, Temple, Elyah, and “2nd Temple.” These are editorial identifications, not words in Charles's printed running translation. They have been removed from the verse text and relocated to a labelled editorial-identifications note immediately after Chapter 89.

Two labels were also wrong in their verse placement:

- **89:37:** Charles identifies the first sheep's falling asleep with **Aaron**, alongside the death of the generation that left Egypt. The stream is the Jordan. The former Moses label was incorrect.
- **89:38:** the sheep who led them and became a man is **Moses**, whose death Charles identifies here. The former Joshuah label was incorrect. Joshua belongs with the later succession of leaders in 89:39.

## Text-layer collation observations

- **89:1:** Charles's text says “built for himself a great vessel”; Noah is supplied by the note, not the verse. Charles also identifies the three bulls as Noah's sons.
- **89:9–13:** the text uses animal imagery without personal names. Charles's notes supply Noah's sons, Abraham, Ishmael, Isaac, Esau, Jacob, and Joseph. These belong in the editorial register, not in the translation.
- **89:15:** Charles himself prints the parenthetical clarification “(the sheep's)” after “their.” This is distinct from repository-added historical glosses and is preserved.
- **89:23 and 89:26:** “Red Sea” and “Egyptians” are not in the running text. The verses say “a sea of water” and “wolves”; the Exodus identification comes from the narrative and Charles's commentary.
- **89:37–39:** the repository's previous Moses/Joshuah labels were misplaced. Charles distinguishes Aaron's death at 89:37, Moses's death at 89:38, and the Judges including Joshua at 89:39.
- **89:45–49:** Saul/David/Solomon are explanatory identifications. Charles discusses David's anointing in 89:45–46, David's reign at 89:49, and Solomon as the little sheep in 89:48. His proposed relocation of the latter part of 89:48 remains a reconstruction; printed verse order is retained.
- **89:50–52:** Charles identifies the house as Jerusalem and the tower as the Temple; Elijah's escape/translation is the subject of 89:52.
- **89:72–74:** Charles names Zerubbabel and allows Ezra or Nehemiah for the third sheep. “Second Temple” is a concise editorial gloss, not source wording.

This is a targeted collation of historical identifications and key apparatus, not a complete diplomatic transcription of every word and variant on pp.231–247. OCR column order and recognition errors still require page-image checking.

## Repository changes
