The text in this file is preserved from the repository source, not silently replaced with wording from another translation. Chapter headings are retained. ## Chapter 91

Now my son Methuselah, call to me all your brothers, and gather together to me all the sons of your mother; for the word calls me and the spirit is poured out upon me, that I should show you everything that will befall you for ever.  Thereupon Methuselah went and called to him all his brothers and assembled his relatives he conversed with all the children of righteousness and spoke: —Hear, you, sons of Enoch, all the words of your father, and hearken accordingly to the voice of my mouth; for I exhort, and say unto you, beloved, love uprightness, and walk therein.  Draw not nigh to uprightness with a double heart, and associate not with those of a double heart; but walk in uprightness, and righteousness, and it will guide you on good paths, and righteousness will be your companion.

For I know that a condition of oppression will increase on the earth, and a great chastisement will be executed on the earth, and all unrighteousness will be consummated, and be cut off from the roots, and its whole superstructure destroyed.  Unrighteousness will again be consummated on the earth, and all the deeds of unrighteousness, and of violence, and transgression will again prevail.  Then when unrighteousness, and sin, and blasphemy, and violence in all kinds of deeds will increase, and apostasy, and transgression, and uncleanness increase, a great chastisement from heaven will come upon them all, and the holy Lord will come forth with wrath, and chastisement to execute judgment on earth.

In those days violence will be cut off from its roots, and the roots of unrighteousness, together with deceit, and they will be destroyed from under heaven.  All the idols of the heathen will be abandoned: the temples will be burned with fire, and they will be removed from the whole earth, and they will be cast into the judgment of fire, and will perish in wrath, and in grievous eternal judgment.  The righteous one will arise from sleep, and wisdom will arise, and be given unto them.

After that the roots of unrighteousness will be cut off, and the sinners will be destroyed by the sword, will be cut off from blasphemers in every place, and those who devise oppression, and those who commit blasphemy will perish by the edge of the sword after that there will be another week, the eighth, that of righteousness, and a sword will be given to it that judgment, and righteousness may be executed on those who commit oppression, and sinners will be delivered into the hands of the righteous.  At its close they will acquire houses through their righteousness, and the house of the Great King will be built in glory forever more.  After that in the ninth week the righteous judgment will be revealed to the whole world, and all the works of the godless will vanish from the whole earth, and the world will be written down for destruction, and all mankind will look to the path of uprightness.

After this, in the tenth week in the seventh part, there will be the great eternal judgment, in which He will execute vengeance amongst the angels.  The first heaven will depart, and pass away, and a new heaven will appear, and all the powers of the heavens will shine sevenfold forever.  After that there will be many weeks without number forever in goodness, and righteousness, and sin will no more be mentioned forever.

Now I tell you, my sons, and I show you the paths of righteousness, and the paths of violence, and I will show them to you again that you may know what will happen.  Now, hearken, my sons, and walk in the paths of righteousness, and walk not in paths of violence; for all who walk in the paths of unrighteousness will perish forever.

## Chapter 92

**[Verse 1]** Written by Enoch, the scribe, this complete doctrine of wisdom which deserves the praise of all men, and is a judge of the whole earth, for all my children who will dwell on the earth, and the future generations who will observe uprightness, and peace.

**[Verse 2]** Let not your spirit be troubled on account of the times; for the Holy Great One has appointed days for all things.

**[Verse 3]** The righteous one will arise from sleep, will arise, and walk in the path of righteousness, and all his path, and conversation will be in eternal goodness, and grace.

**[Verse 4]** He will be gracious to the righteous, and will give him eternal uprightness, and will give him power, and he will live in goodness, and righteousness, and will walk in eternal light.

**[Verse 5]** Sin will perish in darkness forever, and will no more be seen from that day for evermore.

## Chapter 94

**[Verse 1]** Now I say unto you, my sons, love righteousness, and walk therein; for the paths of righteousness are worthy of acceptation, but the paths of unrighteousness are suddenly destroyed, and vanish.

**[Verse 2]** To certain men of a generation will the paths of violence, and of death be revealed, and they will hold themselves afar from them, and will not follow them.

**[Verse 3]** Now I say unto you, the righteous: Walk not in the path of wickedness, nor on the paths of death, and draw not nigh unto them lest you be destroyed.

**[Verse 4]** But seek, and choose for yourselves righteousness, and a holy life, and walk in the paths of peace that you may live, and prosper.

**[Verse 5]** Hold fast my words in the thoughts of your hearts, and suffer them not to be effaced from your hearts; for I know that sinners will tempt men to make wisdom wicked, and no place will be found for her, and no manner of temptation will diminish.

**[Verse 6]** Woe to those who build unrighteousness, and oppression, and lay deceit as a foundation; for they will be suddenly overthrown, and will have no peace.

**[Verse 7]** Woe to those that build their houses with sin; for they will be overthrown from their foundation, and will fall by the sword; and those who acquire gold, and silver will perish in judgment suddenly.

**[Verse 8]** Woe to you rich, for you have trusted in your riches, and from your riches you shall depart, because you have not remembered the Most High in the days of your riches.

**[Verse 9]** You have committed blasphemy, and unrighteousness, and have become ready for the day of slaughter, and the day of darkness, and the day of the great judgment.

**[Verse 10]** Thus I speak, and declare unto you that He who has created you will overthrow you, and for your fall there will be no compassion, and your Creator will rejoice at your destruction.

**[Verse 11]** Your righteous ones in those days will be a cause of shame to the sinners, and the godless.

## Chapter 95

**[Verse 1]** Oh that mine eyes were a cloud of water that I might weep over you, and shed my tears as a cloud of water; that so I might rest from my trouble of heart!

**[Verse 2]** Who has permitted you to practise hate, and wickedness?  May judgment light upon you, sinners!

**[Verse 3]** Fear not the sinners, you righteous; for again will the Lord deliver them into your hands that you may execute judgment on them according to your desires.

**[Verse 4]** Woe to you who fulminate irreversible anathemas: healing shall therefore be far from you because of your sins.

**[Verse 5]** Woe to you who requite your neighbour with evil; for you will be requited according to your works.

**[Verse 6]** Woe to you, lying witnesses, and to those who weigh out injustice, for suddenly will you perish.

**[Verse 7]** Woe to you sinners, for you persecute the righteous; for you will be delivered up, and persecuted, you people of injustice, and heavy will their yoke be upon you.

## Chapter 97

**[Verse 1]** Believe, you righteous; for the sinners will come to shame, and perish in the day of unrighteousness.

**[Verse 2]** It will become known to you that the Most High is mindful of your destruction, and the angels of heaven rejoice over your destruction.

**[Verse 3]** What will you do, you sinners, and where will you flee on that day of judgment when you hear the voice of the prayer of the righteous?

**[Verse 4]** It will not fare with you as with them, you against whom this word will stand as a testimony: you have been companions of sinners.

**[Verse 5]** In those days the prayer of the righteous will reach unto the Lord, and the days of your judgment will overtake you.

**[Verse 6]** All the words of your unrighteousness will be read out before the Great Holy One, and your faces will be covered with shame, and every work which is grounded in unrighteousness will be rejected.

**[Verse 7]** Woe to you, you sinners, on the mid ocean, and the dry land, for their remembrance of you is evil.

**[Verse 8]** Woe to you who acquire silver, and gold in unrighteousness, yet say: We have increased in riches: we have possessions; and we have acquired everything we desire.

**[Verse 9]** Now let us do that which we purpose; for we have gathered silver, and our granaries are full, and plentiful as water are the husbandmen in our houses.

**[Verse 10]** Like water your lies will flow away; for riches will not abide with you but will ascend suddenly from you; for you have acquired it all in unrighteousness, and you will be given over to a great condemnation.

## Chapter 98

**[Verse 1]** Now I swear to you, to the wise and foolish, for you will experience much on the earth.

**[Verse 2]** For you men will put on more adornments than a woman, and coloured garments more than a virgin: in royalty, and in grandeur, and in power, and in silver, and in gold, and in purple, and in splendour, and in food they will be poured out as water.

**[Verse 3]** Therefore they will be wanting in knowledge, and wisdom, and they will perish thereby together with their possessions, and with all their glory, and their splendour, and in shame, and in slaughter, and in great destitution will their spirits be cast into the furnace of lire.

**[Verse 4]** I have sworn unto you, you sinners, as a mountain does not become a slave, and will not, nor a hill the handmaid of a woman, even so sin has not been sent upon the earth, but man of himself has created it, and into great condemnation will those fall who commit it.

**[Verse 5]** barrenness has not been given to the woman, but on account of the deeds of her own hands she dies without children.

**[Verse 6]** I have sworn unto you, you sinners, by the Holy, and Great One that all your evil deeds are revealed in the heavens, and that none of your deeds of oppression are covered or hidden.

**[Verse 7]** Do not think in your spirit nor say in your heart that you do not know, and that you do not see that every sin is every day recorded in the presence of the Most High.

**[Verse 8]** From henceforth you know that all your oppression wherewith you oppressed is written down every day until the day of your judgment.

**[Verse 9]** Woe to you fools, for through your folly will you perish: you have transgressed against the wise, and so good hap will not be your portion.

**[Verse 10]** Now, know you that you are prepared for the day of destruction: wherefore do not hope to live, you sinners, but you shall depart, and die; for you know no ransom; for you are prepared for the day of the great judgment, and for the day of tribulation, and great shame for your spirit.

**[Verse 11]** Woe to you, obstinate of heart, who work wickedness, and eat blood: whence have you good 56 things to eat, and drink, and to be filled?  From all the good things which our Lord, the Most High, has placed in abundance on the earth; and you indeed shall have no peace.

**[Verse 12]** Woe to you who love the deeds of unrighteousness: wherefore do you hope for good hap unto yourselves?  Know that you shall be delivered into the hands of the righteous, and they will cut off your necks, and slay you, and will have no pity upon you.

**[Verse 13]** Woe to you who rejoice in the tribulation of the righteous; for no grave of yours will be seen.

**[Verse 14]** Woe to you who set at nought the words of the righteous; for no hope of life will be yours.

**[Verse 15]** Woe to you who write down lying, and godless words; for they write down their lies that men may hear them, and transgress against neighbour.

**[Verse 16]** Therefore they will have no peace but will die a sudden death.

## Chapter 99

**[Verse 1]** Woe to them who act godlessly, and glory in lying words, and extol them: you will perish, and no happy life will be yours.

**[Verse 2]** Woe to them who pervert the words of uprightness, and transgress the eternal law, and transform themselves into what they were not, mean, into sinners: they shall be trodden under foot upon the earth.

**[Verse 3]** In those days make ready, you righteous, to raise your prayers as a memorial, and you will place them as a testimony before the angels in order that they may place the sin of the sinners for a memorial before the Most High.

**[Verse 4]** In those days the nations will be stirred up, and the families of the nations will arise on the day of destruction.

**[Verse 5]** In those days those who are reduced to want will go forth, and mangle their children, and they will cast them away, and there will be miscarriages; and they will cast away their sucklings, and will not return unto them, and will have no pity on their beloved ones.

**[Verse 6]** Again I swear to you sinners, that sin is prepared for a day of unceasing bloodshed.

**[Verse 7]** They will worship stones, and others will make graven images of gold, and silver, and wood, and clay, and others will worship impure spirits, and demons, and all kinds of superstitions not according to knowledge, notwithstanding no manner of help will be found in them.

**[Verse 8]** They will become godless by reason of the foolishness of their hearts, and their eyes will be blinded through the fear of their hearts, and through visions in their dreams.

**[Verse 9]** Through these they will become godless, and fearful, because they work all their works in a lie, and they worship a stone: therefore in an instant will they perish.

**[Verse 10]** But in those days blessed are all they who accept the words of wisdom, and understand them, and follow out the paths of the Most High, and walk in the path of His righteousness, and become not godless with the godless; for they will be saved.

**[Verse 11]** Woe to you who hope for misfortune to your neighbour; for you will be slain in Sheol.

**[Verse 12]** Woe to you who make deceitful, and false measures, and who tempt on the earth; for they will thereby be utterly consumed.

**[Verse 13]** Woe to you who build your houses through the grievous toil of others, and their building material is nothing save the bricks, and stones of sin, I tell you, you will have no peace.

**[Verse 14]** Woe to them who reject the measure, and eternal heritage of their fathers, and whose souls follow after idols; for they will have no rest.

**[Verse 15]** Woe to them who work unrighteousness, and aid oppression, and slay their neighbours until the day of the great judgment.

**[Verse 16]** For He will cast down your glory, and bring affliction on your hearts, and will arouse the spirit of His indignation to destroy you all with the sword; and all the righteous, and holy will remember your sins.

## Chapter 100

In those days the fathers together with their sons will be smitten in one place, brothers will fall in death one with another until it streams with their blood like a river.  For a man will not withhold his hand from slaying his sons, and his sons, and the sinner will not withhold his hand from his honoured brother: from dawn until sunset they will slay one another.  The horses will walk up to the breast in the blood of sinners, and the chariot will be submerged to its height.

In those days the angels will descend into the secret places, and will gather into one place all those who brought down sin, and the Most High will arise on that day of judgment to execute great judgment amongst sinners.  Over the righteous, and holy he will appoint as guardians holy angels to guard them as the apple of an eye until He has made an end of all wickedness, and all sin, and though the righteous sleep a long sleep, they have nought to fear.  The wise amongst men will see the truth, and the children of earth will understand all the words of this book, and recognise that their riches will not be able to save them in the overthrow of their sins.

Woe to you sinners, when you afflict the righteous on the day of great trouble, and burn them with fire: therefore you will be requited according to your works.  Woe to you, obstinate of heart, who watch in order to devise wickedness: therefore fear shall come upon you, and there will be none to help you.  Woe to you, you sinners; for on account of the words of your mouth, and on account of the deeds of your hands which you have godlessly wrought, you will burn in a fire of blazing flame.

Now know you that the angels will seek out your deeds in heaven from the sun, and from the moon, and from the stars in reference to your sins because you execute judgment on the righteous upon earth.  He will summon to testify against you cloud, and mist, and dew, and rain; for they will all be withheld by you from descending upon you, and that because of your sins.  Now give presents to the rain that it be not withheld from descending upon you, nor yet the dew, when it has received gold, and silver from you that it may descend.

When the hoar frost, and snow with their chilliness, and all the winds of the snow with all their plagues fall upon you, in those days you will not be able to stand before them.

## Chapter 101

Observe the heaven, you children of heaven, and every work of the Most High, and fear you Him, and work no evil in His presence.  When He closes the windows of heaven, and withholds the rain, and the dew from descending on the earth on your account, what will you do then?  When He sends His anger upon you because of all your deeds, you cannot petition Him; for you have spoken proud, and insolent words against His righteousness: therefore you will have no peace.

See you not the sailors of the ships, how their ships are tossed to, and fro by the waves, and are shaken by the winds, and are in sore trouble?  Therefore do they fear because all their goodly possessions go into the sea with them, and they are anxious of heart lest the sea should swallow them, and they should perish therein.  Are not the entire sea, and all its waters, and all its movements the work of the Most High, and all its doings, and its waters, and has He not confined it throughout by the sand?

At His reproofs it is afraid, and dries up, and all its fish die, and all that is in it; but you sinners who are on the earth fear Him not.  Has He not made the heaven, and the earth, and all that is therein; and who has given understanding, and wisdom to all that move on the earth, and to all that move in the sea?  Do not the sailors of the ships fear the sea?

Yet sinners fear not the Most High.

## Chapter 102

In those days when He brings a grievous fire upon you, where will you flee, and where will you find deliverance?  And when he launches forth His word against you, will you not be affrighted, and fear?  All the luminaries will quake with great fear, and all the earth will be affrighted, and tremble, and be alarmed.

All the angels will execute their commands, and will seek to hide themselves from the presence of the Great Glory, and the children of earth will tremble, and quake.  And as for you, you sinners, you are cursed for ever, and you will have no peace.  Fear you not, you souls of the righteous, and be hopeful you that die in righteousness.

Grieve not if your soul descends in grief into Sheol, and that in your life your body has not fared as your goodness deserved but truly as on a day on which you became like the sinners, and on a day of cursing, and chastisement.  When you die the sinners speak over you: as we die, so die the righteous, and what benefit do they reap from their deeds?  Behold, even as we, so do they die in grief, and darkness, and what advantage have they over us?

From henceforth we are equal.  What will they receive, and what will they see forever?  For behold they too have died, and from henceforth forever they will see no light.

I tell you, you sinners, you are content to eat, and drink, and strip men naked, and rob, and sin, and acquire wealth, and see good days.  Have you seen the righteous how their end falls out?  For no manner of violence is found in them until the day of their death.

Nevertheless they perished, and became as though they had not been, and their souls descended into Sheol in tribulation.

## Chapter 103

Now therefore I swear to you, the righteous, by the glory of Him that is great, and honoured, and mighty in dominion, and by His greatness I swear to you.  I know this mystery, and have read it in the heavenly tables, and have seen the book of the holy ones, and have found written therein, and inscribed regarding them: That all goodness, and joy, and glory are prepared for them, and are written down for the spirits of those who have died in righteousness, and that manifold good will be given to you in recompense for your labours, and that your lot is abundantly beyond the lot of the living.  Your spirits of you who die in righteousness, will live, and rejoice, and be glad, and their spirits will not perish, but their memorial will be before the face of the Great One unto all the generations of the world: wherefore then fear not their contumely.

Woe to you, you sinners, when you die in your sins, and those who are like you say regarding you: Blessed are they, the sinners: they have seen all their days.  Now they have died in prosperity, and in riches, and have not seen tribulation or murder in their life; and they have died in honour, and judgment has not been executed on them during their life.  Know you that their souls will be made to descend into Sheol, and they will become wretched, and great will be their tribulation.

Into darkness, and chains, and a burning fire, where there is grievous condemnation, will your spirits enter; and there will be grievous condemnation for the generations of the world.  Woe to you, for you will have no peace.  Say not in regard to the righteous, and good who are in life: In the days of their life they are worn out with their troubles toil, and have experienced every trouble, and met with much evil, and suffered from disease, and have been diminished, and become small in spirit.

They are destroyed, and there has been none to help them in word, and have attained to nothing: they are tortured, and destroyed, and have not hoped to see life from day to day.  They hoped to be the head, and they have become the tail: they toiled laboriously, and attained not to the fruit of their toil; and they became the food of sinners, and the unrighteous laid their yoke heavily upon them.  They that hated them, and smote them have had dominion over them; and they have bowed their necks to those that hated them, and they have had no compassion on them.

They have desired to get away from them that they might escape, and be at rest, but have found no place whereunto they should flee, and be safe from them.  They have complained to their rulers in their tribulation, and cried out against those who devoured them, but they did not attend to their cries, and would not hearken to their voice.  They helped those who robbed, and devoured them, and those who made them few; and they concealed their oppression, and they did not remove from them the yoke of those who devoured, and dispersed, and murdered them, and they concealed their murder, and have thought not of the fact that they had lifted up their hands against them.

## Chapter 104

I swear unto you that in heaven the angels are mindful of you for good before the glory of the Great One: your names are written before the glory of the Great One.  Be hopeful; for aforetime you were put to shame through ills, and affliction; but soon you will shine as the stars of heaven, you will shine, and you will be seen, and the portals of heaven will be opened to you.  Persist in your cry for judgment, and it will appear to you; for all your tribulation will be visited on the rulers, and on all their helpers, and on those who plundered you.

Be hopeful, and cast not away your hope; for you will have great joy as the angels of heaven.  What will you be obliged to do then?  You will not have to hide on the day of the great judgment, and you will not be found as sinners, and the eternal judgment will be far from you for all the generations of the world.

Now fear not, you righteous, when you see the sinners growing strong, and prospering in their ways, and be not like unto them, and have no companionship with them, but keep afar from their violence; for you will become companions of the hosts of heaven.  You sinners, though you say, you cannot ascertain it, and all our sins are not written down.  Still they will write down all your sins continually every day.

Now I show unto you that light, and darkness, day, and night see all your sins.  Be not godless in your hearts, and lie not, and alter not the word of uprightness, and do not charge with lying the words of the Holy Great One, and glorify not your idols; for all your lying, and all your godlessness will prove not to be righteousness but to be great sin.  Now I know this mystery that many sinners will alter, and pervert the words of uprightness, and will speak wicked words, and lie, and practise great deceits, and write books concerning their words.

But when they write down truthfully all my words in their languages, and do not change or diminish ought from my words but write them all down truthfully, all that I first testified concerning them: Then, I know another mystery that books will be given to the righteous, and the wise to become a cause of joy, and uprightness, and much wisdom.  And to them will the books be given, and they will believe in them, and rejoice over them, and then will all the righteous who have learnt there from all the paths of uprightness be recompensed.
---

## Chapter 105

In those days, says the Lord, they shall call and testify to the children of earth concerning their wisdom: —Show it unto them for you are their guide and a recompense over the whole earth.  For I and My Son will unite with them forever in the paths of uprightness in their lives; and you will have peace.  Rejoice, you children of uprightness.
