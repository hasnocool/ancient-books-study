# The Book of Enoch (1 Enoch) — source-attributed reading edition

This directory contains a formatted derivative of the 1 Enoch text in the repository's combined Enoch transcription. The compilation attributes the Ethiopic text to R. H. Charles and Professor August Dillmann (1893), but direct comparison confirms that the supplied wording is **not verbatim Charles 1893**. The exact textual lineage of this variant remains unidentified.

## What is included — and what is not

- **1 Enoch source text:** all 108 numbered chapter bodies in the selected repository transcription are represented across the six reading files, with the compilation's Chapter 71 retained as a separately labelled supplementary section.
- **Verse structure restored:** Chapters I–LXX now have verse divisions restored from the 1893 Charles printing as a structural reference. The repository transcription's wording is preserved and has not been silently replaced.
- **Remaining verse work:** Chapters LXXII–CVIII still require verse-level restoration.
- **2 Enoch:** the combined source also contains 68 Slavonic sections marked `Enoch Slavonic 1.` through `Enoch Slavonic 68.`. They belong to the separate *Book of the Secrets of Enoch* and are excluded here pending their own edition.
- **Full 1893 Charles volume:** this directory does not reproduce the complete scholarly volume, including its introductions, textual notes, appendices, Greek and Latin fragments, and indices.

## Why some chapters look like paragraphs

The heading `Enoch N.` in the repository transcription marks a chapter, not a paragraph. The 1893 printing also numbers verses within chapters. Some chapters are genuinely short: Chapter II has three verses, Chapter III has one numbered verse, and Chapters IV and XXXV are unnumbered units. A single paragraph under a chapter heading can therefore be correct; other chapters have multiple verses which the repository transcription had flattened together.

The reading files now preserve the chapter/verse hierarchy for Chapters I–LXX. The restoration retains irregularities in the printed numbering rather than inventing missing verses: Chapter XVI runs from verse 2 to verse 4. The 1893 editor's bracketed interpolations are also shown in the verse labels and editorial notes, including the Noachic material.

## Source and editorial policy

- Preserve the supplied repository wording unless an emendation is separately documented.
- Restore verse hierarchy only from an identified edition or facsimile.
- Keep source wording distinct from structural information borrowed from another printing.
- Preserve printed numbering gaps and unnumbered units rather than inventing labels.
- Identify interpolations using the reference edition's own editorial apparatus.
- Keep separate textual traditions and translations in separate directories.

## Verification status

- **Chapter-body transfer:** all 108 source chapter bodies were transferred and normalized against the selected repository transcription.
- **Book 1 verse structure:** Chapters I–XXXVI restored from the 1893 printing; all 36 chapter bodies match the selected repository source after removal of inserted labels and notes.
- **Book 2 verse structure:** Chapters XXXVII–LXX restored from the 1893 printing; all 34 chapter bodies match the selected repository source after removal of inserted labels, notes, and source section dividers.
- **Compilation Chapter 71:** preserved separately as a supplementary source section; it is not included in the 1893 Charles structural sequence and has no invented verse labels.
- **Remaining verse structure:** Chapters LXXII–CVIII remain to be restored.
- **Textual identity:** the selected repository text is not a verbatim Charles 1893 transcription. Exact lineage and complete textual accuracy remain unresolved.
- **2 Enoch:** 68 Slavonic sections are identified separately in the combined source and remain outside this directory.

This is a formatted source derivative, not a new translation, verified verbatim Charles transcription, or complete reproduction of the 1893 scholarly volume. See [SOURCE_NOTES.md](./SOURCE_NOTES.md) and the [verse-restoration log](./VERSE_RESTORATION_LOG.md).
