# Corpus Hermeticum — G. R. S. Mead (1906)

This directory contains structured reading editions derived from the repository transcription of G. R. S. Mead's 1906 English translation in *Thrice-Greatest Hermes*, Volume II.

The source file remains unchanged at `books_04/Corpus_Hermeticum_Mead_1906.txt`.

## Editorial policy

- Preserve Mead's wording, order, archaic forms, and meaningful brackets.
- Restore tractate titles and numbered paragraph structure.
- Remove only isolated printed page numbers and clear scan layout artifacts.
- Keep modern introductions and later editorial commentary separate from the translated text.
- Never silently reconstruct missing passages or correct uncertain readings.
- Mark every derivative as a formatted draft until it has been collated against a facsimile of the 1906 edition.

## Verification status

The tractates have undergone a structural formatting pass, not a full textual collation. Section numbering is preserved as found in the supplied transcription. Tractate XV skips from section 4 to section 6 in the source file; no section 5 has been invented. All texts still require comparison against the 1906 printed edition.
