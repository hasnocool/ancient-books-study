**Status:** Complete formatted transfer of the selected public-domain English translation; source-transfer checks passed. This is not a new translation and not a critical edition of the Coptic manuscript.

## Formatting policy
- Wording, punctuation, spelling, paragraph sequence, bracketed gaps, editorial insertions, and slash-delimited corrections are retained.
- Saying titles are translator-supplied headings. Saying numbers are modern reference labels, not manuscript verse numbering.
- No commentary has been inserted into the translated sayings.
- Translator's explanatory notes are kept separately.

## Verification
- 114 saying headings found in numerical order, 1–114.
- Four reading files split at saying headings; no saying was omitted or duplicated.
- Text was transferred from a digital mirror that identifies the primary Gospels.net source and reproduces the translator's public-domain dedication.
- This verifies transfer from the selected digital transcription, not independent collation against the Nag Hammadi Codex II facsimile or the Coptic original.

## Limitations
- This is one modern English translation, not a reconstruction of the lost Greek original.
- The translation's editorial restorations and interpretive choices remain those of Mattison.
- No claim is made that every reading has been independently checked against manuscript photographs.
