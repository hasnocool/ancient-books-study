# Source Notes — The Gospel of Thomas

## Selected text
Mark M. Mattison's English translation, based on the Coptic text of Nag Hammadi Codex II,2.

Primary source: https://www.gospels.net/thomas/

Digital transfer mirror: https://raw.githubusercontent.com/sybil-solutions/all-historical-evidence-of-jesus-christ/main/collections/gospels/gospel-of-thomas.md

The mirror identifies Gospels.net and Mattison as translator; the primary Gospels.net page expressly dedicates the translation to the public domain.

## Textual scope
The formatted reading text contains the prologue, all 114 numbered sayings, and the closing title, “The Gospel According to Thomas.” Translator's explanatory notes are separate.

## Source selection
The two legacy repository files were byte-identical and incomplete, ending at Saying 56. They were not used as the base text. The separate Nag Hammadi collection file was zero bytes. The complete Mattison translation was selected from the translator's own public-domain source page.

## Rights
The public-domain dedication is explicitly stated on the primary source page. The mirror is not treated as the licensor; the rights statement is attributed to Mattison and Gospels.net.

## Verification boundaries
This verifies transfer from the selected digital transcription and confirms all 114 sayings. It is not a Coptic manuscript collation, critical apparatus, or scholarly assessment of every translation choice.
