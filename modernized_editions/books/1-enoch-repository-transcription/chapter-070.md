### Verse 1

After this, while he was still alive, Enoch's name was lifted up from among those who live on the earth, and brought before the Son of Man and the Lord of Spirits.

### Verse 2

He was carried upward in the chariots of the spirit, and his name disappeared from among the people of the earth. From that day onward I was no longer counted among them. He placed me between the two winds, between the north and the west, where the angels held the measuring cords to measure the place prepared for the chosen and righteous. There I saw the earliest ancestors and the righteous who have lived in that place from the beginning.
