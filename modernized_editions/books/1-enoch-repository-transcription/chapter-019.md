# 1 Enoch — Chapter 19
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 19.

**Editorial method:** Complete modernization of the chapter's 3 numbered units, preserving source wording's sequence and content while updating archaic syntax. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

Uriel said to me: “Here the angels who joined themselves to women will remain. Their spirits have taken many different forms and have defiled humanity. They will lead people astray into sacrificing to demons as though they were gods. They will remain here until the day of the great judgment, when they will be judged and their punishment brought to completion.

### Verse 2

“The women who led the angels of heaven astray will suffer the same fate as their companions.”

### Verse 3

I, Enoch, alone saw this vision and the farthest reaches of all things. No other human being will see what I have seen in the way I saw it.
