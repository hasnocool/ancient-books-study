# 1 Enoch — Chapter 53
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 53.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 7 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.



---

### Verse 1

There my eyes saw a valley with deep, open chasms. Everyone who lives on the earth, along with the people of the sea and the islands, will bring gifts, offerings, and signs of honour to him. Yet that deep valley will never be filled.

### Verse 2

They commit crimes with their hands. As sinners, they greedily consume everything they acquire. Because of their wrongdoing they will perish before the Lord of Spirits and be removed from the face of his earth forever.

### Verse 3

I saw the angels of punishment waiting there and preparing all the instruments of Satan.

### Verse 4

I asked the angel of peace who was with me: “For whom are these instruments being prepared?”

### Verse 5

He answered: “They are for the kings and powerful people of the earth, so that they may be destroyed by them.

### Verse 6

“After this, the Righteous and Chosen One will cause the house of his assembly to appear. From then on, no one will be able to obstruct them in the name of the Lord of Spirits.

### Verse 7

“These mountains will not stand firm before his righteousness any more than the earth can stand. The hills will become like springs of water, and the righteous will find rest from the oppression of sinners.”
