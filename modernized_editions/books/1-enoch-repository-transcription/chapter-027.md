# 1 Enoch — Chapter 27
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 27.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 5 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

Then I asked: “Why is this blessed land, filled with trees, located beside this cursed valley?”

### Verse 2

Uriel, one of the holy angels who was with me, answered: “This cursed valley is reserved for those who are cursed forever. Everyone who speaks shameful words against God and insults his Glory will be gathered here. This is where they will be assembled and where their punishment will take place.

### Verse 3

“In the last days, a righteous judgment will be carried out against them, and the righteous will witness it forever. Those who have received mercy will bless the Lord of Glory, the Eternal King, here.

### Verse 4

“At the time when judgment is carried out against those who came before, the righteous will bless him for the mercy he has shown them and for the place he has assigned to them.”

### Verse 5

At that time I also blessed the Lord of Glory, spoke to him, and remembered his greatness as was fitting.
