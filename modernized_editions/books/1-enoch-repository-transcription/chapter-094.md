# 1 Enoch — Chapter 94
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-05-laws-of-enoch-chapters-91-105.md`, Chapter 94.

**Editorial method:** This is a complete, verse-by-verse modernization of all eleven numbered verses in the repository transcription. It updates archaic wording while preserving the sequence and substance of the source. It is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

Now I say to you, my children: Love what is right and live by it. The ways of righteousness are worthy paths, but the ways of wrongdoing are quickly destroyed and disappear.

### Verse 2

People in a coming generation will be shown the paths of violence and death. They will keep their distance from those paths and refuse to follow them.

### Verse 3

Now I say to you who are righteous: Do not walk in the way of wickedness or on the paths that lead to death. Do not go near them, or you too may be destroyed.

### Verse 4

Instead, seek righteousness and choose a holy way of life. Walk along the paths of peace so that you may live and flourish.

### Verse 5

Keep my words firmly in your hearts and do not let them be erased from your thoughts. I know that sinners will try to corrupt wisdom and turn people against it. Wisdom will find no place among them, and their temptations will not cease.

### Verse 6

How terrible it will be for those who build their lives on wrongdoing and oppression, laying deception as their foundation. They will be overthrown suddenly and will find no peace.

### Verse 7

How terrible it will be for those who build their houses through sin. Their foundations will collapse, and they will fall by the sword. Those who accumulate gold and silver will also perish suddenly when judgment comes.

### Verse 8

How terrible it will be for you who are rich, because you have placed your trust in your wealth. You will be separated from your riches, because you did not remember the Most High while you enjoyed your prosperity.

### Verse 9

You have spoken blasphemy and committed injustice. You have made yourselves ready for the day of slaughter, the day of darkness, and the day of the great judgment.

### Verse 10

This is what I declare to you: The One who created you will bring you down. No one will show compassion when you fall, and your Creator will rejoice at your destruction.

### Verse 11

In those days, the righteous among you will become a source of shame to sinners and to those who reject what is right.

---

## Completeness and editorial notes

- All **eleven numbered verses** in the repository's Chapter 94 have been rendered individually.
- The chapter's exhortation to righteousness, warning against violent paths, instruction to preserve wisdom, condemnation of oppression and deceit, warning to the wealthy who forget the Most High, and closing reversal in which the righteous shame the wicked are retained.
- No verse has been intentionally omitted, merged, or replaced with a summary.
- **1893 / 1917 witness difference:** The 1893 main text prints the final sentence of 94:7—concerning those who acquire gold and silver and suddenly perish in judgment—as ordinary, unbracketed text. The 1917 printing places that clause inside square brackets, which its notation guide defines as interpolation. The modernization follows the repository transcription and the 1893 main-text presentation; it does not silently import the later 1917 brackets. Charles's 1893 commentary also states that Chapter 94 followed 91:12–19 in the earlier sequence.
- This rendering follows the repository's chapter and verse divisions and does not silently substitute another translation's wording or arrangement.
