# 1 Enoch — Chapter 25
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 25.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 7 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

He said to me: “Enoch, why are you asking about the fragrance of this tree? What do you want to know?”

### Verse 2

I, Enoch, answered him: “I want to understand everything, but especially this tree.”

### Verse 3

He answered: “This high mountain you have seen, whose summit resembles the throne of the Lord, is his throne. The Holy and Great One, the Lord of Glory and Eternal King, will sit there when he comes down to visit the earth with goodness.

### Verse 4

“No mortal is permitted to touch this fragrant tree until the great day of judgment, when he will bring justice and bring all things to their final completion forever.

### Verse 5

“Then this tree will be given to the righteous and humble. Its fruit will give life to the chosen. The tree will be transplanted to the north, to the holy place and the temple of the Lord, the Eternal King.

### Verse 6

“Then they will rejoice and be glad. They will enter the holy dwelling, and its fragrance will fill their bodies. They will live long lives on earth, as your ancestors did. During their days, sorrow, pain, trouble, and disaster will not affect them.”

### Verse 7

Then I blessed the Lord of Glory, the Eternal King, because he had prepared these things for the righteous, created them, and promised to give them to his people.
