# 1 Enoch — Chapter 60
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 60.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 25 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Textual caution:** Source-marked interpolation status is retained; Chapter 60 is identified in the source as a Noachic interpolation.

---

### Verse 1

In the year five hundred, during Enoch's lifetime, in the seventh month, on the fourteenth day of the month—

### Verse 2

—in this parable I saw the highest heaven shaken by a mighty earthquake. The host of the Most High and the angels—thousands upon thousands and ten thousand times ten thousand—were thrown into tremendous agitation.

### Verse 3

The Ancient of Days sat upon his glorious throne, with the angels and the righteous standing around him.

### Verse 4

A great trembling seized me, and fear took hold of me. My body weakened, my whole being seemed to melt away, and I fell face down.

### Verse 5

Then Michael sent another angel from among the holy ones to raise me up. My spirit returned to me, because I had been unable to endure the sight of that host and the upheaval and shaking of heaven.

### Verse 6

Michael said to me: “What vision has disturbed you so greatly? Until this day, the time of his mercy has continued, because he has shown mercy and patience toward those who live on the earth.

### Verse 7

“But a day is coming with power, punishment, and judgment. The Lord of Spirits has prepared it for those who do not follow the law of righteousness, those who reject righteous judgment, and those who misuse his name. That day is prepared: for the chosen it will confirm a covenant, but for sinners it will bring an accounting.

### Verse 8

“On that day two great creatures will be separated. The female creature is named Leviathan, and she will dwell in the depths of the ocean, above the sources of the waters.

### Verse 9

“The male creature is called Behemoth. His chest occupies a desolate wilderness named Dendain, east of the garden where the chosen and righteous dwell. It is the place where my grandfather was taken up—Enoch, the seventh from Adam, the first human being created by the Lord of Spirits.”

### Verse 10

I asked the other angel to show me the power of these creatures: how they were separated on one day, with one placed in the depths of the sea and the other in the wilderness on dry land.

### Verse 11

He said to me: “Son of man, you are trying to discover what is hidden.”

### Verse 12

Then the other angel who travelled with me and revealed hidden things spoke to me. He showed me what is first and last—in the heights of heaven, beneath the earth in its depths, at the edges of heaven, at heaven's foundations, and in the chambers of the winds.

### Verse 13

“He showed me how the spirits are separated, how they are weighed, and how the sources of the spirits are counted, each according to the power of its spirit and the power of the lights of the moon. He showed me how these powers serve righteousness and how the stars are divided according to their names and appointed groups.

### Verse 14

“He showed me the thunder according to the places where it strikes, and all the divisions among the lightning, so that it may flash and its hosts may obey together.

### Verse 15

“Thunder has places where it must rest. It waits there until it is time to roar. Thunder and lightning cannot be separated. Though they are not one thing, they travel together through the spirit and do not part.

### Verse 16

“When lightning flashes, thunder gives its voice. The spirit makes the thunder pause and divides their activity in proper measure. The storehouses of their thunder are like grains of sand. Each time thunder sounds, it is held back by a bridle, turned aside by the spirit's power, and sent forward according to the divisions of the earth.

### Verse 17

“The spirit of the sea is strong and masculine. According to its great strength, it draws the sea back as though by a rein, then drives it forward and spreads it among all the mountains of the earth.

### Verse 18

“The spirit of frost has its own angel, and the spirit of hail is a good angel.

### Verse 19

“He has released the spirit of snow because of its power. It has a spirit of its own. What rises from it looks like smoke, and it is called frost.

### Verse 20

“The spirit of mist does not share their chambers; it has a chamber of its own. Its course moves through clear and bright conditions, through darkness, through winter, and through summer. Its chamber is light, and it has its own angel.

### Verse 21

“The spirit of dew dwells at the edges of heaven and is connected with the chambers of rain. It travels in winter and summer. The clouds of dew and the clouds of mist are connected, and each supplies the other.

### Verse 22

“When the spirit of rain leaves its chamber, the angels come and open the chamber and lead it out. It spreads across the whole earth and joins with the water already on the ground.

### Verse 23

“These waters are for those who live on the earth. They nourish the earth, coming from the Most High in heaven. Therefore rain is measured, and the angels are responsible for it.

### Verse 24

“All these things I saw in the direction of the Garden of the Righteous.

### Verse 25

“The angel of peace who was with me said: ‘These two great creatures have been prepared to receive food in accordance with God's greatness. The punishment of the Lord of Spirits will bring lamentation and will cause sons and mothers, and children and fathers, to be struck down together. When the punishment of the Lord of Spirits comes upon them, it will not be in vain. Afterward judgment will take place according to his mercy and patience.’”

**Editorial note:** The source edition identifies the whole chapter as a Noachic interpolation. The complete chapter is retained and modernized as such.
