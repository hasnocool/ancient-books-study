# 1 Enoch — Chapter 10
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 10.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 22 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Textual caution:** Interpolations identified by the source edition remain separately bracketed and labelled.

---

### Verse 1 — interpolation

[Then the Most High, the Great and Holy One, spoke and sent Arsjalaljur to the son of Lamech with this message:]

### Verse 2 — interpolation

[“Tell him in my name: ‘Hide yourself!’ Explain to him that the approaching end is near. The whole earth will be destroyed. A flood will soon come over the entire earth, and everything upon it will perish.]

### Verse 3 — interpolation

[“‘Instruct him so that he may escape and his descendants may be preserved for all generations.’”]

### Verse 4

Then the Lord spoke to Rafael: “Bind Azazel by his hands and feet and put him in darkness. Open a place in the desert at Dudael and put him there.

### Verse 5

“Pile rough, jagged rocks over him. Cover him in darkness and leave him there forever. Cover his face so that he cannot see the light.

### Verse 6

“On the great day of judgment, he will be thrown into the fire.

### Verse 7 — interpolation

[“Heal the earth that the angels have corrupted. Announce that I will heal the earth, so that human beings will not all perish because of the forbidden knowledge the Watchers revealed and taught to their children.]

### Verse 8

“The whole earth has been corrupted by the teachings and deeds of Azazel. Charge him with responsibility for all this sin.

### Verse 9

“God then said to Gabriel: ‘Go against the illegitimate ones, the rejected ones, and the children born from forbidden unions. Destroy the children of those unions and the children of the Watchers from among humanity.

### Verse 10

“‘Lead them out and set them against one another, so that they destroy one another in battle. Their lives will not be long. Their fathers may plead for them and hope that each will live for five hundred years, but their requests will not be granted.

### Verse 11

“‘The Lord said to Michael: Go and announce the sentence to Semjaza and his companions, who joined themselves to women and defiled themselves through every kind of uncleanness.

### Verse 12

“‘When their sons have killed one another and they have witnessed the destruction of those they love, bind the Watchers beneath the hills of the earth for seventy generations, until the day of their judgment and final completion—until the everlasting judgment is fulfilled.

### Verse 13

“‘In those days they will be led away to the fiery abyss. They will be confined in torment and imprisonment forever.

### Verse 14

“‘Anyone else who is condemned and destroyed from that time onward will be bound together with them until the end of all generations.

### Verse 15

“‘Destroy all souls given to lust, along with the children of the Watchers, because they have oppressed humanity.

### Verse 16

“‘Remove all oppression from the face of the earth. Let every evil deed come to an end. Then the shoot of righteousness and integrity will appear, and labour will become a blessing. Righteousness and integrity will be established forever in joy.

### Verse 17

“‘Then all the righteous will escape and live until they have a thousand children. They will complete all the days of their youth and old age, and they will observe the Sabbath in peace.

### Verse 18

“‘In those days the whole earth will be cultivated in righteousness. It will be planted with trees everywhere and filled with blessing.

### Verse 19

“‘Every desirable kind of tree will be planted there, along with vines. The vines will produce wine in abundance. Every measure of seed sown will yield ten thousand measures, and every measure of olives will produce ten presses of oil.

### Verse 20

“‘Cleanse the earth of all oppression, injustice, sin, irreverence, and impurity. Destroy all these things from the earth.

### Verse 21

“‘All human beings will become righteous. Every nation will worship me, offer me praise, and honour me.

### Verse 22

“‘The earth will be cleansed of all corruption, sin, punishment, and torment. From generation to generation, forever, I will never again send such destruction upon it.’”

**Editorial note:** The source marks verses 1–3 as an interpolated Noah fragment and verse 7 as an interpolation. They remain bracketed and are not merged into the surrounding narrative.
