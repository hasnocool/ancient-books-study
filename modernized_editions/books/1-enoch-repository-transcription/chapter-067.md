# 1 Enoch — Chapter 67
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 67.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 13 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Textual caution:** The source identifies part or all of this chapter as part of the Noachic interpolation. The source's distinction between interpolated and non-interpolated verses is retained.

---

### Verse 1 — interpolation

[In those days the word of God came to me: “Noah, your destiny has come before me. You are blameless, loved, and righteous.]

### Verse 2 — interpolation

[“The angels are building a wooden vessel. When they have finished it, I will place my hand upon it and preserve it. From it will come a living seed, and the earth will be changed so that it will not remain empty of inhabitants.]

### Verse 3 — interpolation

[“I will establish your descendants before me forever. I will scatter those who live with you across the earth so that they do not lead one another astray. They will be blessed and multiply on the earth in the name of the Lord.”]

**The Judgment of the Watchers**

### Verse 4 — interpolation

[God will imprison the angels who acted unjustly in the burning valley that my grandfather Enoch had previously shown me in the west, among the mountains of gold, silver, iron, soft metal, and tin.]

### Verse 5 — interpolation

[I saw that valley, where a great upheaval had taken place and the waters had surged.]

### Verse 6 — interpolation

[When these things happened, the molten metal produced by the fire and the violent upheaval gave off a smell of sulphur, which mingled with the waters.]

### Verse 7 — interpolation

[The valley of the angels who had led others astray continued to burn beneath the earth.]

### Verse 8 — interpolation

[Streams of fire flow through the valleys of that land. There the angels who led the inhabitants of the earth astray are punished.]

### Verse 9 — interpolation

[In those days these waters will serve the kings, the powerful, the exalted, and the people of the earth. They will bring healing to the body but punishment to the spirit, because those people's spirits are filled with lust. Their bodies will be punished. They have rejected the Lord of Spirits; they see their punishment every day but still refuse to believe in his name.]

### Verse 10 — interpolation

[As the burning of their bodies becomes more severe, their spirits will undergo a corresponding change forever. No one will be able to speak a lie before the Lord of Spirits.]

### Verse 11 — interpolation

[Judgment will come upon them because they followed the desires of their bodies and rejected the Spirit of the Lord.]

### Verse 12 — interpolation

[In those days the waters themselves will change. When the angels are punished in these waters, the temperature of the springs will change. When the angels rise from them, the spring water will become cold.]

### Verse 13 — interpolation

[I heard Michael answer: “This judgment upon the angels is a warning to kings, powerful people, and all who possess the earth. These waters of judgment bring healing to the bodies of kings and satisfy their bodily desires. Because of this, they will not understand or believe that the waters will change and become an everlasting, burning fire.”]

**Editorial note:** The source identifies the entire chapter as part of the Noachic interpolation extending from Chapters 65–69:25. All thirteen verses remain bracketed.
