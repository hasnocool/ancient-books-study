# 1 Enoch — Chapter 83
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-04-visions-chapters-83-90.md`, Chapter 83.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 11 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

Now, my son Methuselah, I will show you all the visions I have seen and recount them to you.

### Verse 2

I saw two visions before I took a wife. They were completely different from each other. The first came while I was learning to write; the second came before I married your mother. I saw a terrifying vision, and I prayed to the Lord about both.

### Verse 3

I had lain down in the house of my grandfather Mahalalel when I saw in a vision that heaven collapsed, was carried away, and fell to the earth.

### Verse 4

When heaven fell to the earth, I saw the earth swallowed by a great abyss. Mountains hung over mountains, hills sank beneath hills, and tall trees were torn from their roots, thrown down, and swallowed by the abyss.

### Verse 5

Then words came to my mouth, and I raised my voice and cried aloud: “The earth has been destroyed!”

### Verse 6

My grandfather Mahalalel woke me as I lay beside him and asked: “Why are you crying out, my son? Why are you lamenting so loudly?”

### Verse 7

I told him the entire vision I had seen. He said: “What you have seen is terrible, my son. Your dream concerns the grave sin of the whole earth. The earth must sink into the abyss and be destroyed in a great catastrophe.

### Verse 8

“Now get up, my son, and plead with the Lord of Glory. Since you believe in him, ask that a remnant may be left on the earth.

### Verse 9

“My son, all these things will come upon the earth from heaven, and a violent destruction will take place on the earth.”

### Verse 10

Then I got up, prayed, and pleaded. I wrote down my prayer for the generations of the world, and I will show everything to you, my son Methuselah.

### Verse 11

Afterward I went outside and looked at the heavens. I saw the sun rising in the east, the moon setting in the west, a few stars, and the whole earth, with everything as it had been known from the beginning. Then I blessed the Lord of judgment and praised him because he made the sun emerge through the eastern windows, rise across the face of heaven, and travel along the path appointed for it.
