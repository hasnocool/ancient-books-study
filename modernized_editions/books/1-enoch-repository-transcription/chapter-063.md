# 1 Enoch — Chapter 63
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 63.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 12 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.



---

### Verse 1

In those days the powerful and the kings who possess the earth will beg the angels of punishment, to whom they have been handed over, to grant them a little time. They will want to fall before the Lord of Spirits, worship him, and confess their sins.

### Verse 2

They will bless and glorify the Lord of Spirits, saying: “Blessed is the Lord of Spirits, the Lord of kings, the Lord of the powerful and rulers, the Lord of glory and the Lord of wisdom, whose every mystery is clear.

### Verse 3

“Your power endures from generation to generation, and your glory forever and ever. Your mysteries are deep and beyond counting, and your righteousness cannot be measured.

### Verse 4

“We have finally learned that we should glorify and bless the Lord of kings, the One who is King over all kings.”

### Verse 5

They will say: “If only we could have a moment of rest in which to glorify and thank him and confess our faith before his glory!

### Verse 6

“Now we long for a little rest, but we cannot find it. We are driven away and cannot obtain it. The light has disappeared from before us, and darkness will be our dwelling forever.

### Verse 7

“For we did not believe in him or glorify the name of the Lord of Spirits. We did not honour our Lord. Instead, we placed our hope in the sceptre of our kingdom and in our own glory.

### Verse 8

“On the day of our suffering and distress, he does not save us. We find no pause in which to confess that our Lord is truthful in all his works and judgments, and that his righteousness and judgments show no favouritism.

### Verse 9

“We will pass away from his presence because of our deeds. All our sins have been counted in the judgment.”

### Verse 10

Then they will say: “Our souls were satisfied with wealth gained through injustice, but that does not keep us from descending into the flames of Sheol's torment.”

### Verse 11

After this, their faces will be covered with darkness before the Son of Man. They will be banished from his presence, and the sword will remain before him among them.

### Verse 12

Thus the Lord of Spirits has declared the decree and judgment concerning the powerful, the kings, the exalted, and all who possess the earth, before the Lord of Spirits.
