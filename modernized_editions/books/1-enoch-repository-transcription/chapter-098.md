# 1 Enoch — Chapter 98
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-05-laws-of-enoch-chapters-91-105.md`, Chapter 98.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 16 numbered units in the repository transcription. Archaic language is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.



---

### Verse 1

Now I make this solemn declaration to both the wise and the foolish: you will experience much suffering on the earth.

### Verse 2

You men will wear more ornaments than women and more colourful clothing than young women. You will surround yourselves with royal display, grandeur, power, silver, gold, purple garments, splendour, and abundant food—as though these things were poured out like water.

### Verse 3

Yet you will lack knowledge and wisdom. Because of this, you will perish along with your possessions, glory, and splendour. Your spirits will be cast into the furnace of fire amid shame, slaughter, and extreme deprivation.

### Verse 4

I declare this to you sinners: just as a mountain does not become a slave, nor a hill a woman's servant, sin was not sent down upon the earth. Human beings created it themselves. Those who practise it will fall under severe condemnation.

### Verse 5

A woman is not made barren; because of her own deeds, she dies without children.

**Editorial note:** This verse preserves the source's ancient moral explanation of childlessness; it is not a factual or medical statement.

### Verse 6

I solemnly declare to you sinners, by the Holy and Great One, that all your evil deeds are exposed in heaven. None of your acts of oppression is covered up or hidden.

### Verse 7

Do not tell yourselves or say in your hearts, “We do not know; we cannot see.” Every day, every sin is recorded in the presence of the Most High.

### Verse 8

From now on, understand that every act of oppression you have committed is recorded each day, right up to the day of your judgment.

### Verse 9

How terrible it will be for you fools! Your own foolishness will bring about your destruction. You have rebelled against the wise, and good fortune will not be yours.

### Verse 10

Understand that you are heading toward the day of destruction. Do not expect to live on, you sinners: you will depart and die. There is no ransom available to you, for you are destined to face the great judgment, a day of suffering and profound shame.

### Verse 11

How terrible it will be for you stubborn-hearted people who practise wickedness and consume blood! How can you expect to enjoy good food and drink and satisfy yourselves with the abundance that the Most High has provided on earth? You will have no peace.

### Verse 12

How terrible it will be for you who love injustice! Why do you expect good things for yourselves? You will be handed over to the righteous, who will cut off your necks, kill you, and show you no pity.

### Verse 13

How terrible it will be for you who celebrate the suffering of the righteous. No grave will be found for you.

### Verse 14

How terrible it will be for you who dismiss the words of the righteous. You will have no hope of life.

### Verse 15

How terrible it will be for you who write down lies and godless statements so that others will hear them and be led to sin against their neighbours.

### Verse 16

For this reason you will have no peace; you will die suddenly.
