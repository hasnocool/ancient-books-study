# 1 Enoch — Chapter 42
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 42.

**Editorial method:** Complete modernization of 3 numbered units, updating archaic wording while retaining the source sequence and substance. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.



---

### Verse 1

Wisdom could find no place where she could live, so a dwelling was assigned to her in the heavens.

### Verse 2

Wisdom came down to make her home among human beings, but she could find no place to dwell. So Wisdom returned to her own place and took her seat among the angels.

### Verse 3

Then injustice came out from its own chambers. It found those it had not been seeking and settled among them, like rain falling on a desert or dew on thirsty ground.
