# 1 Enoch — Chapter 56
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 56.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 8 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Textual caution:** Source-marked interpolations remain separately bracketed and identified.

---

### Verse 1

I saw the armies of the angel of punishment. They carried whips and chains made of iron and bronze.

### Verse 2

I asked the angel of peace who accompanied me: “Where are these angels carrying their whips?”

### Verse 3

He answered: “Each is going to those appointed to him, the chosen and beloved, so that they may be cast into the chasm of the abyss in the valley.

### Verse 4

“Then that valley will be filled with those destined for punishment. Their lives will come to an end, and from that time onward the period during which they were led astray will no longer be counted.

### Verse 5 — interpolation

[In those days the angels will return and attack the East—the Parthians and Medes. They will stir up the kings, fill them with unrest, and drive them from their thrones. Like lions and hungry wolves among their flocks, they will rush out from their resting places.]

### Verse 6 — interpolation

[They will march forward and trample the land of God's chosen people. That land will lie before them like a threshing floor and a road.]

### Verse 7 — interpolation

[But the city of God's righteous people will obstruct their horses. They will begin fighting among themselves, and each person's hand will turn against the others. No one will recognize his brother, nor a son his father or mother, until the number of bodies from the slaughter is beyond counting. Their punishment will be severe.]

### Verse 8 — interpolation

[In those days Sheol will open its jaws and swallow them. Their destruction will be complete, and Sheol will consume the sinners in the presence of the chosen.]

**Editorial note:** Verses 5–8 are bracketed in the source as a later interpolation. Their status is retained here.
