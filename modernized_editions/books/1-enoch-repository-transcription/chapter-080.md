# 1 Enoch — Chapter 80
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-03-luminaries-chapters-72-82.md`, Chapter 80.

**Editorial method:** Complete modernization of all 8 numbered units in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Textual caution:** The source edition marks the entire chapter as an interpolation; all verses remain bracketed.


---

### Verse 1 — interpolation

[In those days the angel Uriel answered me: “Enoch, I have shown you everything and revealed all that you needed to see concerning the sun, the moon, the leaders of the stars of heaven, and all those who guide them—their duties, appointed times, and departures.]

### Verse 2 — interpolation

[“In the days of sinners, the years will become shorter. Their crops will be delayed in the fields, and everything on the earth will change and fail to appear in its proper season.]

### Verse 3 — interpolation

[“Rain will be held back, and the heavens will withhold it.]

### Verse 4 — interpolation

[“At that time the fruits of the earth will be delayed and will not grow in their proper season. The fruit of the trees will also fail to appear at its appointed time.]

### Verse 5 — interpolation

[“The moon will change its course and will not appear at its appointed time.]

### Verse 6 — interpolation

[“In those days an unusual failure of fruitfulness will be seen in the heavens, coming from the outermost western chariot. It will shine more brightly than its proper order allows.]

### Verse 7 — interpolation

[“Many of the leaders of the higher stars will go astray. They will change their orbits and duties and will not appear in their appointed seasons.]

### Verse 8 — interpolation

[“The entire order of the stars will be hidden from sinners. The thoughts of those who live on the earth will become confused about them. They will turn away from their proper paths, go astray, and treat the stars as gods. Evil will multiply among them, and punishment will come to destroy everything.”]

**Editorial note:** The source edition brackets the entire chapter as an interpolation. All eight verses remain bracketed.
