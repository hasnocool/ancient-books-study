# 1 Enoch — Chapter 11
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 11.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 2 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

“In those days I will open the storehouses of blessing in heaven and send their contents down upon the earth, upon the work and labour of human beings.

### Verse 2

“Peace and justice will be joined together throughout all the days of the world and through every generation.”
