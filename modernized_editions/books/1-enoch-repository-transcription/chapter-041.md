# 1 Enoch — Chapter 41
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 41.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 9 numbered units in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Textual caution:** Source-marked interpolations remain separately bracketed. Chapter 39 verse 2 is damaged in the repository transcription and is identified as a cautious rendering rather than a secure reconstruction.

---

### Verse 1

After this I saw all the secrets of heaven: how the kingdom is arranged and how human deeds are weighed on a balance.

### Verse 2

There I saw the dwellings of the chosen and the dwellings of the holy. My eyes also saw all the sinners who had rejected the name of the Lord of Spirits being driven away and dragged out. They could not remain there because of the punishment that comes from the Lord of Spirits.

### Verse 3 — interpolation

[There my eyes saw the mysteries of lightning and thunder, and the mysteries of the winds and how they are divided to blow across the earth. I saw the mysteries of the clouds and dew, where they originate and how they moisten the dust of the earth.]

### Verse 4 — interpolation

[There I saw closed chambers from which the winds are distributed, along with the chambers of hail and winds, and the chamber of mist and its cloud, which has hovered over the earth from ancient times.]

### Verse 5 — interpolation

[I saw the chambers of the sun and moon, where they emerge and where they return. I saw their glorious paths, how one is greater than the other, and how they follow their appointed courses without adding to or taking away from them. They remain faithful to one another and keep the oath established for them.]

### Verse 6 — interpolation

[First the sun goes out and travels along its path according to the command of the Lord of Spirits. Mighty is his name forever and ever.]

### Verse 7 — interpolation

[Then comes the moon, with its hidden and visible path, travelling along its appointed course by day and by night. The sun and moon stand opposite one another before the Lord of Spirits. They continually give thanks and praise and never rest, because giving thanks is their rest.]

### Verse 8 — interpolation

[The sun sometimes brings blessing and sometimes curse. The moon's course brings light to the righteous and darkness to sinners, in the name of the Lord who separated light from darkness, divided the spirits of humanity, and strengthens the spirits of the righteous through his righteousness.]

### Verse 9

No angel or power can prevent this, because God appoints a judge for everyone and judges them all in his presence.

**Editorial note:** The source marks verses 3–8 as interpolations. Their bracketed status is retained here.
