# 1 Enoch — Chapter 6
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 6.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 8 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.



---

### Verse 1

In those days, as the human population increased, beautiful and attractive daughters were born.

### Verse 2

The angels, the sons of heaven, saw these women and desired them. They said to one another: “Come, let us choose wives for ourselves from among human beings and have children with them.”

### Verse 3

Semjaza, their leader, said: “I am afraid you may not all agree to carry out this plan. If that happens, I alone will bear the penalty for this great sin.”

### Verse 4

The others answered: “Let us all swear an oath and bind ourselves with mutual curses. We must not abandon this plan; we must carry it out.”

### Verse 5

They all swore the oath together and bound themselves to fulfil it. There were two hundred of them in all.

### Verse 6

They descended during the days of Jared and gathered on the summit of Mount Hermon. They called it Mount Hermon because they had sworn their oath and bound themselves with curses there.

### Verse 7

These were the names of their leaders: Semjaza, their chief; Urakibarameel, Kokabiel, Tamiel, Ramuel, Danel, Zaqilo, Saraqujal, Asael, Armaros, Batraal, Anani, Zaqebe, Samsaveel, Sartael, Turel, Jomjael, and Arazjal.

### Verse 8

These were their leaders over groups of ten, and all the others were with them.
