# 1 Enoch — Chapter 88
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-04-visions-chapters-83-90.md`, Chapter 88.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 3 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

I saw one of the four beings who had appeared earlier. He seized the first star that had fallen from heaven, bound its hands and feet, and placed it in an abyss. The abyss was narrow, deep, terrifying, and dark.

### Verse 2

One of the beings drew his sword and gave it to the elephants, camels, and donkeys. They began striking one another, and the whole earth shook because of them.

### Verse 3

As I watched the vision, one of the four beings who had come down from heaven gathered all the great stars whose bodies resembled those of horses. He bound their hands and feet and placed them in an abyss in the earth.
