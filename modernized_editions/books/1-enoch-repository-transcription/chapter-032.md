# 1 Enoch — Chapter 32
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 32.

**Editorial method:** Complete modernization of the chapter's 6 numbered verses, updating archaic language while retaining its sequence and substance. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

After seeing these fragrant substances, I looked northward over the mountains and saw seven mountains filled with choice nard, aromatic trees, cinnamon, and pepper.

### Verse 2

From there I travelled over the mountain peaks far toward the east. I crossed above the Erythraean Sea, travelled far beyond it, and passed over the angel Zutel.

### Verse 3

I came to the Garden of Righteousness. Beyond the trees I saw many large trees growing there, fragrant, beautiful, and glorious. I also saw the tree of wisdom, which gives great wisdom to those who eat its fruit.

### Verse 4

It resembled a carob tree, and its fruit looked like clusters of grapes. It was very beautiful. The tree's fragrance spread far and wide.

### Verse 5

I said: “This tree is beautiful! How lovely and attractive it looks!”

### Verse 6

The holy angel Rufael, who was with me, answered: “This is the tree of wisdom. Your ancient father and your aged mother, who lived before you, ate from it. They gained wisdom, their eyes were opened, and they realized that they were naked. Then they were driven out of the garden.”
