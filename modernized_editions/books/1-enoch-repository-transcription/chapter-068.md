# 1 Enoch — Chapter 68
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 68.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 5 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Textual caution:** The source identifies part or all of this chapter as part of the Noachic interpolation. The source's distinction between interpolated and non-interpolated verses is retained.

---

### Verse 1 — interpolation

[After this my grandfather Enoch recorded all the signs of the mysteries in a book, together with the parables that had been given to him. He assembled them for me in the words of the Book of Parables.]

### Verse 2 — interpolation

[In those days Michael answered Rufael: “The power of the spirit moves and troubles me. But who can endure the severity of the judgment concerning these mysteries and the judgment against the angels? Who can withstand the strict sentence before which they melt away?”]

### Verse 3 — interpolation

[Michael again said to Rufael: “Whose heart would not be softened by this? Whose inner being would not be troubled by the judgment pronounced against those whom they led astray?”]

### Verse 4 — interpolation

[When he stood before the Lord of Spirits, Michael said to Rufael: “I will not defend them before the Lord. The Lord of Spirits is angry with them because they acted as though they were equal to the Lord.”]

### Verse 5 — interpolation

[Therefore everything hidden will come upon them forever. Neither angel nor human being will share their fate; they alone will bear their judgment forever and ever.]

**Editorial note:** The source identifies the entire chapter as part of the Noachic interpolation extending from Chapters 65–69:25. All five verses remain bracketed.
