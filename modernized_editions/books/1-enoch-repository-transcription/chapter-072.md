# 1 Enoch — Chapter 72
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-03-luminaries-chapters-72-82.md`, Chapter 72.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 37 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Historical-science note:** The chapter's ancient astronomical model and numerical claims are preserved as part of the source text; this modernization does not present them as current astronomy.

---

### Verse 1

This is the book describing the courses of the heavenly lights, their relationships, classes, authority, seasons, names, places of origin, and months. The holy angel Uriel, their leader, showed me these things. He explained all their laws exactly as they are, throughout the years of the world and into eternity, until the new creation that will also endure forever.

### Verse 2

This is the first law concerning the heavenly lights: the sun rises through the eastern gates of heaven and sets through the western gates.

### Verse 3

I saw six gates through which the sun rises and six through which it sets. The moon also rises and sets through these gates, as do the leaders of the stars and the stars under their direction. There are six gates in the east and six in the west, arranged in corresponding order, with many windows to the right and left of them.

### Verse 4

First comes the great light called the sun. Its circumference is like the circumference of heaven, and it is filled with fire that gives light and heat.

### Verse 5

The wind drives the chariots on which it travels. When the sun sets, it disappears from heaven and returns by way of the north toward the east. It is guided until it reaches the gate from which it shines across the heavens.

### Verse 6

In this way, during the first month, the sun rises through the great gate—specifically, the fourth of the six eastern gates.

### Verse 7

The fourth gate, through which the sun rises in the first month, has twelve window openings. When they open at their appointed times, flames come out from them.

### Verse 8

For thirty mornings in succession, the sun rises through this fourth gate and sets directly opposite it, through the fourth gate in the west.

### Verse 9

During this period, the days gradually become longer and the nights shorter, continuing until the thirtieth morning.

### Verse 10

On that day, daylight is two parts longer than darkness: the day has ten parts and the night has eight.

### Verse 11

The sun then rises through the fourth eastern gate and sets through the fourth western gate. For thirty mornings in succession it moves to the fifth eastern gate, rising and setting through the fifth pair of gates.

### Verse 12

Then daylight increases by two parts, reaching eleven parts, while the night decreases to seven parts.

### Verse 13

The sun returns eastward and enters the sixth gate. It rises and sets through the sixth gate for thirty mornings in succession, according to its appointed course.

### Verse 14

On that day the day is twice as long as the night.

### Verse 15

Then the sun begins its return, making the day shorter and the night longer. It returns eastward and enters the sixth gate, rises from it, and sets through the corresponding western gate for thirty mornings.

### Verse 16

After thirty mornings, the day decreases by one part, becoming eleven parts, while the night becomes seven parts.

### Verse 17

The sun leaves the sixth western gate, travels east, and rises for thirty mornings through the fifth eastern gate. It then sets through the fifth western gate.

### Verse 18

On that day the day decreases by two parts, becoming ten parts, while the night becomes eight.

### Verse 19

The sun rises through the fifth gate and sets through the fifth western gate. It then rises for thirty mornings through the fourth eastern gate, according to its appointed course, and sets in the west.

### Verse 20

On that day the day and night become equal: each has nine parts.

### Verse 21

The sun rises through that gate, sets in the west, returns east, and rises for thirty mornings through the third eastern gate. It sets through the third western gate.

### Verse 22

On that day the night becomes longer than the day. The night continues to lengthen and the day to shorten until the thirtieth morning, when the night has ten parts and the day eight.

### Verse 23

The sun rises through the third gate, sets through the third western gate, returns east, and rises for thirty mornings through the second eastern gate. It likewise sets through the second western gate.

### Verse 24

On that day the night has eleven parts and the day seven.

### Verse 25

The sun rises through the second gate, sets through the second western gate, and returns east. For thirty mornings it rises through the first eastern gate and sets through the first western gate.

### Verse 26

On that day the night becomes twice as long as the day: the night has twelve parts and the day six.

### Verse 27

The sun has now travelled through all the divisions of its course. It turns back along its orbit, enters that gate for thirty mornings, and sets in the western gate directly opposite.

### Verse 28

On that day the night becomes shorter by one part, reaching eleven parts, while the day becomes seven.

### Verse 29

The sun returns and enters the second eastern gate. It follows its course for thirty mornings, rising and setting.

### Verse 30

On that day the night becomes shorter, with ten parts, while the day has eight.

### Verse 31

On that day the sun rises through the second gate, sets in the west, returns east, and rises through the third gate for thirty mornings before setting in the west.

### Verse 32

On that day the night decreases to nine parts and the day increases to nine. Day and night are equal, and the year consists of exactly 364 days.

### Verse 33

The sun's course separates the lengths of day and night and their periods of increase and decrease.

### Verse 34

For this reason, its course during the day becomes longer from day to day, while its course during the night becomes shorter from night to night.

### Verse 35

This is the law and course of the sun and its return. It makes its circuit and rises through the appointed gates in its recurring cycles. This is the great light called the sun, forever and ever.

### Verse 36

The one that rises in this way is the great light, named according to its appearance and according to the Lord's command.

### Verse 37

As it rises, so it sets. It does not diminish or rest, but travels by day and night. Its light is seven times brighter than the moon's, although the two are equal in size.
