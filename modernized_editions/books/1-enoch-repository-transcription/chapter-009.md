# 1 Enoch — Chapter 9
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 9.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 11 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Textual caution:** Interpolations identified by the source edition remain separately bracketed and labelled.

---

### Verse 1

Then Michael, Gabriel, Surjan, and Urjan looked down from heaven. They saw the great amount of blood that had been shed on the earth and all the injustice that had been committed there.

### Verse 2

They said to one another: “The earth, now emptied of its inhabitants, sends the sound of their cries up to heaven's gates.”

### Verse 3

“The souls of human beings are bringing their complaint to you, holy ones of heaven. They are saying: ‘Obtain justice for us from the Most High!’”

### Verse 4

They spoke to their Lord and King: “Lord of lords, God of gods, King of kings, your glorious throne endures through every generation and age. Your name is holy and glorious forever. Blessed and glorious are you!

### Verse 5

“You made all things, and you rule over everything. All things are exposed and open before you. You see everything, and nothing can hide from your sight.

### Verse 6

“Look at what Azazel has done. He has taught every kind of wrongdoing on the earth and revealed the hidden knowledge of the world that originated in heaven.

### Verse 7 — interpolation

[“Semjaza, to whom you gave authority to lead his companions, has taught them spells and enchantments.]

### Verse 8

“They went to the daughters of human beings, slept with them, defiled themselves, and revealed these forbidden practices to the women.

### Verse 9

“The women gave birth to giants, and through them the whole earth has been filled with bloodshed and injustice.

### Verse 10

“Now the spirits of those who have died are crying out and bringing their complaints to heaven's gate. Their laments are rising, and they cannot escape the injustice being committed on the earth.

### Verse 11

“You know everything before it happens. You knew this would happen and knew all that concerned them, yet you did not tell us. What, then, are we to do about it?”

**Editorial note:** Verse 7 is marked as an interpolation in the source and remains bracketed.

**1893 witness and apparatus notes:** Charles's note says verse 2 is not found in the Greek witness. His note on 9:1 describes “Surjan” and “Urjan” as variants of “Suriel” and “Uriel,” and says “Raphael” is probably to be read instead of “Surjan” in accordance with the Greek. The repository-based names are retained in the verse; these alternatives are not silently substituted.
