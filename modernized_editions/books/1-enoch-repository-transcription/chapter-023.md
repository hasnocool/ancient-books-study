# 1 Enoch — Chapter 23
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 23.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 4 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

From there I travelled westward to another place, toward the ends of the earth.

### Verse 2

I saw a burning fire that moved without resting. It never stopped its course, by day or by night, but continued steadily.

### Verse 3

I asked: “What is this thing that never rests?”

### Verse 4

Raguel, one of the holy angels who was with me, answered: “This burning fire in the west, whose course you have seen, is the course followed by all the heavenly lights.”
