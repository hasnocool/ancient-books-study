# 1 Enoch — Chapter 24
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 24.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 6 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

From there I travelled to another region of the earth, and he showed me a mountain range of fire that blazed day and night.

### Verse 2

I approached and saw seven magnificent mountains, each different from the others. They were covered with beautiful and splendid stones, glorious in appearance. Three stood toward the east, one rising upon another, and three stood toward the south, also one above another. Deep winding ravines lay between them, and none of the mountains joined another.

### Verse 3

The seventh mountain stood between them. All the mountains were elevated like seats of a throne, and the throne was surrounded by fragrant trees.

### Verse 4

Among them stood a tree unlike any I had ever smelled. No other tree among them—or anywhere else—was like it. Its fragrance surpassed every other fragrance. Its leaves, blossoms, and wood would never wither, and its fruit was beautiful, resembling dates from a palm tree.

### Verse 5

I said: “Look at this beautiful tree! It is lovely to see. Its leaves are beautiful, and its fruit is delightful to look upon.”

### Verse 6

Then Michael, one of the holy and honoured angels who was with me and was appointed over this place, answered me.
