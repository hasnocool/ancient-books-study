# 1 Enoch — Chapter 5
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 5.

**Editorial method:** Complete modernization of the chapter's 9 numbered verses. The wording is updated for readability while retaining the source's sequence and substance. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

I observed how the trees cover themselves with green leaves and produce fruit. So pay attention to everything and recognize that the Ever-Living One made all these things for you.

### Verse 2

His works remain before him year after year. All his works serve him and do not change; everything happens as God has appointed.

### Verse 3

Look at how the seas and rivers carry out their appointed work together.

### Verse 4

But you have not remained faithful. You have failed to keep the Lord's law and have broken it. With unclean mouths you have spoken proud and harsh words against his greatness. You are stubborn-hearted, and you will find no peace!

### Verse 5

Because of this, your days will become a curse, and you will lose the years of your life. The years of your destruction will be multiplied under an everlasting curse, and you will find no mercy.

### Verse 6

In those days, your names will become an everlasting curse among the righteous. They will continually curse you as sinners, together with all other sinners.

### Verse 7

But the chosen will receive light, joy, and peace, and they will inherit the earth. As for you ungodly people, a curse will rest upon you.

### Verse 8

Wisdom will be given to the chosen. They will all live and will never again sin, whether through carelessness or pride. Those who are wise will be humble and will not fall back into sin.

### Verse 9

They will not be punished throughout their lives, nor will they die through plagues or outbursts of wrath. They will live out their full span of days and grow old in peace. Their years of joy will be many, filled with lasting happiness and peace throughout their lives.
