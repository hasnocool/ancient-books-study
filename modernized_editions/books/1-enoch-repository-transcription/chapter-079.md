# 1 Enoch — Chapter 79
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-03-luminaries-chapters-72-82.md`, Chapter 79.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 6 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Historical-science note:** Ancient astronomical descriptions and numerical models are preserved as part of the source text and are not presented as current scientific fact.



---

### Verse 1

Now, my son, I have shown you everything, and the laws governing all the stars of heaven have been completed.

### Verse 2

He showed me all their laws for every day, every season of authority, every year, their rising, and the rules for each month and each week.

### Verse 3

He showed me the moon's waning through the sixth gate. In that sixth gate its light comes to an end, and afterward the new month begins.

### Verse 4

He also showed me the waning that takes place through the first gate during its appointed period, lasting 177 days. This is counted as twenty-five weeks and two days.

### Verse 5

The moon falls behind the sun and, according to the order of the stars, does so by exactly five days during one period. This happens after it has passed through the place you saw.

### Verse 6

This is the diagram and account of every heavenly light, as the great angel Uriel, their leader, showed them to me.
