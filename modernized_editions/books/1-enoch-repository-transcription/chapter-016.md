# 1 Enoch — Chapter 16
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 16.

**Editorial method:** Complete modernization of the chapter's 3 numbered units, preserving source wording's sequence and content while updating archaic syntax. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

“This is the time of murder, destruction, and the death of the giants. Their spirits leave the bodies of their flesh and go out to destroy without receiving judgment themselves. They will continue destroying until the great completion of the world arrives and judgment is carried out against the Watchers and the godless.

### Verse 2

“As for the Watchers who sent you to plead on their behalf—the ones who were once in heaven—tell them this: You were in heaven, and although the hidden things had not yet been fully revealed to you, you knew worthless mysteries. Because your hearts were hard, you told these mysteries to the women. Through these secrets, women and men have committed great evil on the earth.

### Verse 4

“So tell them: You will have no peace.”

**Editorial note:** The source numbering moves directly from verse 2 to verse 4. Verse 3 is absent from the printed sequence and has not been invented or renumbered.

---

## Completeness and editorial notes

- All **3 numbered units** present in the repository transcription have been represented.
- **1893 wording correction, 16:2:** Charles's printed translation reads “you knew worthless mysteries,” not “you learned forbidden mysteries.” The modernization now follows the printed wording more closely.
- Printed numbering irregularities are retained; no missing verse has been invented.
- This rendering follows the repository's chapter divisions and does not silently substitute another translation's wording or arrangement.
