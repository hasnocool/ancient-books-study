# 1 Enoch — Chapter 76
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-03-luminaries-chapters-72-82.md`, Chapter 76.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 14 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Historical-science note:** Ancient astronomical descriptions and numerical models are preserved as part of the source text and are not presented as current scientific fact.



---

### Verse 1

At the edges of the earth I saw twelve gates opened for all the winds. The winds come out through them and blow across the earth.

### Verse 2

Three gates are on the eastern side of heaven, three on the western side, three on the right side, and three on the left.

### Verse 3

The first three are toward the east, the next three toward the north, the three on the left are toward the south, and the final three are toward the west.

### Verse 4

Four of these gates release beneficial winds. The other eight release harmful winds. When those harmful winds are sent out, they bring destruction upon the earth, its waters, its inhabitants, and everything living in the water or on the land.

### Verse 5

The first wind, called the east wind, comes through the first eastern gate, which is angled toward the south. It brings destruction, drought, heat, and rain.

### Verse 6

Through the second, middle gate comes a favourable wind, bringing rain, fertility, prosperity, and dew. Through the third gate, toward the north, come cold and drought.

### Verse 7

Next come the south winds through three gates. Through the first gate, angled toward the east, comes a hot wind.

### Verse 8

Through the middle gate beside it come fragrant scents, dew, rain, prosperity, and health.

### Verse 9

Through the third gate, toward the west, come dew, rain, locusts, and destruction.

### Verse 10

Next come the north winds. Through the seventh gate, toward the east, come dew, rain, locusts, and destruction.

### Verse 11

Through the middle gate come direct rain, dew, health, and prosperity. Through the third gate, toward the west, come clouds, frost, snow, rain, dew, and locusts.

### Verse 12

Next come the west winds. Through the first gate beside the north come dew, rain, frost, cold, snow, and ice.

### Verse 13

Through the middle gate come dew, rain, prosperity, and blessing. Through the final gate beside the south come drought, destruction, burning heat, and death.

### Verse 14

These are the twelve gates of the four regions of heaven. I have shown you, my son Methuselah, all their laws, their harmful effects, and their benefits.
