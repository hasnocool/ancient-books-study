# 1 Enoch — Chapter 100
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-05-laws-of-enoch-chapters-91-105.md`, Chapter 100.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 13 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

In those days, fathers and their sons will be struck down together. Brothers will kill one another until their blood runs like a river.

### Verse 2

A man will not hold back his hand from killing his sons, and his sons will not hold back either. A sinner will not hold back from killing his honoured brother. From dawn until sunset they will kill one another.

### Verse 3

Horses will wade through the blood of sinners up to their chests, and chariots will be submerged in it.

### Verse 4

In those days the angels will descend into hidden places and gather together all those who brought sin into the world. The Most High will rise on the day of judgment and carry out a great judgment against sinners.

### Verse 5

He will appoint holy angels to guard the righteous and holy as carefully as the pupil of an eye. They will protect them until all wickedness and sin have come to an end. Even if the righteous lie in death for a long time, they have nothing to fear.

### Verse 6

The wise among humanity will recognize the truth. The people of the earth will understand everything written in this book and realize that wealth cannot save anyone when their sins bring them down.

### Verse 7

How terrible it will be for you sinners when you torment the righteous during the time of great distress and burn them with fire. You will be repaid according to your deeds.

### Verse 8

How terrible it will be for you stubborn-hearted people who keep watch in order to plan wickedness. Terror will overtake you, and no one will come to your aid.

### Verse 9

How terrible it will be for you sinners! Because of the words you have spoken and the deeds your hands have carried out in defiance of what is right, you will burn in a blazing fire.

### Verse 10

Understand this: the angels will search the heavenly record—the sun, the moon, and the stars—for evidence concerning your sins, because you have carried out unjust judgments against the righteous on earth.

### Verse 11

He will call the clouds, mist, dew, and rain as witnesses against you. These will be withheld from falling upon you because of your sins.

### Verse 12

You may as well give gifts to the rain so that it will not be withheld, or offer gold and silver to the dew so that it will descend.

### Verse 13

When frost and snow come with their bitter cold, and the snow-bearing winds bring all their afflictions upon you, you will not be able to withstand them in those days.
