# 1 Enoch — Chapter 28
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 28.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 3 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

Then I travelled eastward into the middle of the mountain range in the wilderness. There I saw nothing but an open plain.

### Verse 2

Yet the plain was filled with trees of this kind, and water flowed down over it from above.

### Verse 3

It was clear that many streams flowed through the region, both toward the north and toward the west. Here, as everywhere, water and dew rose up.
