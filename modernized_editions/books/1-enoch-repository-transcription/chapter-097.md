# 1 Enoch — Chapter 97
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-05-laws-of-enoch-chapters-91-105.md`, Chapter 97.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 10 numbered units in the repository transcription. Archaic language is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.



---

### Verse 1

Have confidence, you righteous people. The sinners will be disgraced and perish when the day of injustice comes to its end.

### Verse 2

You will learn that the Most High is mindful of your destruction, while the angels of heaven rejoice over your destruction.

### Verse 3

What will you do, sinners? Where will you run on the day of judgment, when you hear the prayers of the righteous?

### Verse 4

Your outcome will not be like theirs. This testimony stands against you: you chose to associate yourselves with sinners.

### Verse 5

In those days the prayers of the righteous will reach the Lord, and the time of your judgment will overtake you.

### Verse 6

Every word of your injustice will be read aloud before the Great and Holy One. Shame will cover your faces, and every deed founded on injustice will be rejected.

### Verse 7

How terrible it will be for you sinners, whether on the open sea or on dry land. Your memory will be associated with evil.

### Verse 8

How terrible it will be for you who gain silver and gold through injustice, yet boast: “We have become wealthy. We own everything and have acquired whatever we wanted.”

### Verse 9

You say: “Let us do whatever we have planned. We have gathered silver, our storehouses are full, and our houses are stocked with abundant provisions.”

### Verse 10

Your lies will wash away like water. Your wealth will not remain with you; it will suddenly leave your possession. You acquired it all through injustice, and you will face severe condemnation.

## Completeness and editorial notes

- All **10 numbered verses** in the repository's Chapter 97 have been rendered individually.
- The chapter's sequence and major claims have been retained; no verse has been intentionally omitted or replaced with a chapter summary.
- Where the source contains historically situated or ethically troubling assertions, the rendering preserves the passage as text and does not present those assertions as modern factual guidance.
- This rendering follows the repository's chapter and verse divisions and does not silently substitute another translation's wording or arrangement.
