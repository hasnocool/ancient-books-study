# 1 Enoch — Chapter 17
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 17.

**Editorial method:** Complete modernization of the chapter's 8 numbered units, preserving source wording's sequence and content while updating archaic syntax. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

They took me to a place where there were beings that looked like blazing fire, although they could appear in human form whenever they wished.

### Verse 2

They led me to the place of the whirlwind and to a mountain whose summit reached up to heaven.

### Verse 3

I saw the places where the heavenly lights and thunder were located at the ends of the earth. In the depths I saw a fiery bow and arrows, a quiver, a fiery sword, and all the lightning.

### Verse 4

They took me to the water of life and to the western fire that receives the sun each time it sets.

### Verse 5

I came to a river of fire. Its flames flowed like water and emptied into the great sea in the west.

### Verse 6

I saw all the great rivers. Then I came to a place of deep darkness and went to the region where all living flesh wanders.

### Verse 7

I saw the mountains of winter darkness and the place from which the waters of the entire deep flow.

### Verse 8

I saw the mouths of all the rivers on earth and the opening of the deep.

## 1893 witness note

Charles's 1893 printing encloses Chapter XVII in square brackets as an interpolated passage. The repository's selected formatted base and this modernization do not mark the chapter as interpolated. The base text and chapter are retained, but the 1893 structural difference must remain visible to readers.
