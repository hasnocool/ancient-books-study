# 1 Enoch — Chapter 43
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 43.

**Editorial method:** Complete modernization of 4 numbered units, updating archaic wording while retaining the source sequence and substance. **Textual caution:** The source edition marks this chapter as an interpolation; the bracketed status is retained.

### Verse 1 — interpolation

[Again I saw lightning and the stars of heaven. I saw how God called every one of them by name, and they answered him.]

### Verse 2 — interpolation

[I saw how they were weighed in a righteous balance according to the amount of light they gave, the width of their paths, and the day they appeared. Their movements produced lightning. They travelled according to the number assigned by the angels and remained faithful to one another.]

### Verse 3 — interpolation

[I asked the angel who accompanied me and revealed hidden things: “What are these?”]

### Verse 4 — interpolation

[He answered: “The Lord of Spirits has shown you their meaning. They represent the names of the holy people who live on earth and believe in the name of the Lord of Spirits forever and ever.”]

**Editorial note:** The source edition marks the entire chapter as an interpolation. All four verses remain bracketed.
