# 1 Enoch — Chapter 47
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 47.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 4 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.



---

### Verse 1

In those days the prayers of the righteous and the blood of the righteous who had been killed will rise from the earth into the presence of the Lord of Spirits.

### Verse 2

In those days the holy ones who dwell above in heaven will join together with one voice. They will plead, intercede, praise, give thanks, and bless the name of the Lord of Spirits because the blood of the righteous has been shed. They will ask that the prayers of the righteous not be in vain before the Lord of Spirits, that justice be done for them, and that they not have to suffer forever.

### Verse 3

In those days I saw the Ancient of Days seated upon his glorious throne. The books of the living were opened before him, and all his heavenly host, surrounding him above and around, stood in his presence.

### Verse 4

The hearts of the holy ones were filled with joy because the time for justice had drawn near. The prayers of the righteous had been heard, and the Lord of Spirits was demanding an answer for the blood of the righteous.
