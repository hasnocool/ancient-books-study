# 1 Enoch — Chapter 81
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-03-luminaries-chapters-72-82.md`, Chapter 81.

**Editorial method:** Complete modernization of all 10 numbered units in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Textual caution:** The source edition marks the entire chapter as an interpolation; all verses remain bracketed.


---

### Verse 1

He said to me: “Enoch, examine the writing on the heavenly tablets. Read what is written there and pay attention to every detail.

### Verse 2

I examined everything on the heavenly tablets, read all that was written, and understood it. I also read the book containing the deeds of human beings and all the descendants of flesh who will live on the earth, even to the most distant generations.

### Verse 3

Immediately afterward I blessed the Lord, the King of the glory of the world, because he had made all the works of the world. I praised the Lord for his patience and blessed him because of humanity.

### Verse 4

Then I said: “Blessed is the person who dies in righteousness and goodness, whose name is not recorded in the book of injustice and against whom no day of judgment is found.”

### Verse 5

The seven holy ones brought me and placed me on the earth before the door of my house. They said: “Explain everything to your son Methuselah. Show all your children that no human being is righteous before the Lord, because he is their Creator.

### Verse 6

“We will leave you with your children for one year, until another command is given. During that time you must teach your children, write these things down for them, and bear witness to them. In the second year they will take you away from among them.

### Verse 7

“Be strong in your heart. The good will proclaim righteousness to the good. The righteous will rejoice together and congratulate one another.

### Verse 8

“Sinners will die with sinners, and those who abandon the right path will descend with those who have abandoned it.

### Verse 9

“Those who practise righteousness will also die because of the deeds of human beings, and they will be gathered together because of the actions of the godless.

### Verse 10

In those days they stopped speaking to me, and I returned to my people, blessing the Lord of the world.

**1893 critical-apparatus note:** Charles says Chapter LXXXI cannot belong to the Book of Celestial Physics and classifies it as an interpolation in his introduction and notes. Unlike Chapter 80, however, the 1893 printed translation does not enclose Chapter 81 in square brackets. This modernization therefore leaves the ten verses unbracketed and records Charles's source-critical classification separately.
