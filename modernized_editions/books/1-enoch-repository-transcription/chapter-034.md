# 1 Enoch — Chapter 34
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 34.

**Editorial method:** Complete modernization of the chapter's 3 numbered verses, updating archaic language while retaining its sequence and substance. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

From there I travelled north to the ends of the earth. There I saw a great and glorious wonder at the farthest edge of the whole earth.

### Verse 2

I saw three open gates in the heavens. From each gate came north winds. When one of these winds blew, it brought cold, hail, frost, snow, dew, and rain.

### Verse 3

One of the gates sent out a beneficial wind. But when the winds came through the other two gates, they blew violently and brought harm upon the earth.
