# 1 Enoch — Chapter 54
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 54.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 10 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Textual caution:** Source-marked interpolations remain separately bracketed and identified.

---

### Verse 1

I looked around and turned toward another part of the earth. There I saw a deep valley filled with burning fire.

### Verse 2

The kings and powerful people were brought there and placed inside this deep valley.

### Verse 3

Then I saw them making instruments for them: iron chains of immeasurable weight.

### Verse 4

I asked the angel of peace who was with me: “For whom are these chains and instruments being prepared?”

### Verse 5

He answered: “They are being prepared for the armies of Azazel. They will seize them and throw them into the abyss of complete condemnation. They will cover their jaws with rough stones, as the Lord of Spirits has commanded.

### Verse 6

“On that great day Michael, Gabriel, Rufael, and Fanuel will seize them and cast them into a blazing furnace. The Lord of Spirits will take vengeance upon them for their injustice, for submitting to Satan and leading the inhabitants of the earth astray.”

### Verse 7 — interpolation

[In those days punishment will come from the Lord of Spirits. All the reservoirs of water above the heavens will be opened, along with the springs beneath the heavens and below the earth.]

### Verse 8 — interpolation

[All the waters will join together: the water above the heavens is described as male, and the water beneath the earth as female.]

### Verse 9 — interpolation

[Everyone living on the earth will be destroyed, including those who live at the farthest edges of heaven.]

### Verse 10 — interpolation

[Through this destruction they will recognize the injustice they committed on the earth, and because of that injustice they will perish.]

**Editorial note:** Verses 7–10 are identified in the source as part of the Noachic interpolation and remain separately bracketed.
