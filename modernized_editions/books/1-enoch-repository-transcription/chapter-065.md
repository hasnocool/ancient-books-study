# 1 Enoch — Chapter 65
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 65.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 12 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Textual caution:** The source identifies this chapter as part of a larger Noachic interpolation. Its verses remain explicitly bracketed.

---

### Verse 1 — interpolation

[In those days Noah saw that the earth was sinking and that its destruction was near.]

### Verse 2 — interpolation

[He rose and went to the ends of the earth. There he called loudly to his grandfather Enoch, repeating three times with great urgency: “Listen to me! Listen to me! Listen to me!”]

### Verse 3 — interpolation

[I said to him: “Tell me what is happening on the earth. Why is the earth so exhausted and shaken? Will I perish along with it?”]

### Verse 4 — interpolation

[Then a great disturbance shook the earth. A voice came from heaven, and I fell face down.]

### Verse 5 — interpolation

[My grandfather Enoch came and stood beside me. He said: “Why have you called to me with such a loud and sorrowful cry?”]

### Verse 6 — interpolation

[“A command has gone out from the presence of the Lord concerning those who live on the earth: their end must come, because they have learned all the secrets of the angels, all the violence of the accusers, all their hidden powers, the power of sorcery and witchcraft, and the power of those who make molten images for the whole earth.]

### Verse 7 — interpolation

[“They have also learned how silver is produced from the dust of the earth and how soft metals originate in the earth.]

### Verse 8 — interpolation

[“Lead and tin do not come from the earth in the same way as the other metals. They come from a spring, where an angel stands guard, and that angel is an important one.”]

### Verse 9 — interpolation

[After this my grandfather took me by the hand, raised me up, and said: “Go. I have asked the Lord of Spirits about this disturbance on the earth.”]

### Verse 10 — interpolation

[He told me: “Because of their injustice, their judgment has been decided once and for all. It will no longer be reconsidered before me. They have studied the appointed months and learned through them that the earth and its inhabitants will be destroyed.]

### Verse 11 — interpolation

[“There will never be a place of refuge for them, because they revealed what was hidden and shared it with those who are condemned. But as for you, my son, the Lord of Spirits knows that you are pure and innocent of the charge concerning these mysteries.]

### Verse 12 — interpolation

[“He has appointed your name among the holy ones and will preserve you from those who live on the earth. He has appointed your descendants to righteousness, kingship, and great honour. From your descendants will come an uncountable multitude of righteous and holy people forever.”]

**Editorial note:** The source identifies Chapter 65 as part of the Noachic interpolation extending through Chapter 69, verse 25. All twelve verses remain bracketed.
