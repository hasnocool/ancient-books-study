# 1 Enoch — Chapter 105
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-05-laws-of-enoch-chapters-91-105.md`, Chapter 105.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 2 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

In those days, says the Lord, people will call upon the children of the earth and bear witness to them about wisdom. They will say: “Show wisdom to them, for you are their guide and a source of reward throughout the whole earth.”

### Verse 2

“For I and my Son will be united with them forever as they walk through life along the paths of righteousness. You will have peace. Rejoice, children of righteousness.”
