# 1 Enoch — Chapter 105
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-05-laws-of-enoch-chapters-91-105.md`, Chapter 105.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 2 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

In those days, says the Lord, people will call upon the children of the earth and bear witness to them about wisdom. They will say: “Show wisdom to them, for you are their guide and a source of reward throughout the whole earth.”

### Verse 2

“For I and my Son will be united with them forever as they walk through life along the paths of righteousness. You will have peace. Rejoice, children of righteousness.”

---

## Completeness and editorial notes

- All **2 numbered verses** in the repository's Chapter 105 have been rendered individually.
- No verse has been intentionally omitted or replaced by a chapter summary.
- **1917 textual-witness comparison:** The 1917 Charles/Oesterley text (printed p. 150) reads that the Lord “bade (them) to summon and testify to the children of earth concerning their wisdom,” and continues “ye are their guides.” The repository-based modernization instead says “people will call upon the children of the earth” and addresses the children as “you are their guide.” This is an agency/subject and number difference, not merely modernization of archaic grammar. In 105:2, the 1917 syntax differs and uses “uprightness” where the repository rendering uses “righteousness.” The repository wording is retained here; the 1917 alternatives are recorded rather than silently substituted. The separate authentic 1917 Charles/Oesterley facsimile, printed p. 150, was rendered and visually inspected. It confirms the 1917 wording. The repository-bundled 101-page PDF is a 2005 typeset transcription; its PDF page 99 was also rendered, but that is not a historical page image. The authentic facsimile page check is the evidence for this witness.
- **Ethiopic manuscript cross-reference:** The [Theosis Library presentation of 1 Enoch 91–108](https://theosislibrary.com/library/verified-enoch-ethiopic-epistle) places the Charles 1917 English text alongside Dillmann's 1851 Ge'ez text and photographs of Rylands Ethiopic MS 23, with folio/column navigation for this passage. This is a secondary digital cross-reference, not an independent transcription or a substitute for direct manuscript examination.
- This rendering follows the repository's chapter and verse divisions and does not silently substitute another translation's wording or arrangement.
