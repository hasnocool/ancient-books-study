# 1 Enoch — Chapter 33
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 33.

**Editorial method:** Complete modernization of the chapter's 4 numbered verses, updating archaic language while retaining its sequence and substance. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

From there I travelled to the ends of the earth. I saw great animals there, each different from the others. I also saw birds that differed in appearance, beauty, and voice. Every kind was distinct.

### Verse 2

East of these animals I saw the edge of the earth, where the heavens rest, and the gates of heaven stood open.

### Verse 3

I saw the stars of heaven coming out. I counted the gates through which they emerged and recorded every route, listing each star individually according to its number, name, connections, position, appointed time, and month. The holy angel Uriel, who was with me, showed me these things.

### Verse 4

He showed me everything and wrote it down for me. He also recorded their names, their laws, and their appointed groups.
