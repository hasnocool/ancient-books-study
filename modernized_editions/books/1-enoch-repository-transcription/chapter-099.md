# 1 Enoch — Chapter 99
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-05-laws-of-enoch-chapters-91-105.md`, Chapter 99.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 16 numbered units in the repository transcription. Archaic language is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.



---

### Verse 1

How terrible it will be for those who act without reverence, boast in lying words, and praise falsehood. You will perish, and you will have no life of lasting happiness.

### Verse 2

How terrible it will be for those who twist the words of righteousness, violate the eternal law, and turn themselves into what they were not—making themselves sinners. They will be trampled underfoot on the earth.

### Verse 3

In those days, you righteous people, prepare your prayers as a lasting record. Present them as testimony before the angels, so that the angels may place the sins of sinners on record before the Most High.

### Verse 4

In those days the nations will be stirred up, and the peoples of the nations will rise on the day of destruction.

### Verse 5

In those days, people driven into desperation will go out and harm their children or abandon them. Miscarriages will occur; infants will be cast aside and left without care. People will show no compassion even toward those they love.

### Verse 6

I declare again to you sinners: a day of unending bloodshed is prepared as the consequence of sin.

### Verse 7

Some will worship stones. Others will make carved images from gold, silver, wood, and clay. Still others will worship impure spirits, demons, and every kind of superstition that has no foundation in knowledge. None of these things will be able to help them.

### Verse 8

Their foolish hearts will lead them away from God. Fear will blind their eyes, along with the visions they experience in dreams.

### Verse 9

Through these things they will become irreverent and afraid. They will conduct all their affairs through falsehood and worship a stone; therefore they will perish in an instant.

### Verse 10

But in those days, blessed are all who accept the words of wisdom, understand them, follow the paths of the Most High, and walk in the way of righteousness. They must not join the godless in their irreverence, for they will be saved.

### Verse 11

How terrible it will be for you who wish misfortune upon your neighbours. You will be destroyed in Sheol.

### Verse 12

How terrible it will be for you who use dishonest and false measures and deceive people on the earth. Because of these practices you will be utterly consumed.

### Verse 13

How terrible it will be for you who build your houses through the exhausting labour of others, using nothing but the bricks and stones of sin as your building materials. I tell you: you will have no peace.

### Verse 14

How terrible it will be for those who reject the inheritance and established measure handed down by their ancestors, and whose souls pursue idols. They will find no rest.

### Verse 15

How terrible it will be for those who practise injustice, support oppression, and murder their neighbours until the day of the great judgment.

### Verse 16

God will bring down your glory, fill your hearts with distress, and unleash the force of divine anger to destroy you all by the sword. All the righteous and holy will remember your sins.

## Completeness and editorial notes

- All **16 numbered verses** in the repository's Chapter 99 have been rendered individually.
- The chapter's sequence and major claims have been retained; no verse has been intentionally omitted or replaced with a chapter summary.
- Where the source contains historically situated or ethically troubling assertions, the rendering preserves the passage as text and does not present those assertions as modern factual guidance.
- This rendering follows the repository's chapter and verse divisions and does not silently substitute another translation's wording or arrangement.
