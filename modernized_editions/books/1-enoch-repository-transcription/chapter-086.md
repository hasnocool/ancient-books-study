# 1 Enoch — Chapter 86
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-04-visions-chapters-83-90.md`, Chapter 86.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 6 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

Again, while I slept, I looked with my eyes and saw the heavens above. A star fell from heaven—the Watchers—and it rose up and began feeding among the oxen.

### Verse 2

After this I saw large black oxen. They all changed their stalls and pastures, along with their herds, and began living together.

### Verse 3

Again I looked toward heaven in the vision. I saw many stars descend and throw themselves down from heaven toward the first star. They became bulls among the cattle and grazed with them.

### Verse 4

I watched them. They exposed their sexual organs like horses and began mounting the cows of the oxen. All the cows became pregnant and gave birth to elephants, camels, and donkeys.

### Verse 5

All the oxen were afraid and terrified of them. They began biting with their teeth, devouring, and goring with their horns.

### Verse 6

Then they began eating the oxen. All the children of the earth trembled and shook before them and fled.
