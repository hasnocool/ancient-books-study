# 1 Enoch — Chapter 46
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 46.

**Editorial method:** Complete modernization of 8 numbered units, updating archaic wording while retaining the source sequence and substance. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

### Verse 1

There I saw someone whose head was ancient, and whose hair was white like wool.

### Verse 2

With him was another being whose appearance was human. His face was full of kindness, like the face of one of the holy angels.

### Verse 3

I asked the angel who accompanied me and revealed all the hidden things about the Son of Man: “Who is he? Where does he come from? And why is he with the Ancient of Days?”

### Verse 4

He answered: “This is the Son of Man, who embodies righteousness and with whom righteousness dwells. He reveals all the treasures of what is hidden. The Lord of Spirits has chosen him because his portion before the Lord of Spirits surpasses everything in righteousness forever.

### Verse 5

“ This Son of Man whom you have seen will awaken kings and powerful people from their couches and the mighty from their thrones. He will break the power of the strong and crush the teeth of sinners.

### Verse 6

“He will remove kings from their thrones and take away their kingdoms, because they did not honour or praise him and did not give thanks or acknowledge who granted them their authority. He will lower the faces of the powerful, and shame will cover them. Darkness will be their dwelling and worms their bed. They will have no hope of rising from their beds, because they did not honour the name of the Lord of Spirits.

### Verse 7

“These are the people who claim authority over the stars of heaven, raise their hands against the Most High, trample the earth, and live upon it. Everything they do is unjust. Their power rests on their wealth, and they trust in gods they have made with their own hands. They have rejected the name of the Lord of Spirits.

### Verse 8

“They will be driven out from the houses where the Lord's people assemble and from the community of the faithful who remain devoted to the name of the Lord of Spirits.”
