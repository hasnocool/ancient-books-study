# 1 Enoch — Chapter 1
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 1.

**Editorial method:** Complete modernization of the chapter's 9 numbered verses. The wording is updated for readability while retaining the source's sequence and substance. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

These are the words of blessing with which Enoch blessed the chosen and righteous—the people who will be alive during the time of suffering, when all the wicked and godless are removed.

### Verse 2

Enoch answered and said: “I am Enoch, a righteous man whose eyes God has opened so that I may see a vision of the Holy One in heaven. The angels showed me these things, and I heard everything from them. I understood what I saw, but this message is not for the present generation. It is for generations far in the future.

### Verse 3

“I spoke about the chosen people and told a parable concerning them: The Holy and Great One will come out from his dwelling, the God of the world.

### Verse 4

“From there he will set foot on Mount Sinai. He will appear with his hosts and reveal the strength of his power from heaven.

### Verse 5

“Everyone will be struck with fear. The Watchers will tremble, and great terror and shaking will seize them, reaching to the ends of the earth.

### Verse 6

“The high mountains will shake, the lofty hills will be brought low, and they will melt like wax before a flame.

### Verse 7

“The earth will split open, and everything upon it will perish. Judgment will come upon everything, including all the righteous.

### Verse 8

“But he will give peace to the righteous and protect the chosen. His grace will rest upon them. They will all belong to God; it will go well with them, they will be blessed, and God's light will shine upon them.

### Verse 9

“Look! He comes with tens of thousands of his holy ones to carry out judgment against them. He will destroy the ungodly and expose every deed that sinners and the wicked have committed against him.”
