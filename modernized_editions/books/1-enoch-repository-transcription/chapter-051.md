# 1 Enoch — Chapter 51
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 51.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 5 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.



---

### Verse 1

In those days the earth will give back those entrusted to it, and Sheol will return everything it has received. The realm of the dead will give back what it owes.

### Verse 2

God will choose the righteous and the holy from among them, because the day of their redemption has drawn near.

### Verse 3

In those days the Chosen One will sit upon my throne, and every mystery of wisdom will flow from the words of his mouth. The Lord of Spirits has given him this wisdom and honoured him.

### Verse 4

In those days the mountains will leap like rams, and the hills will jump like lambs that have had their fill of milk. They will all become like angels in heaven.

### Verse 5

Their faces will shine with joy, because in those days the Chosen One will have appeared. The earth will rejoice, the righteous will live upon it, and the chosen will move freely across it.
