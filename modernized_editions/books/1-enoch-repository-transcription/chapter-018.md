# 1 Enoch — Chapter 18
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 18.

**Editorial method:** Complete modernization of the chapter's 16 numbered units, preserving source wording's sequence and content while updating archaic syntax. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

I saw the chambers from which all the winds come. I saw how God had equipped the whole creation with them and established the firm foundations of the earth.

### Verse 2

I saw the cornerstone of the earth. I saw the four winds that support the earth and the firm structure of heaven.

### Verse 3

I saw how the winds stretch out the vaults of heaven and remain stationed between heaven and earth. These are the pillars of heaven.

### Verse 4

I saw the winds that turn the heavens and carry the sun and all the stars along their courses toward their settings.

### Verse 5

I saw the winds on earth that carry the clouds. I saw the paths of the angels, and at the edge of the earth I saw the firmament above.

### Verse 6

I travelled toward the south. There was a place that burned day and night, where seven magnificent mountains made of precious stones stood—three toward the east and three toward the south.

### Verse 7

Of those toward the east, one was made of coloured stone, one of pearls, and one of antimony. The mountains toward the south were made of red stone.

### Verse 8

The middle mountain rose toward heaven like the throne of God. It was made of alabaster, and the top of the throne was sapphire.

### Verse 9

I saw a blazing fire burning throughout all the mountains.

### Verse 10

I saw a place there, facing the great earth, where the heavens came together.

### Verse 11

I saw a deep abyss with pillars of heavenly fire. Among them I saw countless pillars of heavenly fire falling, extending beyond number both upward and downward.

### Verse 12

Above that abyss I saw a place with no heavenly firmament above it and no earthly foundation beneath it. There was no water and no birds there; it was an empty wasteland.

### Verse 13

What I saw there was terrifying: seven stars, like enormous burning mountains and like spirits, were pleading with me.

### Verse 14

The angel said: “This is where heaven and earth come to an end. It serves as a prison for the stars of heaven and the heavenly host.

### Verse 15

“Those stars that move through the fire are the ones that disobeyed God's command before their rising, because they did not appear at the time appointed for them.

### Verse 16

“God was angry with them and bound them until the time when their guilt would reach its full measure, in the year of the mystery.”
