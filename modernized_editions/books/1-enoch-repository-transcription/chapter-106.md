# 1 Enoch — Chapter 106
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-06-deluge-chapters-106-108.md`, Chapter 106.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 19 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Order note:** The repository keeps verse 17 after verse 16. Charles's 1917 text prints the giants clause immediately after 106:14 and before 106:15–16; his introduction explicitly says 106:17a should be read immediately after 106:14. The repository sequence is retained, and the witness order is documented below rather than silently applied.

---

### Verse 1

Some days later, my son Methuselah arranged a wife for his son Lamech, and she became pregnant and gave birth to a son.

### Verse 2

His body was white as snow and red as a blooming rose. The hair on his head and his long locks were white like wool, and his eyes were beautiful. When he opened his eyes, the whole house lit up like the sun and filled with light.

### Verse 3

When he was taken from the midwife's hands, he opened his mouth and conversed with the Lord of righteousness.

### Verse 4

His father Lamech was frightened by him. He ran away and went to his own father, Methuselah.

### Verse 5

Lamech said to him: “I have fathered an extraordinary child. He does not look like an ordinary human being; he resembles the children of the angels of heaven. His nature is unlike ours. His eyes shine like the rays of the sun, and his face is glorious.

### Verse 6

“I fear that he may not have come from me at all, but from the angels. I am afraid that something extraordinary may happen on the earth during his lifetime.

### Verse 7

“Father, I ask and beg you to go to our ancestor Enoch and learn the truth from him, for he lives among the angels.”

### Verse 8

When Methuselah heard his son's words, he came to me at the ends of the earth, because he had heard that I was there. He called out loudly. I heard his voice, went to him, and said: “Here I am, my son, for you have come to me.”

### Verse 9

He answered: “I have come to you because I am deeply troubled. I have approached you because of a disturbing vision and a matter causing great anxiety.

### Verse 10

“Listen to me, father. My son Lamech has had a son whose appearance and nature are unlike those of ordinary human beings. His body is whiter than snow and redder than a blooming rose. The hair on his head is whiter than wool, and his eyes shine like the rays of the sun. When he opened his eyes, the whole house was filled with light.

### Verse 11

“When the midwife lifted him from her hands, he opened his mouth and blessed the Lord of heaven.

### Verse 12

“Lamech was frightened and ran to me. He could not believe that the child had come from him; he thought the child resembled the angels of heaven. I have therefore come to you so that you can reveal the truth to me.”

### Verse 13

I, Enoch, answered: “The Lord is about to do something new on the earth. I have already seen it in a vision, and I will explain it to you. In the generation of my father Jared, some beings descended from the heights of heaven and disobeyed the Lord's command.

### Verse 14

“They sinned and broke the law. They joined themselves to women, committed sin with them, took wives, and fathered children.

### Verse 15

“A great catastrophe will come upon the earth: a flood and widespread destruction lasting for a year.

### Verse 16

“But this child who has been born to you will remain on the earth. He and his three children will be saved when all the other human beings living on the earth perish.

### Verse 17

“The giants are not spiritual beings; they belong to the physical world. A great punishment will come upon the earth, and the earth will be cleansed of all impurity.

### Verse 18

“Tell your son Lamech that the child truly is his son, and name him Noah, for he will remain with you. He and his children will be saved from the destruction that is coming upon the earth because of all the sin and wrongdoing that will reach its fullness during his days.

### Verse 19

“After that, even greater wickedness will arise on the earth than the wickedness that came before. I know the mysteries of the holy ones, because the Lord has shown and explained them to me, and I have read them in the heavenly tablets.”

## Completeness and editorial notes

- All **19 numbered verses** in the repository's Chapter 106 have been rendered individually.
- No verse has been intentionally omitted or replaced by a chapter summary.
- **1917 witness reading and order:** In the 1917 printed text, the clause following 106:14 reads that the angels “shall produce on the earth giants not according to the spirit, but according to the flesh,” followed by the great punishment and cleansing of the earth. Charles identifies this as 106:17a and directs that it be read immediately after 106:14. The repository transcription instead phrases the clause as “The giants are not spiritual beings; they belong to the physical world” and places it after 106:16. Both the wording/agency and sequence differ. This modernization preserves the selected repository wording and order; it does not silently replace them with the 1917 witness.
- The separate authentic 1917 facsimile printed p. 151 was rendered and visually inspected; it contains 106:1–14. The following printed p. 152, needed to verify the remaining sequence, could not be retrieved as an image in this pass. The repository-bundled 2005 typeset transcription's PDF pp. 100–101 were also rendered and show the full Chapter 106 text, including 106:17a after 106:14, but those pages are not historical facsimile images. Accordingly, visual verification of Chapter 106 against the authentic 1917 scan remains incomplete at p. 152.
- This rendering follows the repository's chapter and verse divisions and preserves its chapter sequence; it does not silently substitute another translation's wording or arrangement.
