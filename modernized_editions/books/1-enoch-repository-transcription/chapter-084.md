# 1 Enoch — Chapter 84
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-04-visions-chapters-83-90.md`, Chapter 84.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 6 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

I raised my hands in righteousness and blessed the Holy and Great One. With my breath and human tongue—the tongue God made for people to speak—I prayed. He gave human beings breath, a tongue, and a mouth so they could speak.

### Verse 2

“Blessed are you, Lord, great and mighty King, Lord of all creation in heaven, King of kings, and God of the whole world. Your power, kingdom, and greatness endure forever and ever. Your rule continues through every generation. All the heavens are your throne forever, and the whole earth is your footstool forever and ever.

### Verse 3

“You created and govern everything. You established all things firmly, and no wisdom escapes you. Wisdom never leaves your throne or your presence. You know, see, and hear everything; nothing is hidden from you, because you see all things.

### Verse 4

“Now the angels of heaven have transgressed, and your anger rests upon human flesh until the day of the great judgment.

### Verse 5

“Now, God, Lord, and Great King, I plead with you and pray that you will answer my request. Leave descendants for me on the earth. Do not destroy all human beings or leave the earth without inhabitants, so that it suffers everlasting destruction.

### Verse 6

“My Lord, destroy from the earth the corrupt flesh that has provoked your anger. But establish the righteous and upright as a plant whose seed will endure forever. Do not hide your face from the prayer of your servant, O Lord.”
