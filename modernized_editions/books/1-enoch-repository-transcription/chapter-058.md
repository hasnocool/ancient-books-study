# 1 Enoch — Chapter 58
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 58.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 6 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.



---

### Verse 1

I began to speak the third parable, concerning the righteous and the chosen.

### Verse 2

“Blessed are you, righteous and chosen people, for a glorious destiny is prepared for you!

### Verse 3

“The righteous will live in the light of the sun, and the chosen in the light of eternal life. Their lives will have no end, and the days of the holy will be beyond counting.

### Verse 4

“They will seek the light and find righteousness with the Lord of Spirits. In the name of the Lord of the world, peace will belong to the righteous.

### Verse 5

“After this, the holy will be told to seek in heaven the mysteries of righteousness and the inheritance of faith. This light has become as bright as the sun upon the earth, and darkness has passed away.

### Verse 6

“There will be unending light. Their days will no longer be counted, because the former darkness will be destroyed. Light will be established in the presence of the Lord of Spirits, and the light of righteousness will remain before him forever.”
