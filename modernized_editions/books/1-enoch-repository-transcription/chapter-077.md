# 1 Enoch — Chapter 77
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-03-luminaries-chapters-72-82.md`, Chapter 77.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 8 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Historical-science note:** Ancient astronomical descriptions and numerical models are preserved as part of the source text and are not presented as current scientific fact.



---

### Verse 1

The first region is called the east because it comes first. The second is the south, because the Most High descends there, and in a special way the One who is blessed forever comes down there.

### Verse 2

The west is called the declining region, because all the heavenly lights decline and set there.

### Verse 3

The fourth region, the north, is divided into three parts. The first is where human beings live. The second contains seas, valleys, forests, rivers, darkness, and clouds. The third part contains the Garden of Righteousness.

### Verse 4

I saw seven high mountains, taller than all the other mountains on earth. From there come frost, and the passing of days, seasons, and years.

### Verse 5

I saw seven rivers on earth, larger than all the others. One flows from the west and empties into the Great Sea.

### Verse 6

Two rivers come from the north and flow into the sea, emptying into the Erythraean Sea in the east.

### Verse 7

The remaining four flow from the northern region toward their own sea, the Erythraean Sea. Two of them also flow into the Great Sea there.

### Verse 8

I saw seven great islands in the sea and on the mainland: two on the mainland and five in the Great Sea.
