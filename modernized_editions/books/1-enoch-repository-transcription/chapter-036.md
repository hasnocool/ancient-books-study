# 1 Enoch — Chapter 36
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 36.

**Editorial method:** Complete modernization of the chapter's 4 numbered verses, updating archaic language while retaining its sequence and substance. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

From there I travelled south to the ends of the earth. I saw three open gates in heaven, from which came the south wind, dew, rain, and wind.

### Verse 2

From there I travelled eastward to the farthest edge of heaven. I saw the three eastern gates of heaven open, with smaller gates above them.

### Verse 3

The stars of heaven passed through each of these smaller gates and travelled westward along the paths appointed for them.

### Verse 4

When I saw these things, I blessed the Lord of Glory. I blessed him each time, because he had made such great and glorious wonders. He revealed the greatness of his works to the angels and to human souls, so that they might praise his work and all his creation, see the power of his might, praise the great work of his hands, and bless him forever.
