# 1 Enoch — Chapter 37
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 37.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 5 numbered units in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.



---

### Verse 1

This is the second vision of wisdom, which I, Enoch, son of Jared, son of Mahalalel, son of Cainan, son of Enos, son of Seth, son of Adam, saw.

### Verse 2

These are the opening words of the wisdom teachings that I raised my voice to proclaim and explain to those who live on the earth. Listen, people of earlier times, and pay attention, you who come after them, to the holy words I will speak before the Lord of Spirits.

### Verse 3

It would have been better to declare these things to the people of ancient times, but I will not withhold the beginning of wisdom from those who come later.

### Verse 4

Until now, the Lord of Spirits has never given anyone wisdom like the wisdom I have received. It has been given according to my understanding and according to the good pleasure of the Lord of Spirits, who has granted me a share in eternal life.

### Verse 5

Three parables were entrusted to me. I raised my voice and proclaimed them to those who live on the earth.

**The First Parable begins.**
