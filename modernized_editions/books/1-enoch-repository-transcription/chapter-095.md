# 1 Enoch — Chapter 95
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-05-laws-of-enoch-chapters-91-105.md`, Chapter 95.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 7 numbered units in the repository transcription. Archaic language is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.



---

### Verse 1

If only my eyes were a cloud of water, so I could weep for you and shed my tears like a cloud of water! Perhaps then I could find rest from my troubled heart.

### Verse 2

Who gave you permission to practise hatred and wickedness? May judgment fall upon you, sinners!

### Verse 3

Do not be afraid, you righteous people. The Lord will once again place the sinners in your hands, so that you may carry out judgment against them according to your wishes.

### Verse 4

How terrible it will be for you who pronounce unforgiving curses. Because of your sins, healing will remain far from you.

### Verse 5

How terrible it will be for you who repay your neighbours with evil. You will receive treatment in keeping with your own actions.

### Verse 6

How terrible it will be for you false witnesses and for those who make injustice their measure. You will perish suddenly.

### Verse 7

How terrible it will be for you sinners who persecute the righteous. You will yourselves be handed over and persecuted, you who practise injustice; a crushing burden will be placed upon you.

## Completeness and editorial notes

- All **7 numbered verses** in the repository's Chapter 95 have been rendered individually.
- The chapter's sequence and major claims have been retained; no verse has been intentionally omitted or replaced with a chapter summary.
- Where the source contains historically situated or ethically troubling assertions, the rendering preserves the passage as text and does not present those assertions as modern factual guidance.
- This rendering follows the repository's chapter and verse divisions and does not silently substitute another translation's wording or arrangement.
