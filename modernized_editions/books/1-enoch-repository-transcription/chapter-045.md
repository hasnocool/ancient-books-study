# 1 Enoch — Chapter 45
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 45.

**Editorial method:** Complete modernization of 6 numbered units, updating archaic wording while retaining the source sequence and substance. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.



---

### Verse 1

This is the second parable, concerning those who reject the name of the dwelling place of the holy ones and the name of the Lord of Spirits.

### Verse 2

They will not ascend into heaven, nor will they find a place on earth. This will be the fate of sinners who reject the name of the Lord of Spirits. They are being kept for the day of suffering and distress.

### Verse 3

“On that day my Chosen One will sit upon the throne of glory and judge their deeds. His dwelling places will be beyond counting. Their spirits will grow strong when they see my chosen people and those who have called upon my glorious name.

### Verse 4

“On that day I will cause my Chosen One to live among them. I will transform the heavens and make them an everlasting blessing and light.

### Verse 5

“I will transform the earth and make it a blessing. I will cause my chosen people to live upon it, but sinners and evildoers will never set foot there.

### Verse 6

“I have seen my righteous people and have given them peace. I have caused them to dwell before me. But judgment is waiting for sinners, so that I may remove them from the face of the earth.”
