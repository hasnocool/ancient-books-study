# 1 Enoch — Chapter 82
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-03-luminaries-chapters-72-82.md`, Chapter 82.

**Editorial method:** Complete modernization of all 20 numbered units in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.


**Textual caution:** The source numbering skips verse 13 and contains two successive verses numbered 14. Both occurrences are preserved, and no missing verse is invented.

---

### Verse 1

Now, my son Methuselah, I am recounting and writing down all these things. I have revealed everything to you and given you books concerning them. My son, preserve the books written by your father and pass them on to the generations of the world.

### Verse 2

I have given wisdom to you, to your son, and to the children who are yet to come, so that they may pass it on to their own children, generation after generation forever. This is wisdom beyond ordinary human thought.

### Verse 3

Those who understand it will not sleep. They will listen attentively so they can learn this wisdom, and it will please them more than good food.

### Verse 4

Blessed are all the righteous, and blessed are all who walk in the way of righteousness and do not sin as sinners do. This is true throughout all the days counted as the sun travels across heaven, entering and leaving its gates for thirty days at a time, together with the leaders of the thousands of stars and the four additional days divided among the four parts of the year.

### Verse 5

Because of these additional days, people make mistakes and fail to include them in the calculation of the whole course of the world. They make errors and do not recognize the calendar accurately.

### Verse 6

These days belong to the calculation of the year and are permanently recorded: one is associated with the first gate, one with the third, one with the fourth, and one with the sixth. The year is completed in 364 days.

### Verse 7

The account is accurate and the recorded calculation is exact. Uriel has shown and revealed to me the heavenly lights, months, festivals, years, and days. The Lord of all creation has appointed him over the host of heaven.

### Verse 8

He has authority over night and day in heaven, to make the sun, moon, stars, and all the heavenly powers travelling in their circular chariots give light to humanity.

### Verse 9

These are the appointed orders of the stars: they set in their places and at their proper seasons, festivals, and months.

### Verse 10

These are the names of the leaders who guide them and ensure that they enter at their appointed seasons. They direct them in their places, orders, times, months, periods of authority, and positions.

### Verse 11

The four leaders who divide the four parts of the year enter first. After them come the twelve leaders who divide the months. For the 360 days there are heads over thousands who divide the days, and for the four additional days there are leaders who separate the four parts of the year.

### Verse 12

One of the heads over thousands is added between each leader, behind the appointed position, while their leaders carry out the division.

### Verse 13

These are the names of the leaders who divide the four appointed parts of the year: Melkeel, Helemmelek, Melejal, and Narel.

### Verse 14 — second occurrence in source numbering

These are the names of those who serve under them: Adnarel, Ijasusael, and Ijelumiel. These three follow the leaders of the orders, and another follows the three leaders of the orders who come after the leaders responsible for dividing the four parts of the year.

### Verse 15

At the beginning of the year, Melkeel rises first and takes authority. He is also called Tamaani, the sun. All the days of his period of rule amount to ninety-one days.

### Verse 16

These are the signs seen on earth during his period of rule: sweat, heat, and anxiety. All the trees bear fruit and produce leaves. Wheat is harvested, roses and all flowers bloom in the fields, while the trees of the winter season wither.

### Verse 17

These are the names of the leaders subordinate to them: Berkeel, Zalbesael, and another appointed as a head over a thousand, called Helojaseph. The days of his authority then come to an end.

### Verse 18

The next leader is Helemmelek, also called the shining sun. All the days of his light amount to ninety-one days.

### Verse 19

These are the signs seen on earth during his days: intense heat and dryness. The trees bring their fruit to ripeness, and all their produce matures. The sheep mate and become pregnant. The fruits of the earth are gathered in, along with everything in the fields and the winepress. These things take place during his period of authority.

### Verse 20

These are the names and appointed orders of the leaders over those thousands: Gedael, Keel, and Heel, together with the additional head over a thousand, Asfael. The days of their authority then come to an end.

**1893 numbering note:** The authentic 1893 printed text numbers the names of the four year-section leaders as verse 13, followed by verse 14 for the names of those who serve under them. The repository base had accidentally labelled the first of these passages verse 14, creating a duplicate. The modernization now restores the printed sequence 12, 13, 14, 15–20; no verse has been invented or omitted.
