# 1 Enoch — Chapter 75
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-03-luminaries-chapters-72-82.md`, Chapter 75.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 9 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Historical-science note:** Ancient astronomical descriptions and numerical models are preserved as part of the source text and are not presented as current scientific fact.

**Textual caution:** Verse 5 is marked as an interpolation in the source and remains bracketed.

---

### Verse 1

The leaders of the thousands, who are appointed over all creation and all the stars, also have responsibility for the four additional days. These days cannot be separated from their work in the calculation of the year. They serve on the four days that are not included in the ordinary count of the year.

### Verse 2

Because of these days, people sometimes make mistakes in their calculations. The heavenly lights serve the stations of the world: one at the first gate, one at the third, one at the fourth, and one at the sixth. The harmony of the world's course is maintained through its 364 appointed stations.

### Verse 3

The angel Uriel showed me the signs, seasons, years, and days. The eternal Lord of Glory appointed him over all the lights of heaven, in heaven and throughout the world, so that they might govern the heavens, be visible on earth, and guide both day and night. These include the sun, moon, stars, and all the beings that serve them as they travel in the chariots of heaven.

### Verse 4

In the same way, Uriel showed me twelve openings around the sun's chariot in heaven. The sun's rays emerge through them, spreading warmth over the earth whenever they are opened at their appointed seasons.

### Verse 5 — interpolation

[There are similar openings for the winds and for the spirit of dew. When they are opened, they stand open in the heavens at the edges.]

### Verse 6

I saw twelve gates in heaven at the edges of the earth. Through them the sun, moon, stars, and all the works of heaven emerge, both in the east and in the west.

### Verse 7

Many windows stand to the right and left of these gates. At its appointed time, one window produces warmth, corresponding to the gates through which the stars emerge and set, each according to the number and order God has commanded.

### Verse 8

I saw chariots in heaven moving across the world above these gates. The stars that never set travel in them.

### Verse 9

One of these stars is larger than all the others and travels throughout the entire world.

**Editorial note:** The source edition marks verse 5 as an interpolation; it remains bracketed.
