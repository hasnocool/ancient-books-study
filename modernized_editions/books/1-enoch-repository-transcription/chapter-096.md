# 1 Enoch — Chapter 96
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-05-laws-of-enoch-chapters-91-105.md`, Chapter 96.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 8 numbered units in the repository transcription. Archaic language is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Textual caution:** Verse 2 is marked as an interpolation in the source and remains separately bracketed.

---

### Verse 1

Take courage, you righteous people. The sinners will suddenly perish before you, and you will gain authority over them, as you have longed for.

### Verse 2 — interpolation

[On the day when sinners suffer, your children will rise and soar like eagles. Your nest will be higher than that of vultures. You will climb like squirrels and enter the cracks of the earth and the clefts of the rocks, forever beyond the reach of the unrighteous. They will sigh and weep because of you like satyrs.]

### Verse 3

So do not be afraid, you who suffer. Healing will be your portion. A brilliant light will shine upon you, and you will hear a voice of peace from heaven.

### Verse 4

How terrible it will be for you sinners! Your wealth makes you look like righteous people, but your own hearts testify that you are guilty. These words will stand as evidence against you and preserve the memory of your wickedness.

### Verse 5

How terrible it will be for you who consume the finest grain, take the strength of the spring for yourselves, and use your power to trample the poor.

### Verse 6

How terrible it will be for you who continually drink from the water supply but have abandoned the fountain of life. You will suddenly face repayment; you will dry up and wither.

### Verse 7

How terrible it will be for you who practise injustice, deception, and blasphemy. These deeds will remain as an evil record against you.

### Verse 8

How terrible it will be for you powerful people who use your strength to oppress the righteous. The day of your destruction will come. In those days the righteous will enjoy many good days, even as your judgment arrives.

---

## Completeness and editorial notes

- All **8 numbered verses** in the repository's Chapter 96 have been rendered individually.
- The chapter's sequence and major claims have been retained; no verse has been intentionally omitted or replaced with a chapter summary.
- Where the source contains historically situated or ethically troubling assertions, the rendering preserves the passage as text and does not present those assertions as modern factual guidance.
- This rendering follows the repository's chapter and verse divisions and does not silently substitute another translation's wording or arrangement.
