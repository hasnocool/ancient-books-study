# 1 Enoch — Chapter 61
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 61.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 13 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.



---

### Verse 1

In those days I saw that long measuring cords were given to the angels. They took their wings and flew northward.

### Verse 2

I asked the angel: “Why have these angels taken the cords and gone away?” He answered: “They have gone to measure.”

### Verse 3

The angel who accompanied me said: “They are bringing measures and cords of righteousness to the righteous, so that they may hold firmly to the name of the Lord of Spirits forever and ever.

### Verse 4

“The chosen will begin to live among the chosen. These measures will be given in connection with faith and will strengthen righteousness.

### Verse 5

“These measures will reveal everything hidden in the depths of the earth, including those who were destroyed in the wilderness and those devoured by the fish of the sea and by wild animals. They will return and find support on the day of the Chosen One. No one will be destroyed before the Lord of Spirits, and no one can be destroyed.

### Verse 6

“All the powers dwelling above in heaven received one command, one voice, and one light like fire.

### Verse 7

“They blessed, praised, and honoured the One who is above them all. They showed wisdom in their speech and in the spirit of life.

### Verse 8

“The Lord of Spirits placed the Chosen One upon the throne of glory. He will judge all the works of the holy ones in heaven and weigh their deeds in the balance.

### Verse 9

“When he raises his face to judge their hidden ways according to the word and name of the Lord of Spirits, and to judge their paths according to the Lord's righteous judgment, they will all speak with one voice, blessing, glorifying, praising, and honouring the name of the Lord of Spirits.

### Verse 10

“He will summon the entire host of heaven, all the holy ones above, and the army of God: the cherubim, seraphim, and ophanim; all the angels of power and authority; the Chosen One; and every other power on earth and over the waters.

### Verse 11

“They will raise one voice and bless and glorify him in the spirit of faith, wisdom, patience, mercy, judgment, peace, and goodness. Together they will say: ‘Blessed is he! May the name of the Lord of Spirits be blessed forever and ever!’

### Verse 12

“All those who never sleep in heaven will bless him. All the holy ones in heaven will bless him, as will all the chosen who live in the Garden of Life and every spirit of light able to bless, glorify, praise, and honour your blessed name. All flesh will glorify and bless your name beyond measure forever and ever.

### Verse 13

“For great is the mercy of the Lord of Spirits, and he is patient. All his works, in all their vast extent, he has revealed to the righteous and chosen in the name of the Lord of Spirits.”

## 1893 witness note

At 61:1 Charles prints “towards the North.” His critical note explains this as “the North-West.” The modernization retains the printed cardinal direction in the verse and records the more specific geographical gloss here rather than silently rewriting the verse.
