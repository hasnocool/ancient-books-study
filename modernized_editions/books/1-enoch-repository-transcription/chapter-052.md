# 1 Enoch — Chapter 52
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 52.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 9 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.



---

### Verse 1

After those days, in the place where I had seen all the hidden visions, I was carried away in a whirlwind and taken toward the west.

### Verse 2

There my eyes saw all the hidden things of heaven that are yet to come: a mountain of iron, one of copper, one of silver, one of gold, one of a soft metal, and one of lead.

### Verse 3

I asked the angel who accompanied me: “What are these things I have seen in secret?”

### Verse 4

He answered: “All these things you have seen will serve the rule of God's Anointed One, so that he may be powerful and mighty on the earth.”

### Verse 5

The angel of peace answered me: “Wait a little, and everything hidden that the Lord of Spirits has established will be revealed to you.

### Verse 6

“Those mountains you saw—the mountains of iron, copper, silver, gold, soft metal, and lead—will all melt before the Chosen One, like wax before fire and like water flowing down from above. They will become powerless beneath his feet.

### Verse 7

“In those days no one will be saved by gold or silver, and no one will be able to escape through wealth.

### Verse 8

“There will be no iron left for weapons or material for armour. Bronze will be useless, and tin will have no value. Lead will no longer be wanted.

### Verse 9

“All these things will be rejected and destroyed from the face of the earth when the Chosen One appears before the Lord of Spirits.”
