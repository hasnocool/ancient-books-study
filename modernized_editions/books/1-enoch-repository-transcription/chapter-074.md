# 1 Enoch — Chapter 74
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-03-luminaries-chapters-72-82.md`, Chapter 74.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 17 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Historical-science note:** The chapter's ancient astronomical model and numerical claims are preserved as part of the source text; this modernization does not present them as current astronomy.

---

### Verse 1

I saw another course and the law appointed to the moon as it completed its monthly revolution.

### Verse 2

Uriel, the holy angel who leads them all, showed me everything. I recorded their positions as he showed them, their months, and the appearance of their lights through the passage of fifteen days.

### Verse 3

The moon grows brighter by successive seventh-parts until its light is complete in the east. It then wanes by seventh-parts until its darkness is complete in the west.

### Verse 4

In certain months the moon changes its setting, and in other months it follows its own particular course.

### Verse 5

In two of the middle gates—the third and fourth—the moon sets together with the sun.

### Verse 6

For seven days the moon moves out and turns back, returning through the gate where the sun rises. There its light is full. It then moves away from the sun and, after eight days, enters the sixth gate from which the sun emerges.

### Verse 7

When the sun emerges through the fourth gate, the moon travels for seven days and passes through the fifth gate. After another seven days it returns to the fourth gate, where its light becomes full. It then moves away and enters the first gate after eight days.

### Verse 8

After seven more days it returns to the fourth gate, from which the sun emerges.

### Verse 9

In this way I observed their positions and the order in which the sun and moon rise and set throughout their months.

### Verse 10

Over a period of five years, the sun accumulates an excess of thirty days. Each of those five full years contains 364 days.

### Verse 11

The excess associated with the sun and stars amounts to six days each year. Over five years, these six days add up to thirty. The moon falls behind the sun and stars by thirty days.

### Verse 12

The moon brings all the years into exact order, so that their positions are never advanced or delayed by even one day forever. The changing years are completed with exactness at 364 days.

### Verse 13

Three years contain 1,092 days, and five years contain 1,820 days. Together, eight years contain 2,912 days.

### Verse 14

In three years the moon itself completes 1,062 days, and over five years it falls fifty days behind.

### Verse 15

In five lunar years there are 1,770 days, so eight lunar years contain 2,832 days.

### Verse 16

In this way the moon falls behind by eighty days over eight years. The total amount of its delay across those eight years is eighty days.

### Verse 17

The year is completed accurately according to the appointed positions and stations of the sun. The sun and moon rise through their assigned gates, and the sun sets after its thirty-day course.
