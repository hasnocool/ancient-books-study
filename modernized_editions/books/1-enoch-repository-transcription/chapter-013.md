# 1 Enoch — Chapter 13
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 13.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 10 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

Enoch went and said: “Azazel, you will find no peace. A severe sentence has been issued against you. Rufael will bind you.

### Verse 2

“You will receive no relief, no intercession, and no mercy, because of the oppression you taught and all the blasphemous, oppressive, and sinful acts you revealed to human beings.”

### Verse 3

Then I went farther and spoke to all of them together. They were afraid, and terror and trembling seized them.

### Verse 4

They begged me to write a petition for them so that they might receive forgiveness, and to carry their petition into the presence of God in heaven.

### Verse 5

From that time onward, they were too ashamed of their sins and punishment to speak or even raise their eyes toward heaven.

### Verse 6

I wrote their petition and their prayer concerning their spirits. I included the individual deeds for which they asked forgiveness and patience.

### Verse 7

I went away and sat beside the waters of Dan, in the land of Dan, west of Mount Hermon. I read their petition until I fell asleep.

### Verse 8

Then a dream came to me, and visions appeared. I saw a vision of punishment, so that I might recount it to the children of heaven and rebuke them.

### Verse 9

When I awoke, I went to them. They were all sitting together, weeping with their faces covered, at Ublesjael, between Lebanon and Senecer.

### Verse 10

I told them all the visions I had seen in my sleep. Then I began to speak words of righteousness and rebuke the heavenly Watchers.
