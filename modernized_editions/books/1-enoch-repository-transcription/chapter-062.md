# 1 Enoch — Chapter 62
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 62.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 16 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.



---

### Verse 1

Then the Lord commanded the kings, the powerful, the exalted, and everyone who lives on the earth: “Open your eyes and raise your heads, if you are able to recognize the Chosen One.”

### Verse 2

The Lord of Spirits seated him upon the throne of his glory. The spirit of righteousness was poured out upon him. The words from his mouth put an end to sinners, and all who practise injustice were destroyed before him.

### Verse 3

On that day all the kings, powerful people, exalted ones, and rulers of the earth will stand up. They will see and recognize him seated on his glorious throne. Righteous judgment will be carried out before him, and no lie will be spoken in his presence.

### Verse 4

Then pain will seize them, like the pain of a woman in labour when childbirth becomes difficult and her child is about to be born.

### Verse 5

They will look at one another and be terrified. Their faces will fall, and anguish will seize them when they see the Son of Man seated on his throne of glory.

### Verse 6

The kings, the powerful, and everyone who possesses the earth will praise, bless, and honour the One who rules over all, the One who had been hidden.

### Verse 7

The Son of Man had been hidden in God's presence. The Most High preserved him through his power and revealed him to the chosen.

### Verse 8

The community of the holy and chosen will be established, and all the chosen will stand before him on that day.

### Verse 9

All the kings, powerful people, exalted ones, and rulers of the earth will fall face down before him. They will worship him, place their hope in the Son of Man, and plead with him for mercy.

### Verse 10

But the Lord of Spirits will press them to leave his presence quickly. Their faces will be covered with shame, and darkness will cover them.

### Verse 11

The angels of punishment will take charge of them and carry out vengeance, because they oppressed God's children and chosen people.

### Verse 12

They will become a spectacle for the righteous and chosen. The righteous will rejoice because the anger of the Lord of Spirits rests upon their oppressors and his sword is filled with their blood.

### Verse 13

On that day the righteous and chosen will be saved. From then on they will never again see the faces of sinners and those who practise injustice.

### Verse 14

The Lord of Spirits will remain with them. They will eat, lie down, and rise again with the Son of Man forever and ever.

### Verse 15

The righteous and chosen will rise from the earth. Their faces will no longer be downcast; they will be clothed in garments of glory.

### Verse 16

“These will be your garments: garments of life in the presence of the Lord of Spirits. They will never wear out, and your glory will never fade before the Lord of Spirits.”
