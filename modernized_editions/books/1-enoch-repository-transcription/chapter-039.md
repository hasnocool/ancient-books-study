# 1 Enoch — Chapter 39
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 39.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 14 numbered units in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Textual caution:** Source-marked interpolations remain separately bracketed. Chapter 39 verse 2 is damaged in the repository transcription and is identified as a cautious rendering rather than a secure reconstruction.

---

### Verse 1 — interpolation

[In those days, chosen and holy beings from the highest heaven will descend, and their descendants will become united with the children of humanity.]

### Verse 2 — interpolation; damaged source wording

[In those days Enoch received records concerning zeal, anger, distress, and expulsion. The source text is damaged at this point; it appears to state that mercy will not be granted to them, according to the Lord of Spirits.]

### Verse 3

In those days a cloud and a whirlwind carried me away from the earth and set me down at the edge of the heavens.

### Verse 4

There I saw another vision: the dwellings of the holy and the resting places prepared for the righteous.

### Verse 5

There my eyes saw their dwellings with God's righteous angels and their resting places among the holy ones. They pleaded, interceded, and prayed for humanity. Righteousness flowed before them like water, and mercy fell upon the earth like dew. This is how they live forever and ever.

### Verse 6

In that place my eyes saw the Chosen One, who is righteous and faithful. I saw how righteousness would prevail during his days, and how the righteous and chosen would stand before him forever, beyond all counting.

### Verse 7

I saw his dwelling beneath the wings of the Lord of Spirits. All the righteous and chosen who stood before him shone beautifully, like lights of fire. Their mouths were full of blessing, and their lips praised the name of the Lord of Spirits. Righteousness and integrity never fail before him.

### Verse 8

I wanted to remain there, and my soul longed for that dwelling. It had already been appointed as my portion, for this had been established concerning me before the Lord of Spirits.

### Verse 9

In those days I praised and honoured the name of the Lord of Spirits with blessings and songs of praise, because according to his good pleasure he had appointed blessing and glory for me.

### Verse 10

For a long time my eyes looked upon that place. I blessed and praised him, saying: “Blessed is he, and may he be blessed from the beginning and forevermore.”

### Verse 11

There is no end to his existence. Before the world was created, he knew what it would be, and he knows every generation that will arise.

### Verse 12

Those who never sleep bless you. They stand before your glory and bless, praise, and honour you, saying: “Holy, holy, holy is the Lord of Spirits; he fills the earth with spirits.”

### Verse 13

There my eyes saw all those who never sleep. They stood before him, blessing him and saying: “Blessed are you, and blessed is the name of the Lord forever and ever.”

### Verse 14

My face changed, and I could no longer see.
