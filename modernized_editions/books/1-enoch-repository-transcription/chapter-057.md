# 1 Enoch — Chapter 57
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 57.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 3 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Textual caution:** Source-marked interpolation status is retained; Chapter 60 is identified in the source as a Noachic interpolation.

### Verse 1 — interpolation

[After this I again saw a company of chariots. People rode in them, and they came on the wings of the wind from the east, west, and south.]

### Verse 2 — interpolation

[The noise of their chariots could be heard. When this commotion began, the holy ones in heaven noticed it, and the pillars of the earth shook in their places. The sound was heard from one end of heaven to the other in a single day.]

### Verse 3 — interpolation

[They will all fall down and worship the Lord of Spirits.]

**Editorial note:** These three verses belong to the interpolation identified in the source as extending from Chapter 56, verse 5, through Chapter 57, verse 3.
