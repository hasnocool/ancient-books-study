# 1 Enoch — Chapter 55
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 55.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 4 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Textual caution:** Source-marked interpolations remain separately bracketed and identified.

---

### Verse 1 — interpolation

[After this, the Ancient of Days relented and said: “I have destroyed all those who live on the earth in vain.”]

### Verse 2 — interpolation

[He swore by his great name: “From now on I will not do this again to all who live on the earth. I will place a sign in the heavens as a lasting pledge between me and them, for as long as heaven remains above the earth.”]

### Verse 3

“This will happen according to my command: when I decide to take hold of them through the angels on the day of distress and suffering, I will first bring my anger and punishment upon them,” says God, the Lord of Spirits.

### Verse 4

“You mighty kings who live on the earth will have to see my Chosen One. He will sit upon the throne of glory and judge Azazel, all his companions, and all their armies, in the name of the Lord of Spirits.”

**Editorial note:** The source identifies verses 1–2 as part of the Noachic interpolation; verses 3–4 return to the main Similitudes text.
