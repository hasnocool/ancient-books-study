# 1 Enoch — Chapter 14
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 14.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 25 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

This book contains the words of righteousness and the rebuke of the eternal Watchers, given in accordance with the command of the Holy and Great One through the vision.

### Verse 2

I saw this in my sleep, and I will now recount it with human speech and with the breath the Great One placed in the mouths of people, so that they could speak and understand with their hearts.

### Verse 3

Just as God created human beings and gave them the ability to understand words of wisdom, he also created me and gave me the ability to rebuke the Watchers, the sons of heaven.

### Verse 4

“I wrote down your petition, but this is what appeared to me in the vision: Your request will not be granted for all eternity. Judgment has been finally pronounced against you, and no pardon will be given.

### Verse 5

“From now on, you will never again ascend into heaven. The decree has gone out against you on earth: you will be bound for all eternity.

### Verse 6

“Before that, however, you will witness the destruction of your beloved sons. They will no longer remain in your care; they will fall by the sword before your eyes.

### Verse 7

“Your petition for them will not be granted, nor will your petition for yourselves. Despite your tears and prayers, nothing written in the document I prepared will be granted to you.

### Verse 8

“This is how the vision appeared to me: clouds and mist called to me. The movements of the stars and the flashes of lightning drove me onward. In the vision, the winds gave me wings and carried me forward.

### Verse 9

“They lifted me into heaven until I came near a wall built of crystal and surrounded by a blazing fire. It filled me with fear.

### Verse 10

“I entered the fiery enclosure and approached a great house built of crystal. Its walls resembled a floor made of mosaic crystal, and its foundation was crystal.

### Verse 11

“Its ceiling resembled the paths of the stars and lightning, with fiery cherubim between them. Above them was something like a sea of water.

### Verse 12

“A blazing fire surrounded the walls of the house, and its entrance burned with fire.

### Verse 13

“I entered the house. It was hot like fire and cold like ice. It held none of the comforts of life. Fear covered me, and trembling seized me.

### Verse 14

“As I shook with fear, I fell face down and saw a vision.

### Verse 15

“Then I saw a second house, greater than the first. All its gates stood open before me, and it was built of flames of fire.

### Verse 16

“Its splendour, magnificence, and vastness surpassed the first house in every way. I cannot describe its glory or its size.

### Verse 17

“Its floor was fire. Above it were lightning and the paths of the stars, and its ceiling was also blazing fire.

### Verse 18

“I looked and saw a lofty throne. It looked like frost; its surrounding brilliance was like a shining sun, and there were the voices of cherubim.

### Verse 19

“Streams of blazing fire flowed from beneath the great throne, so that no one could look upon them.

### Verse 20

“The Great Glory sat upon the throne. His clothing shone more brightly than the sun and was whiter than any snow.

### Verse 21

“None of the angels could enter and look upon the face of the honoured and glorious One. No human being could see him.

### Verse 22

“A blazing fire surrounded him, and a great fire stood before him. No one around him could approach. Ten thousand times ten thousand stood before him, yet he needed no counsellor.

### Verse 23

“The holy ones who were near him never left his presence, by night or by day.

### Verse 24

“Until then I had kept a covering over my face, and I was trembling. Then the Lord called me with his own voice: ‘Come here, Enoch, and listen to my holy words.’

### Verse 25

“He raised me up and brought me toward the entrance, but I kept my face turned downward.”
