# 1 Enoch — Chapter 85
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-04-visions-chapters-83-90.md`, Chapter 85.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 10 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

After this I saw another dream, and I will show you the entire vision, my son.

### Verse 2

Enoch raised his voice and said to his son Methuselah: “My son, I will speak to you. Listen to my words and pay attention to your father's dream and vision.

### Verse 3

“Before I married your mother Edna, I had a vision while lying in my bed. A bull came out of the earth, and it was white. After it came a heifer, and with her came two young bulls, one black and one red.

### Verse 4

“The black young bull attacked the red one with its horns and chased it across the earth. After that I could no longer see the red young bull, who represents Abel.

### Verse 5

“But the black young bull grew up, joined with the heifer, and many oxen came from him. They resembled him and followed after him.

### Verse 6

“The first cow left the first bull and went looking for the red young bull, but she could not find him. She mourned loudly and continued searching for him.

### Verse 7

“I watched until the first bull came to her and comforted her. From that moment onward, she no longer cried.

### Verse 8

“After this she gave birth to another white bull, who represents Seth. After him she gave birth to many bulls and black cows.

### Verse 9

“In my sleep I saw that white bull grow until he became a great white bull. Many white oxen came from him, all resembling him.

### Verse 10

“They began to produce many white oxen, each resembling the others, one generation following another.”
