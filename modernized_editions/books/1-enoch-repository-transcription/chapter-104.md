# 1 Enoch — Chapter 104
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-05-laws-of-enoch-chapters-91-105.md`, Chapter 104.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 13 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

I solemnly declare to you that the angels in heaven remember you for good before the glory of the Great One. Your names are written in the presence of that glory.

### Verse 2

Take courage. In the past you were shamed through suffering and hardship, but soon you will shine like the stars of heaven. You will be seen shining, and heaven's gates will be opened to you.

### Verse 3

Keep crying out for justice, and justice will come to you. All the suffering you endured will be brought upon the rulers, their assistants, and those who robbed you.

### Verse 4

Take courage and do not abandon hope. You will experience great joy, like the angels of heaven.

### Verse 5

What will you need to do then? You will not need to hide on the day of the great judgment. You will not be counted among sinners, and the eternal judgment will remain far from you throughout all generations.

### Verse 6

Do not be afraid, you righteous people, when you see sinners becoming powerful and prospering. Do not imitate them or join them. Keep away from their violence, for you will become companions of the hosts of heaven.

### Verse 7

You sinners may say, “No one can know what we do, and our sins are not being recorded.” But all your sins are being written down continually, every day.

### Verse 8

I tell you that light and darkness, day and night, all bear witness to your sins.

### Verse 9

Do not become corrupt in your hearts. Do not lie, alter the words of righteousness, accuse the Holy and Great One of falsehood, or glorify idols. Your lies and irreverence will not become righteousness; they will be recognized as serious sin.

### Verse 10

I know this mystery: many sinners will alter and distort the words of righteousness. They will speak wickedly, lie, practise great deception, and write books promoting their own words.

### Verse 11

But if people write down all my words truthfully in their own languages, changing nothing and leaving nothing out, and faithfully record everything I first testified concerning them—

### Verse 12

—then I know another mystery: books will be given to the righteous and the wise. These books will become a source of joy, integrity, and great wisdom.

### Verse 13

The books will be given to them, and they will believe what is written in them and rejoice. All the righteous who learn from them the ways of integrity will receive their reward.
