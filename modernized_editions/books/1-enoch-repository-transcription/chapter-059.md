# 1 Enoch — Chapter 59
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 59.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 3 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Textual caution:** Source-marked interpolation status is retained; Chapter 60 is identified in the source as a Noachic interpolation.

---

### Verse 1 — interpolation

[In those days my eyes saw the mysteries of lightning, the heavenly lights, and the judgments they carry out. They shine for blessing or for curse, according to the will of the Lord of Spirits.]

### Verse 2 — interpolation

[Then I saw the mysteries of thunder: when it sounds in the heavens, its roar can be heard. I was shown the places on earth where its sound is heard and how thunder can serve for well-being and blessing, or for a curse before the Lord of Spirits.]

### Verse 3 — interpolation

[After this, all the mysteries of the heavenly lights and lightning were shown to me, and how they shine to bring blessing and satisfaction.]

**Editorial note:** The source edition brackets the entire chapter as an interpolation. All three verses remain bracketed.
