# 1 Enoch — Chapter 69
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 69.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 29 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Textual caution:** The source identifies part or all of this chapter as part of the Noachic interpolation. The source's distinction between interpolated and non-interpolated verses is retained.

---

### Verse 1 — interpolation

[After this judgment, they will inspire fear and anger because they revealed these things to the people who live on the earth.]

### Verse 2 — interpolation

[These are the names of those angels, listed in their stated order: first, Semjaza; second, Arestiqifa; third, Armen; fourth, Kokabael; fifth, Turel; sixth, Rimjal; seventh, Danel; eighth, Nuqael; ninth, Baraqel; tenth, Azazel; eleventh, Armers; twelfth, Batarjal; thirteenth, Basasael; fourteenth, Ananel; fifteenth, Turjal; sixteenth, Simapisiel; seventeenth, Jetarel; eighteenth, Tumael; nineteenth, Tarel; twentieth, Rumael; and twenty-first, Izezeel.]

### Verse 3 — interpolation

[These are the chiefs of the angels, along with the names of those appointed as leaders over groups of one hundred, fifty, and ten.]

### Verse 4 — interpolation

[The first is named Jequn. He led astray all the children of the angels, brought them down to earth, and led them astray through the daughters of human beings.]

### Verse 5 — interpolation

[The second is named Asbeel. He gave evil advice to the children of the holy angels and led them astray, so that they defiled themselves with the daughters of humanity.]

### Verse 6 — interpolation

[The third is named Gadreel. He taught human beings every deadly weapon and method of killing. He led Eve astray and showed humanity the weapons of death: armour, shields, swords for battle, and every other instrument of killing.]

### Verse 7 — interpolation

[These things have spread from his hand among those who live on the earth, from that time onward forever.]

### Verse 8 — interpolation

[The fourth is named Penemue. He taught human beings about bitterness and sweetness and revealed all kinds of wisdom and knowledge.]

### Verse 9 — interpolation

[He taught humanity to write with ink and paper. Through this, many have sinned throughout the ages, right up to the present day.]

### Verse 10 — interpolation

[When humanity was created, it was not intended that people should confirm their words and actions through written records made with pen and ink in this way.]

### Verse 11 — interpolation

[Human beings were created like the angels so that they might remain righteous and pure. Death, which destroys everything, would not have gained power over them. But through this knowledge they are perishing, and through this power it is consuming me.]

### Verse 12 — interpolation

[The fifth is named Kasdeja. He taught human beings every evil attack carried out by spirits and demons: attacks against the unborn child and the infant that cause death; attacks against the soul; the bites of serpents; and attacks that occur at midday, including the serpent's offspring named Tabat.]

### Verse 13 — interpolation

[This is the account of Kesbeel, who revealed the chief secret of the oath to the holy ones when he lived high above in glory. The name of that secret is Beqa.]

### Verse 14 — interpolation

[He asked Michael to reveal this hidden name, so that it could be spoken in the oath. Those who had revealed everything hidden to human beings would then tremble before that name and oath.]

### Verse 15 — interpolation

[This is the power of the oath: it is powerful and strong. God placed the oath, called Akae, in the hand of Michael.]

### Verse 16 — interpolation

[These are the mysteries of the oath: heaven was made firm through it and was suspended before the world was created, remaining so forever.]

### Verse 17 — interpolation

[Through the oath, the earth was founded upon the waters. Beautiful waters flow from the hidden depths of the mountains, from the creation of the world and throughout eternity.]

### Verse 18 — interpolation

[Through the oath, the sea was created. As its boundary, God placed the sand against the day of anger, and the sea dares not pass beyond it, from the creation of the world forever.]

### Verse 19 — interpolation

[Through the oath, the depths are made firm. They remain in place and do not move, from eternity to eternity.]

### Verse 20 — interpolation

[Through the oath, the sun and moon complete their courses and never depart from the paths appointed to them, from eternity to eternity.]

### Verse 21 — interpolation

[Through the oath, the stars complete their courses. God calls each by name, and they answer him, from eternity to eternity.]

### Verse 22 — interpolation

[The same is true of the spirits of the waters, the winds, every breeze, and the paths followed by all the groups of spirits.]

### Verse 23 — interpolation

[The voices of thunder and the light of lightning are preserved within it. So are the chambers of hail, frost, mist, rain, and dew.]

### Verse 24 — interpolation

[All these things remain faithful and give thanks before the Lord of Spirits. They glorify him with all their power. Their only food is thanksgiving: they thank, praise, and honour the name of the Lord of Spirits forever and ever.]

### Verse 25 — interpolation

[This oath has authority over them and preserves them. Their paths are kept safe, and their courses are not destroyed.]

### Verse 26

There was great joy among them. They blessed, glorified, and praised God because the name of the Son of Man had been revealed to them.

### Verse 27

He sat upon the throne of his glory, and the full authority to judge was entrusted to him, the Son of Man. He caused sinners and those who had led the world astray to disappear and be destroyed from the face of the earth.

### Verse 28

They will be bound with chains and imprisoned in the place appointed for their destruction. All their works will vanish from the face of the earth.

### Verse 29

From then on, nothing corruptible will remain. The Son of Man has appeared and sits upon the throne of his glory. All evil will disappear and depart before him. The word of the Son of Man will have authority before the Lord of Spirits.

**This is the third parable of Enoch.**

**Editorial note:** Verses 1–25 are part of the Noachic interpolation identified by the source. Verses 26–29 conclude the third parable and are not bracketed in the source edition.
