# 1 Enoch — Chapter 73
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-03-luminaries-chapters-72-82.md`, Chapter 73.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 8 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Historical-science note:** The chapter's ancient astronomical model and numerical claims are preserved as part of the source text; this modernization does not present them as current astronomy.

---

### Verse 1

After this law I saw another law, concerning the smaller light called the moon.

### Verse 2

Its circumference is like the circumference of heaven. The wind drives the chariot in which it travels, and its light is given to it in measured amounts.

### Verse 3

The moon's rising and setting change each month. Its days follow the pattern of the sun's days, and when its light is full it amounts to one seventh of the sun's light.

### Verse 4

This is how it rises: its first appearance in the east comes on the thirtieth morning. On that day it becomes visible and marks the moon's first phase, appearing with the sun at the gate through which the sun rises.

### Verse 5

At first, one seventh of its visible half is illuminated. Most of its circumference remains dark, except for one seventh of the moon and one fourteenth of that half of its light.

### Verse 6

When it receives one seventh of the half of its light, its brightness amounts to one seventh plus half of that portion.

### Verse 7

The moon sets with the sun. When the sun rises, the moon rises with it and receives half of one part of light. At the beginning of that night and the beginning of its day, it sets with the sun and remains invisible throughout the night, with all fourteen parts and half of one part hidden.

### Verse 8

On that day it rises with exactly one seventh of its light, appears, and moves away from the sun's rising. During the remaining days its light increases through the other thirteen parts.
