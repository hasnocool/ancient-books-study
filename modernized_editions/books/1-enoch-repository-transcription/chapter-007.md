# 1 Enoch — Chapter 7
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 7.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 6 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.



---

### Verse 1

They took women as wives. Each chose one for himself, and they began to have relations with them. They joined themselves to the women and taught them spells and magical practices. They also taught them about cutting roots and working with plants and wood.

### Verse 2

The women became pregnant and gave birth to enormous giants, whose height was three thousand ells.

### Verse 3

The giants consumed everything people had produced, until humanity could no longer provide enough to sustain them.

### Verse 4

Then the giants turned against humanity itself, intending to devour people.

### Verse 5

They sinned against birds, animals, reptiles, and fish. They ate one another's flesh and drank blood.

### Verse 6

Then the earth raised its complaint against the lawless ones.
