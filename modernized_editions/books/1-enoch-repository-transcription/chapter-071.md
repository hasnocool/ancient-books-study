# 1 Enoch — Chapter 71
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 71.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 17 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.



---

### Verse 1

After this, my spirit was hidden and carried up into the heavens. I saw the sons of the holy angels walking upon flames of fire. Their clothing was white, and their garments and faces shone like snow.

### Verse 2

I saw two streams of fire, and the light of the fire shone like hyacinth. I fell face down before the Lord of Spirits.

### Verse 3

Michael, one of the archangels, took me by my right hand, raised me up, and introduced me to all the mysteries of mercy and righteousness.

### Verse 4

He showed me all the mysteries at the farthest edges of heaven and all the chambers of the stars and heavenly lights, from which they come out before the holy ones.

### Verse 5

The spirit carried Enoch into the highest heaven. There, in the midst of the light, I saw a structure made of crystal, with flames of living fire between the crystals.

### Verse 6

My spirit saw fire surrounding that house on all four sides. Streams full of living fire flowed around it and encircled the house.

### Verse 7

Around it stood seraphim, cherubim, and ophanim. These are the beings who never sleep and who guard the throne of God's glory.

### Verse 8

I saw angels beyond counting—thousands upon thousands and ten thousand times ten thousand—surrounding the house. Michael, Gabriel, Rufael, Fanuel, and the holy angels of heaven went in and out of it.

### Verse 9

Michael, Gabriel, Rufael, Fanuel, and countless other holy angels came out of the house.

### Verse 10

The Ancient of Days came with them. His head was white and pure like wool, and his clothing was beyond description.

### Verse 11

I fell face down, and my whole body seemed to melt away, but my spirit was transformed. With a powerful voice and spirit I cried out, blessed, glorified, and praised him.

### Verse 12

The blessings that came from my mouth were pleasing before the Ancient of Days.

### Verse 13

The Ancient of Days came with Michael, Gabriel, Rufael, and Fanuel, together with thousands and tens of thousands of angels beyond counting.

### Verse 14

He came to me, greeted me with his voice, and said: “You are a son of man, born for righteousness, and righteousness rests upon you. The righteousness of the Ancient of Days will never abandon you.

### Verse 15

“He announces peace to you in the name of the world to come. Peace has existed from the creation of the world, and it will remain with you forever and ever.

### Verse 16

“All who in future generations walk in your ways, you whom righteousness never abandons, will have their dwelling places and inheritance with you. They will never be separated from you, forever and ever.

### Verse 17

“The Son of Man will have a long and enduring existence. The righteous will have peace, and the righteous will follow his path of integrity in the name of the Lord of Spirits forever and ever.”

**Editorial note:** The source transcription contains a note that Charles's later 1912 text records a lost passage between verses 13 and 14. That missing passage is not present in the selected repository text and has not been reconstructed. The rendering follows the repository's 17-verse sequence.
