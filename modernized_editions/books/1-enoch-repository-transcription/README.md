This directory contains a separate modern-language rendering of all 108 numbered chapters of 1 Enoch found in the repository's selected transcription.

## Editorial scope

- Every chapter from 1 through 108 has its own Markdown file.
- Each chapter is rendered verse by verse, retaining the repository transcription's chapter sequence and verse divisions.
- Unnumbered passages remain unnumbered; numbering gaps and duplicate verse labels are documented rather than silently repaired.
- Interpolations identified by the source edition remain explicitly marked.
- Damaged or uncertain source passages are identified rather than presented as certain reconstructions.
- The modernized text is separate from the formatted source transcription.

## Important source limitation

The source compilation attributes its text to R. H. Charles and Professor August Dillmann (1893), but the formatted-edition notes state that the supplied wording has not been verified as a verbatim transcription of that printing and that its exact textual lineage remains unresolved.

Accordingly, these files are complete modern-language renderings **of the selected repository transcription**, not a new translation from the Ethiopic and not a claim of a verified word-for-word Charles 1893 edition. The historical source transcription remains unchanged in `formatted_editions/`.

The smaller artifacts are not facsimiles; the larger scans are the intended historical page-image witnesses.
