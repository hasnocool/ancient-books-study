# 1 Enoch — Chapter 2
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 2.

**Editorial method:** Complete modernization of the chapter's 3 numbered verses. The wording is updated for readability while retaining the source's sequence and substance. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

I observed everything that happens in the heavens. The heavenly lights do not stray from their courses. They all rise and set in their proper order, each at its appointed time, and none breaks the pattern assigned to it.

### Verse 2

Look at the earth and pay attention to everything that happens upon it, from beginning to end. See how steadily every work of God unfolds.

### Verse 3

Look at summer and winter: the whole earth is filled with water, and clouds, dew, and rain settle upon it.
