# 1 Enoch — Chapter 38
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 38.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 6 numbered units in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.



---

### Verse 1

When the assembly of the righteous appears, sinners will be judged for their sins and driven from the face of the earth.

### Verse 2

When the Righteous One appears before the chosen righteous, whose deeds are carried out in devotion to the Lord of Spirits, light will shine upon the righteous and chosen who live on the earth. Then where will the sinners live? Where will those who rejected the Lord of Spirits find a place to rest? It would have been better for them if they had never been born.

### Verse 3

When the secrets of the righteous are revealed, sinners will be judged and the godless will be driven away from the presence of the righteous and chosen.

### Verse 4

From that time onward, those who possess the earth will no longer be powerful or exalted. They will not be able to look upon the faces of the holy, because the light of the Lord of Spirits will shine upon the faces of the holy, the righteous, and the chosen.

### Verse 5

Then kings and powerful people will perish and be handed over to the righteous and holy.

### Verse 6

From that time onward, no one will plead with the Lord of Spirits for mercy on their behalf, because their lives will have come to an end.
