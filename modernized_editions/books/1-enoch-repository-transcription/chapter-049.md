# 1 Enoch — Chapter 49
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 49.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 4 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.



---

### Verse 1

Wisdom flows out like water, and glory never fades in his presence, forever and ever.

### Verse 2

He is powerful in every mystery of righteousness. Injustice will disappear like a shadow and will have no lasting existence. The Chosen One stands before the Lord of Spirits; his glory endures forever, and his power continues through every generation.

### Verse 3

The spirit of wisdom dwells in him, along with the spirit of the One who gives knowledge, the spirit of understanding and strength, and the spirit of those who have died in righteousness.

### Verse 4

He will judge hidden matters, and no one will be able to speak a lie in his presence. He is the Chosen One before the Lord of Spirits, according to God's good pleasure.
