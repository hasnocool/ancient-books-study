# 1 Enoch — Chapter 8
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 8.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 4 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Textual caution:** Interpolations identified by the source edition remain separately bracketed and labelled.

---

### Verse 1 — interpolation

[Azazel taught people to make swords, knives, shields, and coats of armour. He showed them how to work with metals and make objects from them. He taught them to make bracelets and ornaments, use antimony, decorate their eyelids, and work with precious and beautiful stones and coloured dyes. Through these teachings, the world was changed.]

### Verse 2 — interpolation

[Great irreverence and sexual immorality spread. People sinned, and all their ways became corrupt.]

### Verse 3 — interpolation

[Amezarak taught the enchanters and those who cut roots. Armaros taught the breaking of spells. Baraqal taught astrology; Kokabel taught the signs; Temel taught the study of the stars; and Asradel taught the course of the moon.]

### Verse 4

As people perished, they cried out, and their voices rose up to heaven.

**Editorial note:** The source marks verses 1–3 as interpolations. They remain bracketed and separately identified here. Charles's 1893 errata, p. xiii, corrects the printed Chapter 8:1 reading “eyebrows” (printed p. 66, line 8) to “eyelids”; this modernization follows the published correction. The repository's formatted base retains “eyebrows,” so this is a disclosed source-based correction.
