# 1 Enoch — Chapter 87
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-04-visions-chapters-83-90.md`, Chapter 87.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 4 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

Again I saw them goring and devouring one another, and the earth began to cry out loudly.

### Verse 2

I raised my eyes to heaven and saw a vision: beings like white men came down from heaven. One came out from that place, and three others came with him.

### Verse 3

The three who had come last took me by the hand, lifted me away from the generations of the earth, and carried me to a high place. They showed me a tower rising far above the earth, while all the hills were lower than it.

### Verse 4

They said to me: “Stay here until you have seen everything that happens to the elephants, camels, donkeys, stars, oxen, and all the others.”
