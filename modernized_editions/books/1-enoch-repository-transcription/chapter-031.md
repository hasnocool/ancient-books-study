# 1 Enoch — Chapter 31
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Editorial method:** Complete modernization of the chapter's 3 numbered verses, updating archaic language while retaining its sequence and substance. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

I saw other mountains covered with trees. From them flowed something like nectar, called sarira and galbanum.

### Verse 2

Above those mountains I saw another mountain covered with aloe trees. The trees were full of a hard substance resembling almonds.

### Verse 3

The taste of that fruit was better than every other fragrance.
