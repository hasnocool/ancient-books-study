# 1 Enoch — Chapter 50
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 50.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 5 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Textual caution:** The source edition marks the entire chapter as an interpolation. All verses remain bracketed.

---

### Verse 1 — interpolation

[In those days the condition of the holy and chosen will be transformed. The light of day will remain with them, and glory and honour will belong to the holy.]

### Verse 2 — interpolation

[On the day of suffering, evil will gather over sinners, but the righteous will triumph in the name of the Lord of Spirits. He will make the others witness this, so that they may repent and abandon the deeds of their hands.]

### Verse 3 — interpolation

[They will not possess honour through the name of the Lord of Spirits, yet through his name they will be saved. The Lord of Spirits will show them compassion, because his mercy is great.]

### Verse 4 — interpolation

[He is righteous in his judgment. In the presence of his glory and under his judgment, no injustice can stand. Whoever refuses to repent before him will perish.]

### Verse 5 — interpolation

[From that time onward he will show them no further mercy, says the Lord of Spirits.]

**Editorial note:** The source edition brackets the entire chapter as an interpolation. All five verses remain separately bracketed.
