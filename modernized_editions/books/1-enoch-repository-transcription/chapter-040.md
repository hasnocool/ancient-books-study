# 1 Enoch — Chapter 40
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 40.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 10 numbered units in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.



---

### Verse 1

After this I saw thousands upon thousands and ten thousand times ten thousand—a multitude beyond counting—standing before the Lord of Spirits.

### Verse 2

I looked and saw four presences on the four sides of the Lord of Spirits. They were different from the beings who never sleep. I learned their names because the angel who accompanied me revealed them and showed me all the hidden things.

### Verse 3

I heard the voices of these four presences as they gave glory before the Lord of Glory.

### Verse 4

The first voice blesses the Lord of Spirits forever and ever.

### Verse 5

The second voice blesses the Chosen One and the chosen people who remain devoted to the Lord of Spirits.

### Verse 6

The third voice prays and intercedes for those who live on the earth, making requests in the name of the Lord of Spirits.

### Verse 7

The fourth voice drives away the accusers and prevents them from appearing before the Lord of Spirits to accuse the people who live on the earth.

### Verse 8

After this I asked the angel of peace who travelled with me and showed me everything hidden: “Who are these four presences I have seen, whose words I have heard and written down?”

### Verse 9

He answered: “The first is Michael, merciful and patient. The second is Rufael, who is appointed over the illnesses and wounds of human beings. The third is Gabriel, who is appointed over all powers. The fourth is Fanuel, who is appointed over repentance and the hope of those who inherit eternal life.”

### Verse 10

“These are the four angels of the Lord of Spirits, and these are the four voices I heard in those days.”
