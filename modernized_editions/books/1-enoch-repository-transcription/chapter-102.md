# 1 Enoch — Chapter 102
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-05-laws-of-enoch-chapters-91-105.md`, Chapter 102.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 11 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

In those days, when he brings a terrible fire upon you, where will you run? Where will you find safety? When he sends out his word against you, will you not be terrified and afraid?

### Verse 2

All the heavenly lights will tremble with great fear. The whole earth will be frightened, shaking and filled with alarm.

### Verse 3

All the angels will carry out their assigned commands and will seek to hide from the presence of the Great Glory. The people of the earth will tremble and shake. But you sinners are cursed forever, and you will have no peace.

### Verse 4

Do not be afraid, you souls of the righteous. Take courage, you who die while living righteously.

### Verse 5

Do not despair if your soul descends sorrowfully into Sheol, or if your body did not receive in life the treatment your goodness deserved. Your experience may have seemed like that of sinners, under a curse and punishment.

### Verse 6

When you die, sinners will say about you: “We die just as the righteous die. What benefit did they gain from their good deeds? They too died in sorrow and darkness, just as we do. What advantage did they have over us?”

### Verse 7

They will say: “From now on, we are all the same.”

### Verse 8

“What will the righteous receive? What will they see forever? They have died too, and from now on they will never see light again.”

### Verse 9

I say to you sinners: you are satisfied with eating and drinking, stripping people naked, robbing them, and committing sin.

### Verse 10

You accumulate wealth and enjoy what you call good days.

### Verse 11

Have you considered what becomes of the righteous? No violence is found in them up to the day they die.

Yet they too have died, have disappeared as though they had never existed, and their souls have descended into Sheol in distress.
