# 1 Enoch — Chapter 26
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 26.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 6 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

From there I travelled to the centre of the earth. I saw a blessed and fertile place, where branches had taken root and were growing from a tree that had been cut down.

### Verse 2

Then I saw a holy mountain. Beneath it, on its eastern side, a stream flowed southward.

### Verse 3

Toward the east I saw another mountain of the same height. Between the two mountains was a deep, narrow valley, and a stream flowed through it alongside the mountain.

### Verse 4

To the west stood another mountain, lower and less elevated than the first two. Beneath it, between the mountains, was a valley. Deep, barren ravines lay at the outer edges of all three mountains.

### Verse 5

All the ravines were deep and narrow, with hard rocky sides. Trees had been planted in them.

### Verse 6

I was amazed by the rocks and by the ravine. I was filled with great wonder.
