# 1 Enoch — Chapter 90
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-04-visions-chapters-83-90.md`, Chapter 90.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 42 numbered units in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Textual caution:** Verse 15 is marked as an interpolation in the source edition and remains bracketed. The symbolic narrative and the source's historical identifications are preserved without presenting them as independent historical proof.

---

### Verse 1

I watched as thirty-six shepherds in succession took charge of the sheep, each completing an appointed period as the earlier shepherds had done. Other shepherds then received the flock, each caring for it during his own assigned period.

### Verse 2

After this I saw all the birds of heaven arrive: eagles, vultures, kites, and ravens. The eagles led them all. They began attacking the sheep, eating their flesh and tearing out their eyes.

### Verse 3

The sheep cried out as the birds devoured their flesh. As I watched, I lamented in my sleep over the shepherd who was supposed to care for them.

### Verse 4

I watched as dogs, eagles, and kites devoured the sheep. They left no flesh, skin, or sinew; only skeletons remained. Even those skeletons fell to the earth, and the flock became very small.

### Verse 5

I saw that twenty-three shepherds then took charge of the flock, completing fifty-eight periods among them.

### Verse 6

But lambs were born among the white sheep. They began opening their eyes, seeing, and crying out to the other sheep.

### Verse 7

The other sheep did not cry out in response or listen to what the lambs said. They had become extremely deaf, and their eyes were completely and forcefully blinded.

### Verse 8

In the vision I saw ravens swoop down upon the lambs. They seized one of the lambs, struck the sheep apart, and devoured them.

### Verse 9

I watched until horns grew on the lambs, but the ravens knocked their horns down. Then a great horn grew from one of the sheep and branched out, and the sheep's eyes were opened.

### Verse 10

The great-horned sheep looked at the others. Their eyes opened, and it cried out to them. The rams saw it, and they all ran toward it.

### Verse 11

Even then the eagles, vultures, ravens, and kites continued tearing at the sheep, swooping down and devouring them. The sheep did nothing, but the rams mourned and cried out.

### Verse 12

The ravens fought against the great-horned sheep and tried to destroy its horn, but they had no power over it.

### Verse 13

I watched as the shepherds, eagles, vultures, and kites arrived. They called to the ravens and urged them to break the ram's horn. They fought against it, but it fought back and cried out for help.

### Verse 14

I watched until the man who recorded the names of the shepherds and carried the record before the Lord of the sheep arrived. He helped the ram and showed it everything, making clear that he had come down to assist it.

### Verse 15 — interpolation

[I saw the Lord of the sheep come to them in anger. Everyone who saw him fled, and all cast themselves into darkness to escape his presence.]

### Verse 16

All the eagles, vultures, ravens, and kites gathered together. They brought all the sheep of the field with them, joined forces, and helped one another try to break the ram's horn.

### Verse 17

I watched as the man who wrote the book according to the Lord's command opened the record of the destruction caused by the twelve final shepherds. He showed the Lord that they had destroyed far more sheep than the shepherds before them.

### Verse 18

I watched until the Lord of the sheep came to them. He took the staff of his anger in his hand and struck the earth, splitting it apart. All the animals and birds fell away from the sheep and sank into the earth, which closed over them.

### Verse 19

I saw a great sword given to the sheep. They went out against all the wild animals to kill them, and every beast and bird fled before them.

### Verse 20

I saw a throne erected in the pleasant land. The Lord of the sheep sat upon it on the day of judgment, and the other being took the sealed books and opened them before the Lord.

### Verse 21

The Lord called the seven first white beings and commanded them to bring before him all the stars, beginning with the first star that had led the way—the stars whose bodies resembled those of horses. They brought them all before him.

### Verse 22

He spoke to the man who wrote in his presence, one of the seven white beings, and said: “Bring the seventy shepherds to whom I entrusted the sheep. They took authority into their own hands and killed more sheep than I commanded them to destroy.”

### Verse 23

I saw that all the shepherds were bound and stood before him.

### Verse 24

Judgment was first carried out against the stars. They were found guilty and sent to the place of condemnation. They were thrown into an abyss filled with fire, flames, and pillars of fire.

### Verse 25

The seventy shepherds were also judged, found guilty, and thrown into that fiery abyss.

### Verse 26

At that time I saw a similar abyss opened in the middle of the earth, filled with fire. The blinded sheep were brought there, judged, found guilty, and thrown into the fiery abyss, where they burned. This abyss was to the right of the house.

### Verse 27

I saw the sheep burning, and their bones burning as well.

### Verse 28

I stood watching until the Lord folded up the old house. All its pillars, beams, and ornaments were taken down and folded together with it. It was removed and placed in a region to the south.

### Verse 29

I watched until the Lord of the sheep brought a new house, greater and taller than the first, and set it up where the old one had stood. All its pillars and ornaments were new and larger than those of the house that had been removed. The Lord of the sheep was inside it. This is the New Jerusalem.

### Verse 30

I saw all the sheep that remained, together with all the animals of the earth and all the birds of heaven. They bowed down and paid homage to the sheep, making requests of them and obeying everything they said.

### Verse 31

Afterward the three beings dressed in white, who had previously carried me away, took me by the hand. The ram also took hold of my hand. Together they lifted me up and set me down among the sheep before the judgment took place.

### Verse 32

All those sheep were white, and their wool was abundant and clean.

### Verse 33

Everything that had been destroyed and scattered, together with all the wild animals and birds of heaven, gathered in that house. The Lord of the sheep rejoiced greatly because they were all good and had returned to his house.

### Verse 34

I saw them lay down the sword that had been given to the sheep. They carried it back into the house and sealed it in the presence of the Lord. All the sheep were invited into the house, but there was not enough room to contain them all.

### Verse 35

The eyes of every one of them were opened so they could see what was good. Not one among them remained unable to see.

### Verse 36

I saw that the house was large, broad, and completely full.

### Verse 37

I saw a white bull born with great horns. All the wild animals and all the birds of heaven feared him and continually made requests of him.

### Verse 38

I watched until all their kinds were transformed and became white oxen. The first among them became a buffalo, and that buffalo grew into a great animal with large black horns on its head. The Lord of the sheep rejoiced over them and over all the oxen.

### Verse 39

I slept among them. Then I awoke and saw everything.

### Verse 40

This is the vision I saw while sleeping. When I awoke, I blessed the Lord of Righteousness and gave him glory.

### Verse 41

Then I began to weep bitterly. My tears would not stop, and I could no longer endure what I had seen. Everything that was to come and be fulfilled had been shown to me, and all human deeds had been revealed in their proper order.

### Verse 42

That night I remembered my first dream. I wept because of it as well, and I was overwhelmed by what I had seen in the vision.
