# 1 Enoch — Chapter 48
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-02-parables-chapters-37-71.md`, Chapter 48.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 10 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.



---

### Verse 1

In that place I saw an inexhaustible fountain of righteousness. Many fountains of wisdom surrounded it. Everyone who was thirsty drank from them, was filled with wisdom, and took up a dwelling among the righteous, holy, and chosen.

### Verse 2

At that time the Son of Man was given a name in the presence of the Lord of Spirits, and his name was spoken before the Ancient of Days.

### Verse 3

His name was spoken before the Lord of Spirits before the sun and the signs of the heavens were created, and before the stars of heaven were made.

### Verse 4

He will be a support for the righteous, so they can lean on him and not fall. He will be a light to the nations and a hope to those whose hearts are troubled.

### Verse 5

All who live on the earth will bow down before him. They will bless, praise, and celebrate the Lord of Spirits with songs.

### Verse 6

For this reason he was chosen and kept hidden in God's presence before the creation of the world, and he will remain so forever.

### Verse 7

The wisdom of the Lord of Spirits has revealed him to the holy and righteous. He protects the inheritance of the righteous, because they have rejected this world of injustice and all its works and ways in the name of the Lord of Spirits. They are saved through his name, and he is the one who vindicates their lives.

### Verse 8

In those days the kings of the earth and the powerful who possess the land will look downcast because of what they themselves have done. On the day of their anguish and suffering, their lives will not be saved.

### Verse 9

“I will hand them over to my chosen ones. They will burn before the holy like straw in a fire and sink before the righteous like lead in water. No trace of them will remain.

### Verse 10

“On the day of their suffering, the earth will have rest. They will fall before the righteous and will not rise again. No one will be able to take them by the hand and lift them up, because they rejected the Lord of Spirits and his Anointed One. Blessed be the name of the Lord of Spirits!”
