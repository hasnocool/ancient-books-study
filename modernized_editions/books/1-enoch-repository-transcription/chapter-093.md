# 1 Enoch — Chapter 93
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-05-laws-of-enoch-chapters-91-105.md`, Chapter 93.

**Editorial method:** This is a complete, verse-by-verse modernization of all fourteen numbered units in the repository transcription. The wording is updated for readability while retaining the sequence and substance of the source. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

**Textual cautions:** Verses 11–14 are explicitly marked as interpolations in the source and remain separately bracketed here. Stray characters and obvious punctuation debris in the source transcription (including the trailing “l” in verse 1, the “j” after “deceit” in verse 4, and the page number embedded in verse 13) are treated as transcription artifacts, not as words of the passage.

---

### Verse 1

After this, Enoch began reading from the books. He said:

### Verse 2

“I will speak to you, my sons, about the children of righteousness, the chosen people of the world, and the shoot of righteousness. I, Enoch, will announce these things to you according to what was shown to me in a heavenly vision, what I learned through the words of the holy angels, and what I discovered in the heavenly tablets.

### Verse 3

“I began reading from the books and said: I was born as the seventh generation in the first week, when judgment and righteousness were still delayed.

### Verse 4

“After me, in the second week, great wickedness will arise and deception will take root. During that period the first decisive ending will come, and one man will be saved. After that period has passed, unrighteousness will grow, and God will establish a law for sinners.

### Verse 5

“Then, at the end of the third week, a man will be chosen as the shoot of righteous judgment. After him, the shoot of righteousness will continue forever.

### Verse 6

“Then, at the end of the fourth week, visions of the holy and righteous will be revealed. A law will be given for all future generations, and a protected enclosure will be established for them.

### Verse 7

“Then, at the end of the fifth week, a glorious house and a dominion will be established forever.

### Verse 8

“Then, during the sixth week, everyone living in that period will become spiritually blind, and their hearts will be overcome by a corrupt forgetfulness of wisdom. A man will arise during that week. At its end, the house of dominion will be burned with fire, and the entire people descended from the chosen root will be scattered.

### Verse 9

“After that, during the seventh week, a generation will arise whose deeds will be many, but all its actions will be rebellious.

### Verse 10

“At the end of that week, the chosen people of righteousness—the enduring shoot of righteousness—will be selected to receive sevenfold instruction concerning all of God's creation.

### Verse 11 — interpolation

[Who among all human beings could hear the voice of the Holy One without trembling? Who could comprehend the thoughts of the Holy One?]

### Verse 12 — interpolation

[Who could see all the works of heaven? Who could look upon heaven and understand its mysteries? Who could see a soul or a spirit and explain it? Who could rise up, see all their limits, understand them, or do what they do?]

### Verse 13 — interpolation

[Who among all people could know the length and breadth of the earth? To whom has the full measure of these things been revealed?]

### Verse 14 — interpolation

[Is there anyone who could discern the extent of heaven, how high it is, what supports it, how many stars there are, and where all the heavenly lights have their appointed places?]

## Completeness and editorial notes

- All **fourteen numbered units** in the repository's Chapter 93 have been rendered, including the four units marked as interpolations.
- Verses 1–10 retain the sequence of Enoch's account of the successive weeks: his own birth in the first week; wickedness and a survivor in the second; the chosen figure and enduring shoot of righteousness in the third; visions, law, and protection in the fourth; the glorious house in the fifth; blindness, destruction, and dispersion in the sixth; and an apostate generation followed by the selection and instruction of the righteous in the seventh.
- Verses 11–14 remain visibly identified as interpolated material. Charles's 1917 printing also encloses this four-verse block in square brackets; its stated convention defines square brackets as interpolations.
- **1893 reconstructed sequence:** Charles's 1893 apparatus says 93:1–10 should be followed by 91:12–19; 93:11–14 are out-of-place interpolated material and are excluded from the reconstructed Apocalypse of Weeks order. The modernization retains the selected repository's numbered chapter arrangement but identifies 11–14 as interpolations.
- **1917 witness arrangement:** The 1917 printing places 91:12–17 after 93:1–14, whereas the 1893 reconstruction places 91:12–19 after 93:1–10. These are different witness/editorial arrangements; this chapter retains the selected repository sequence. See Chapter 91 and the historical collation log.
- **Damaged wording:** The 1917 text prints a daggered/corrupt token in 93:1 (“both †gave† and began…”). The repository transcription has a trailing stray “l” after the verse's opening clause. This modernization removes that stray transcription character and does not invent a replacement word for the corrupt token.
- The source's damaged punctuation and stray scanning/transcription marks have not been reproduced as meaningful text.
- This rendering follows the repository's chapter and verse divisions. It does not silently substitute another translation's wording or arrangement.
