# 1 Enoch — Chapter 22
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 22.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 14 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

Then I went to another place. In the west he showed me a great, high mountain with hard rocks and four beautiful hollow places.

### Verse 2

There were deep, wide hollows, completely smooth, rounded, and dark to look at.

### Verse 3

Rufael, one of the holy angels who was with me, answered: “These hollow places are where the spirits of the souls of the dead are gathered. They were created for this purpose: all the souls of human beings are to assemble here.

### Verse 4

“These places are appointed as their dwelling until the day of judgment and until the time set for them has been completed. That appointed period is long, lasting until the great judgment comes upon them.

### Verse 5

“I saw the spirits of the human beings who had died. Their voices rose all the way to heaven, where they made their complaints.

### Verse 6

Then I asked Rufael, the angel who was with me: “Whose spirit is that over there, whose voice rises so far and makes such a complaint?”

### Verse 7

He answered: “That is the spirit that came from Abel, whom his brother Cain murdered. Abel continues to cry out against Cain until Cain's descendants are destroyed from the face of the earth and disappear from among humanity.”

### Verse 8

At that time I asked about the separation of the spirits and about the judgment of all people: “Why are they divided from one another?”

### Verse 9

He answered: “Three divisions have been established to separate the spirits of the dead. The souls of the righteous are separated in this way: there is a spring of water, with light above it.

### Verse 10

“A separate place has likewise been prepared for sinners who die and are buried in the earth without having received judgment during their lifetimes.

### Verse 11

“Their souls are placed here in great suffering until the great day of judgment, punishment, and torment. Those who insulted and oppressed others will face everlasting judgment and vengeance for their victims. They will remain bound forever.

### Verse 12

“Another division has been made for the souls of those who cry out and make known the destruction they suffered when they were killed during the days of sinners.

### Verse 13

“This division is for the souls of people who were not righteous but were sinners who completed their crimes. They will remain with criminals like themselves. Their souls will not be killed on the day of judgment, nor will they rise from that place.”

### Verse 14

Then I blessed the Lord of Glory and said: “Blessed be my Lord, the Lord of righteousness, who reigns forever!”
