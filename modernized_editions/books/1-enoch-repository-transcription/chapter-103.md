# 1 Enoch — Chapter 103
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-05-laws-of-enoch-chapters-91-105.md`, Chapter 103.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 15 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

Now I make this solemn declaration to you righteous people, by the glory of the One who is great, honoured, and mighty in dominion. I swear by his greatness.

### Verse 2

I know this mystery. I have read it in the heavenly tablets, examined the book of the holy ones, and found the following written and recorded about them:

### Verse 3

Goodness, joy, and glory have all been prepared for them and recorded for the spirits of those who died in righteousness. You will receive abundant good in return for your labour, and your portion will be far greater than the portion of those who are still alive.

### Verse 4

The spirits of those who die in righteousness will live, rejoice, and be glad. They will not perish. Their memory will remain before the Great One throughout every generation of the world. So do not be afraid of the insults directed at them.

### Verse 5

How terrible it will be for you sinners when you die in your sins and people like you say: “The sinners were fortunate; they enjoyed all their days.”

### Verse 6

“They died prosperous and wealthy. They experienced no suffering or violence during their lives. They died honoured, and no judgment was carried out against them while they lived.”

### Verse 7

Understand that their souls will descend into Sheol. They will become wretched, and their suffering will be great.

### Verse 8

Your spirits will enter darkness, chains, and burning fire, where severe condemnation awaits. Condemnation will remain for the generations of the world. How terrible it will be for you: you will have no peace.

### Verse 9

Do not say this about the righteous and good who are still alive: “During their lives they are worn down by hardship. They have experienced every kind of trouble, encountered much evil, suffered illness, and become weak and discouraged.”

### Verse 10

“ They have been brought low, with no one to help or speak for them. They have gained nothing. They are tortured and worn down, and each day they have little hope of seeing a better life.”

### Verse 11

“They hoped to become leaders, but instead they became the lowest. They worked hard but never received the reward of their labour. They became prey for sinners, and unjust people placed a heavy burden upon them.”

### Verse 12

“Those who hated and struck them gained power over them. The righteous bowed their necks before their enemies, but their enemies showed them no compassion.”

### Verse 13

“They wanted to escape and find rest, but they could find nowhere to flee and nowhere to be safe from their oppressors.”

### Verse 14

“They appealed to their rulers in their suffering and cried out against those who devoured them. But their rulers paid no attention and refused to listen.”

### Verse 15

“The rulers helped those who robbed and consumed the righteous and reduced their numbers. They concealed the oppression instead of removing the burden imposed by those who exploited, scattered, and murdered them. They covered up the murders and refused to acknowledge that they had raised their hands against the innocent.”
