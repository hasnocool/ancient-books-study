# 1 Enoch — Chapter 21
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 21.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 10 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

I travelled around to a place where nothing had been created.

### Verse 2

There I saw something terrifying. There was no heaven above and no firmly established earth beneath, only a chaotic and dreadful place.

### Verse 3

I saw seven stars of heaven bound together there, resembling great mountains and blazing with fire.

### Verse 4

I asked: “For what sin have they been bound? Why have they been thrown into this place?”

### Verse 5

Uriel, one of the holy angels who was with me and was appointed over them, answered: “Why do you ask? Why are you investigating and trying to understand?”

### Verse 6

“These are the stars that disobeyed God's command. They are bound here until ten thousand ages have passed—the appointed length of the days of their guilt.”

### Verse 7

From there I went to another place, even more dreadful than the first. I saw a terrible sight: a great fire blazing and flaming. The place was split open down to the abyss and filled with enormous descending pillars of fire. I could not see how far it extended or where it began.

### Verse 8

I said: “How terrifying this place is! It is horrible to look upon!”

### Verse 9

Uriel, one of the holy angels with me, answered: “Why are you so frightened and alarmed by this dreadful place and its suffering?”

### Verse 10

He said to me: “This is the prison of the angels. They will be imprisoned here forever.”
