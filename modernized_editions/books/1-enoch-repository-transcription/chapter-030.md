# 1 Enoch — Chapter 30
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 30.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 3 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

Above that region, beyond the Eastern Mountain and not far away, I saw another place: valleys with streams that never ran dry.

### Verse 2

I saw a beautiful tree resembling a fragrant mastic tree.

### Verse 3

Along the sides of those valleys I saw sweet-smelling cinnamon. After passing these places, I continued farther east.
