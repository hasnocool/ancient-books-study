# 1 Enoch — Chapter 101
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-05-laws-of-enoch-chapters-91-105.md`, Chapter 101.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 9 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

Look at the heavens, you children of heaven, and consider everything the Most High has made. Revere him, and do nothing evil in his presence.

### Verse 2

When he closes heaven's windows and prevents rain and dew from falling upon the earth because of you, what will you do?

### Verse 3

When he sends his anger against you because of all your deeds, you will not be able to plead your case. You have spoken arrogantly and insultingly against his righteousness; therefore you will have no peace.

### Verse 4

Have you not watched sailors at sea? Their ships are thrown about by the waves and shaken by the winds, and they face terrible danger.

### Verse 5

They are afraid because their valuable possessions may sink with them into the sea. Their hearts are anxious in case the waters swallow them and they perish.

### Verse 6

Is not the whole sea, with all its waters and movements, the work of the Most High? Are not its currents and all its activity his work? Has he not set the sand as its boundary?

### Verse 7

At his rebuke the sea becomes afraid and dries up; all its fish and everything in it die. Yet you sinners who live on the earth do not fear him.

### Verse 8

Did he not make the heavens, the earth, and everything in them? Who gave understanding and wisdom to all creatures that move on land and in the sea?

### Verse 9

Do not even the sailors fear the sea?

Yet you sinners do not fear the Most High.
