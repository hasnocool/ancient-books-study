# 1 Enoch — Chapter 12
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 12.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 6 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

Before these events took place, Enoch had been hidden away. No human being knew where he was hidden, where he lived, or what had happened to him.

### Verse 2

His activities were connected with the holy ones and with the Watchers who lived during his time.

### Verse 3

I, Enoch, was blessing the great Lord, the King of the world, when the Watchers called to me. They called me “Enoch, the scribe,” and spoke to me.

### Verse 4

They said: “Enoch, scribe of righteousness, go and announce this to the Watchers of heaven: You have abandoned the highest heaven and the holy, eternal dwelling place. You have defiled yourselves with women, behaved like human beings, taken wives, and made yourselves thoroughly impure on the earth.

### Verse 5

“You will have no peace on the earth and no forgiveness for your sin. You may take pleasure in your children,

### Verse 6

“but you will witness the deaths of those you love. You will mourn the destruction of your children and plead forever, but you will never obtain mercy or peace.”
