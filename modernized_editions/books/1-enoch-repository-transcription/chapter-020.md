# 1 Enoch — Chapter 20
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 20.

**Editorial method:** Complete modernization of the chapter's 7 numbered units, preserving source wording's sequence and content while updating archaic syntax. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

These are the names of the holy angels who keep watch.

### Verse 2

Uriel is one of the holy angels. He is appointed over the world and over Tartarus.

### Verse 3

Rufael is one of the holy angels, appointed over the spirits of human beings.

### Verse 4

Raguel is one of the holy angels. He carries out judgment against the world and the heavenly lights.

### Verse 5

Michael is one of the holy angels. He is appointed over the best part of humanity and over the people.

### Verse 6

Saraqael is one of the holy angels. He is appointed over the spirits of the children of humanity whose spirits have sinned.

### Verse 7

Gabriel is one of the holy angels. He is appointed over Paradise, the seraphim, and the cherubim.
