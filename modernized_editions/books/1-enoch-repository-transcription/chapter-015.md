# 1 Enoch — Chapter 15
## Complete modern-language rendering

**Source basis:** The repository transcription in `formatted_editions/books/book-of-enoch-charles-1893/book-01-watchers-chapters-01-36.md`, Chapter 15.

**Editorial method:** Complete verse-by-verse modernization of all 12 numbered verses in the repository transcription. Archaic wording is updated while the source sequence and substance are retained. This is not a new translation from the Ethiopic, and the repository transcription has not been verified as a verbatim copy of the 1893 Charles/Dillmann printing.

---

### Verse 1

He answered and spoke to me in the voice I had heard: “Do not be afraid, Enoch, righteous man and scribe of righteousness. Come closer and listen to my voice.

### Verse 2

“Go and tell the Watchers of heaven, who sent you to plead for them: You should be pleading on behalf of human beings; human beings should not be pleading on your behalf.

### Verse 3

“Why did you abandon the high, holy, eternal heaven? You lay with women, defiled yourselves with the daughters of humanity, took wives, behaved like earthly people, and fathered giant sons.

### Verse 4

“You were spiritual and holy, enjoying eternal life. Yet you defiled yourselves with women, joined yourselves to mortal flesh, desired human blood, and produced children of flesh and blood, just like mortal beings whose lives are short.

### Verse 5

“I gave human beings wives so that they could become pregnant and bear children, and so that nothing would be lacking to them on earth.

### Verse 6

“But you were originally spiritual beings, enjoying immortal life for all generations of the world.

### Verse 7

“That is why I did not appoint wives for you. Spiritual beings have their dwelling in heaven.

### Verse 8

“The giants, born from the union of spirit and flesh, will be called evil spirits on the earth, and their dwelling will be on the earth.

### Verse 9

“Evil spirits come from their bodies. Their origin is from above, because their beginning came from the holy Watchers. They will become evil spirits on earth, and that is what they will be called.

### Verse 10

“The spirits of heaven dwell in heaven. The spirits born on earth, from earthly beings, have their dwelling on earth.

### Verse 11

“The spirits of the giants will devour, oppress, destroy, attack, wage war, and bring ruin upon the earth. They will cause suffering. They will not eat food or become thirsty, and they will be invisible.

### Verse 12

“These spirits will rise up against human beings and against women, because they came from them.”
