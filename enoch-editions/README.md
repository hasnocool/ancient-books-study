## Source scope

### 1893 Clarendon Press

The 1893 main translated text covers Chapters 1–104. The volume also contains extensive introduction, apparatus, appendices, and fragmentary material. Appendix E separately includes a New Latin fragment of 1 Enoch 106:1–18; that fragment is not a complete translation of Chapters 105–108 and must not be mistaken for one.

External catalogue / text-layer reference: [Cornell / Internet Archive record](https://archive.org/details/cu31924014590545) (identifier `cu31924014590545`). The catalogue identifies the Cornell University Library copy as Oxford: Clarendon Press, 1893.

### 1917 SPCK textual edition

The 1917 Charles/Oesterley edition includes Chapters 105–108. The available repository scan is a 1962 tenth impression of the edition first published in 1917; the preface explains that the translation reprints Charles's 1912 second edition. The distinction between publication date and the date of the physical copy matters for accurate citations.

External scan reference: [Internet Archive / Claremont scan](https://archive.org/details/bookofenoch0000unse).

The searchable text layer for Chapters 105–108 has been reviewed.
- The older 66-page and 101-page PDFs remain preserved as project artifacts. They are useful for documenting prior project inputs, but they are not substitutes for the larger historical scans.
- The exact textual lineage of the selected combined repository transcription remains unresolved. Do not silently replace its wording with a reading from either historical edition; log each witness difference in the source-collation record.

## Follow-up visual page-image access audit — 25 November 2026

A third distinct 1893 Clarendon scan was identified: a Knox College / University of Toronto copy mirrored at [Luminist](https://s3.us-west-1.wasabisys.com/luminist/EB/C/Charles_ed%20-%20Book%20of%20Enoch.pdf). Its searchable PDF text identifies the 1893 Clarendon title page and the translated-text range pp. 55–308. The scan is approximately 30.5 MB; the page-image service refused the PDF as too large, and no internal page image was inspected.

The Cornell (428-page), University of Michigan/Google Books (418-page), and Knox College/Toronto copies are three distinct digitizations of the 1893 edition. Internal printed-page images for the 1893 translation range could not be rendered from any of them in this environment. Title-page thumbnails, OCR/searchable text, and catalogue metadata do not count as visual page proofing; printed pp. 55–308 remain outstanding.

The authentic 1917 Claremont SPCK scan was also retried via the 6.73 MB Commons-hosted PDF. Printed p. 152 still fails to render (zero-based PDF index 153, cache miss). The visually inspected pages remain printed pp. 149–151 and 153–154; p. 152 remains open. The file is a 1962 tenth impression of the edition first published in 1917.
