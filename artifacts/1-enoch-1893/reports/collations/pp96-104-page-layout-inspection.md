## Material correction to the OCR/alignment pilot

The p.104 image visibly prints **XXXV** for the westward journey (“From thence I went towards the west…”) and then **XXXVI.1** for the southward journey (“From thence I went to the south…”). These are consecutive chapter units and different directions, not a single west/south variant at one location. The pilot's row that describes “XXXV” as unnumbered and its conclusion that a west→south direction variant exists at XXXV/XXXVI are not supported by this facsimile. The page image establishes the printed chapter headings and separate directions; the derivative's chapter mapping still needs direct comparison.

The pilot's chapter map also needs correction: p.103 visibly covers XXXII.2–XXXIII.6, and p.104 begins with the continuation XXXIII.2–4 before XXXIV–XXXVI. The pilot's coarse alignment labels should not be treated as verified page-to-verse boundaries.
