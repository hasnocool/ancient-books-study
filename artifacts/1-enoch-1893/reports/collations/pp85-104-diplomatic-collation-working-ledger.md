## Status and evidence rules

This work package consolidates page-level English comparison, apparatus separation, and a discrepancy ledger. It is **not yet a completed diplomatic proof**. The OCR files contain recognition errors in English, Greek, Ethiopic, Roman numerals, punctuation, and sigla. The Cornell p.91 OCR is absent from the first OCR batch; any text recovered from the Knox/Toronto scan is a parallel witness, not Cornell OCR.

The facsimile is the control witness. OCR is used to locate passages and generate candidates. The repository derivative is never silently corrected. Readings not safely recoverable from the image are marked `IMAGE_RECHECK` or `UNRESOLVED_GLYPH`; no conjectural completion is promoted to a source reading.

## Punctuation, brackets, and page continuity

1. The square bracket opens at XVII.1 on p.87 and closes after XIX.3 on p.91. The bracketed span is XVII.1–XIX.3.
2. Printed verse numbering is not to be regularized: XVI moves from verse 2 to verse 4.
3. The OCR page boundary at XXII.12 (p.95–96), XVIII.12 (p.89–90), and XXXIII.1 (p.103–104) must be treated as continuous text, not duplicate or missing verse material.
4. Parentheses in XXII.2, XXIII.2, XXX.1, XXX.1's “(fed)”, XXXI.3, XXXIII.1, and XXXIV.3 require exact reproduction and classification (authorial translation gloss vs apparatus).
5. Quotation marks, colon/semicolon usage, apostrophes, and capitalization in the OCR are not authoritative. They must be checked against the page image before a final diplomatic transcription.
6. Roman numeral OCR forms such as `XVL`, `XXTI`, `XXVTI`, `XXVIIIL`, `XXXT`, and `XXXIL` are OCR errors, not printed variants.
